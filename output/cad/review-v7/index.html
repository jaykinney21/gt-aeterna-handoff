GRACEFUL TRIBUTE / AETERNA

Every Step Legacy — revision 7

Appearance review of the revised Blender model. These are actual 3D renders. The revised exterior has not been approved for manufacturing; the previous model’s capacity measurement does not apply to it. The visual proportions remain provisional and are not a dimensional approval.

This revision follows the agreed visual critique: a fuller cap and bronze rim, tighter shoulder transition, revised bronze sweep, longer and stronger football-style stitches, more visible surface character, larger centered lettering and heart, and a lower base with heavier trim. Bronze is warmer and less polished. Front reads “before the crowds there was you”; rear reads “every step i took began beside you.”

Click any image to enlarge it. “Cap outline” isolates the actual cap and bronze rim so its silhouette is easy to judge; “Top” shows the complete model. No 3D software is needed.
