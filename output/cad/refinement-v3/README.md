# Every Step Legacy — appearance revision 3

This is a newly constructed **appearance model**, not a modified capacity envelope. It contains editable independent artistic meshes. It has no engineered cavity, verified capacity, validated closure or manufacturing STL release. The v2 engineering files remain untouched.

## Twelve visual changes implemented

1. Independent tall textured core, lower front/rear writing shells, and supported rolled upper fold establish overlapping construction.
2. Curved/tapered lower body and narrowed upper core replace the straight or swollen vase silhouette. Cap width is about 71% of the widest body in this interpretation.
3. Both bronze sweeps share the actual writing-shell boundary, taper toward the foot, roll upward onto a black supporting fold and meet the cap rim.
4. A lower shoulder lip and dark reveal distinguish the two heights; a closed textured inset cap has a continuous bronze perimeter.
5. Rear composition reverses the front: smooth writing panel at image left, bronze at right; front lacing is not repeated on the rear.
6. Fine dense geometric pebbles replace scattered chevrons.
7. A continuous narrow bronze seam and closely spaced cross stitches replace isolated bars.
8. Three dense pebble base bands use stronger top/bottom bronze trim and restrained internal separators.
9. Separate framed front dedication and rear personalization plaques are wider and flatter. Rear text is explicitly placeholder copy.
10. Georgia serif lettering uses the source lowercase phrases, with a bronze heart below each. Front base text is uppercase.
11. Dense sampled surfaces, rounded borders and corrected rear normals avoid the previous pinches and buried text. These independent surfaces still require later manufacturing integration.
12. Warm bronze, readable black surfaces and repeatable studio lighting expose form and detail.

- `appearance-detail.png`, `appearance-base-detail.png`: upper detail and base treatment.

The concept board is not a measured scan. Hidden surfaces, exact fold depth, cap dimensions and rear construction are explicit interpretations for human approval. Bronze appearance is a material/finish proposal, not a guarantee of supplier finish. The source’s 200+ cubic-inch claim has not been transferred to this model. Once the appearance is approved, the connected cavity, walls, closure, physical texture scale and printing process must be reconciled with it.

Final cleanup removed a duplicate cap surface, welded the outer shell seam, projected text/hearts to the curved surfaces, and reduced cap specular reflection. All ten final images are listed with SHA-256 hashes in `appearance-status.json`; `finish-cap.log` records the final render pass.
