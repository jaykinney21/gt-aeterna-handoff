## Final render contract

The frozen pass-8 script generates ten 900 × 1100 images at 64 Cycles samples: front, rear, side, top, detail, base-detail, front-reference, rear-reference, front-noplaque, and cap-top. Front-reference/rear-reference use the same cameras as front/rear. Front-noplaque is explicitly a comparison, with full beads behind the hidden plaque; cap-top isolates the actual inset and annulus and rotates the camera so the tapered end points upward. The script restores all visibility states and saves the editable Blender scene with the front camera after rendering.

## Proportions and remaining limits

All sizes are appearance parameters. XY scale remains 0.85 to follow the narrow source illustration. V7 compresses the base height by 10% and translates the body downward 5.08 mm to remain seated. These intentional visual changes do not preserve the written manufacturing envelope. The generated status JSON records actual evaluated bounds separately from the source diameter target and records pre-transform parameters explicitly.

The conceptual source views do not uniquely determine hidden depth or a complete solid. The rolled return and closed shoulder are interpretations. Repeated pebble placement is procedurally jittered rather than an exact reconstruction of every source pebble. No cavity capacity, watertightness, closure fit, minimum wall thickness, printability, physical seal, or production readiness is claimed. Owner visual approval is still required before engineering the revised appearance.
