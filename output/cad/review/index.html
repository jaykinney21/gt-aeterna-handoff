Your review

Review shape and appearance first; supplier tolerances and physical performance are separate checks.

  1. Shape: does it capture the original folded, athletic character? What should be taller, softer or more pronounced?
  2. Bronze accents: is the divider broad enough, and are the rims balanced?
  3. Texture and lettering: are the tread, stitching, wording and placement right?
  4. Closure: is bottom loading with a fixed top the desired experience?
  5. Priority: name the three most important changes before the next revision.

You can also annotate the PDF in Preview and share the annotated copy. No CAD software is required to review these views.
