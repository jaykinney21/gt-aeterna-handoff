# Every Step Legacy v6 — requested appearance corrections

September 22, 2026. V5 and prior engineering files remain unchanged. This is an appearance study, not a manufacturing release.

## Changes

- Shifted the complete vertical football-style seam right toward the copper sweep in the front view, approximately 8.7 mm at source-coordinate height 220 mm after the retained appearance scaling. Stitch geometry hidden by the sweep is trimmed to avoid showing through the writing panel.
- Replaced the oval cap outline with a true rounded teardrop in XY geometry. The broad rounded end is on the left, with a softly pointed right end meeting the copper sweep. Cap plane, appearance footprint and the approved black foil below are retained. The upper textured neck transitions locally into the cap outline.
- Changed the base to eight evenly spaced, staggered rows of rounded black beads, removing the wide middle gap. There are 136 placements around a full row before plaque clearance; plaques and thin copper rims are preserved.

## Preview loop

1. Front/top/base preview showed the requested direction, but exposed an uneven neck transition, hidden lower stitches and an overly wide central gap in the 3/2/3 base-row layout.
2. Aligned the neck transition with the sloped cap plane, trimmed occluded stitches, and added an isolated cap-top view showing the actual teardrop outline. The isolated view rotates only the camera so the tapered point faces upward.
3. Replaced grouped base rows with eight evenly spaced rows from 8 to 43.5 mm in the pre-scaled model. Refined rounded bead spacing and restrained highlights. Base-detail preview checked before full delivery rendering.

## Review views and scope

The final set contains front, rear, side, whole-model top, detail, base detail, front reference, rear reference and isolated cap top. The isolated component view temporarily hides surrounding geometry for the render, then restores all visibility before saving the native scene with its front review camera.

Top-view textured areas outside the smaller teardrop outline are the lower shoulder visible beneath the locally narrowed neck. The final neck ring follows the teardrop. The approved lower curved black foil has not been widened or reshaped to hide that shoulder.

The source picture and written dimensions differ in proportion. This study retains v5's explicit 0.85 XY appearance scaling; actual visual bounds are recorded separately from the source's written dimensions. No cavity, usable capacity, minimum wall, closure fit, manufacturing tolerance or printability claim is made.
