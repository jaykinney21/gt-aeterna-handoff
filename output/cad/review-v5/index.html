Every Step Legacy — revision 5

Appearance review of the revised Blender model. These are actual 3D renders. The revised exterior has not been approved for manufacturing; the previous model’s capacity measurement does not apply to it. Width and camera angle were adjusted for visual fidelity, so this is not a dimensional approval.

This revision tests a slimmer silhouette, a more visible oval top, a tighter upright black blade, broader bronze edging and rounded base texture against the original concept. It remains a design interpretation awaiting your review. Front reads “before the crowds there was you”; rear reads “every step i took began beside you.”
