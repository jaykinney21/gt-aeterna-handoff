# CAD and catalog delivery plan

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

Authorized by Jay after the six supplier inquiries were sent. Two execution specialists own the CAD and catalog lanes; an independent reviewer checks acceptance criteria, and the coordinator checks delivered artifacts. This is an active delivery plan, not a request for another blanket approval.

## CAD: Every Step Legacy

Working scope is the original 11-inch athletic vessel from document 63, with a connected 200+ cubic-inch cavity. Bottom access and a fixed/decorative top are provisional engineering choices. Body width, walls, seals, inserts and tolerances remain explicit parameters until source clarification and supplier review.

1. Reconcile the reference views and dimensional contradictions; maintain an assumption register.
2. Create the curved exterior with front/side/back previews; check the complete envelope including decoration.
3. Measure connected usable cavity after contouring and mechanical intrusions; revise without silently changing original dimensions.
4. Develop access plate, aperture, fixing bosses and hardware/seal interfaces; document filling and manufacturing cleanout access.
5. Add source-supported relief and lettering; preserve editable source and reproducible measurements.
6. Export separate candidate parts, check units, topology, assembly and cavity, and provide a section/assembly view. Label any unresolved part clearly.
7. Obtain supplier review of material, wall/feature limits, clearances, inserts, seal and finish. This requires a response from a manufacturer; it cannot be certified by rendering.
8. Quote then purchase authorized closure/finish tests and full sample. Measure physical capacity, closure fit, stability, finish and packaging; revise before production release. Current authorization does not place sample orders or commission paid engineering.

Digital milestone: editable review candidate and evidence of checks, not a declaration of production readiness. A capacity failure or source mismatch is a finding to resolve, not a completed requirement.

## Catalog: seven-design review edition

Scope: the five originals, the original Every Step Legacy, and DOC NOTES Infinite Loop. Existing designs stay anchored to their individual references; the alternate DOC NOTES descriptions are separate future concepts. The full 72-entry collection remains a separately sized expansion, not silently included in this edition.

1. Record each card's source, identity, finish, reference images and exact generation prompt.
2. Create consistent individual hero imagery; compare against source silhouettes and decoration, flag deviations.
3. Add source-supported companion/jewelry concept imagery. Generic accessories must be labeled generic; missing design-specific briefs remain visible.
4. Assemble editable captions and SKU/source links, with concept status and no unsupported prices, capacity, material performance or availability claims.
5. Produce an actual review PDF as well as editable HTML and saved assets; inspect browser layout and every PDF page.
6. Record remaining visual/engineering decisions and obtain design-owner selection before treating a direction as final.
7. After physical samples/quotes, replace provisional imagery and facts with verified product information for a sales-ready edition. No public publishing is part of this task.

The internal review edition can be delivered while suppliers are quoting. A sales-ready catalog depends on actual products, costs and fulfillment commitments.
