> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

September 19, 2026. Reference: [source board, document 63](../references/source-documents/63/image1.png). Compared directly with the v2 front, rear, side, perspective and top renders in `output/cad/refinement-v2/`. No model was edited for this review.

## Overall finding

The v2 model carries some identifiers—black and bronze, an upward curved divider, athletic marks and the correct phrases—but does not yet capture the source's sculptural identity. The reference looks like layered, overlapping, twisting shells with deliberately different surfaces. V2 reads as one swollen vase with a bronze strap and sparse marks attached. Better lighting alone will not close that gap.

The user's C-minus assessment is understandable. Treat v2 as a mechanical study with an early appearance study, rather than a near-finished reproduction.

## What to retain

- Black/bronze direction, bottom-loading intent and the core wording are aligned with the source.
- Editable source and separate mechanical work remain useful for subsequent reconstruction.
- The existing cavity study provides engineering context, but its exact outer envelope should not become an unexamined constraint that forces the wrong artwork.

## Important limits of the source

The board is a concept/reference illustration, not a measured 3D scan or consistent engineering drawing. Its front, rear, top and lower hardware illustrations should guide interpretation, but they do not uniquely define hidden geometry. The stated lid diameter and apparent pill-shaped cap require reconciliation with the five-inch body depth. The dimension/capacity labels should not be used as proof that the illustrated shape actually meets them.

Do not silently invent missing surfaces and call the result exact. Record those choices, show them clearly and ask the owner to approve the interpretation once a reviewable proposal exists.

## Recommended review gates before more detailing

1. **Silhouette blockout:** front, rear, left, right and three-quarter views in a plain material, with source comparison at matching visual scale. Approve the two overlapping masses, shoulder notch, cap and overall slenderness. No decorative texture yet.
2. **Panel and bronze layout:** flat black/bronze regions, with the sweep and cap rim clearly visible from all angles. Approve where each region wraps around the vessel.
3. **Detail swatches:** enlarged examples of athletic texture, seam/lacing, plaque, lettering and heart at actual intended size. Choose the visual vocabulary before applying it everywhere.
4. **Complete appearance review:** consistent product-lighting views, close-ups and a rotatable viewer. Each view should state whether it shows the complete intended appearance or only the validated manufacturing shell.
5. **Engineering reconciliation:** check capacity, walls, closure and print detail after the approved exterior is developed; disclose any shape changes required for manufacturing and bring those back for visual review.

The immediate deliverable should be an approved shape interpretation, not another heavily decorated near-identical vase. No new modeling, renders or purchases were performed in this audit.
