Graceful Tributes: Warranty & Shipping Damage Claim Form
I. Our "Arrival Peace of Mind" Warranty
At Graceful Tributes, we understand the sacred nature of your purchase. We warrant that your memorial will arrive free of structural defects and transit damage. Due to the custom, 3D-printed nature of our products:
Transit Damage: Fully covered if reported within 72 hours of delivery.
Structural Integrity: Covered for 1 year against resin cracking or de-lamination (excluding damage from drops or improper handling).

II. Damage Claim Submission Form

Name: __________________________________________________

2. Damage Assessment (Check all that apply)
[ ] External Box Damage: (Crushed corners, punctures, water stains)

[ ] Finish Issues: (Scratches, paint chipping, clouded vitrine)
[ ] Structural Issue: (Lid does not fit, baseplate screw alignment)
3. Photo Requirements (Mandatory for Insurance)
Please attach the following four photos to your email:
The Outer Box: Showing the shipping label and any visible exterior damage.
The Inner Packaging: Showing how the urn was seated in the foam/cushioning.
The Full Product: A wide shot of the entire urn.
The Close-Up: A clear, focused shot of the specific break or defect.
4. Preferred Resolution
[ ] Full Replacement: (We will prioritize a new 3D print and shipment).
[ ] Professional Repair Kit: (For minor detachments; we provide specialized resin adhesive and touch-up paint).
[ ] Full Refund: (Available only if a replacement cannot be produced in time for the service).

III. Internal Processing Instructions (For Your Team)
Carrier Claims: Immediately upload the customer’s photos to the carrier's portal. 3D-printed items are often classified as "Art" or "Fragile Sculptures"; ensure the declared value matches the Wholesale + Labor cost.
Design Feedback Loop: If the same part breaks on multiple shipments (e.g., the "Match Point" net or the "She Taught Us to Fly" wing), flag the CAD file for revision. The designer must increase the "Minimum Feature Size" by 0.5mm in the next print run.
The "Return" Rule: For damaged memorials, do not ask the family to ship the broken urn back. It is emotionally taxing and often results in further breakage. Ask them to dispose of it respectfully once the replacement arrives.
