2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a reengineered, dual-component architecture where a secure circular pedestal base functions as the primary ash storage foundation, supporting a stylized, architectural-themed cylindrical vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The continuous hollow storage compartment is engineered to span both the interior of the expanded pedestal base and the upper vessel. It is designed to securely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal access plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits entirely flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into heavy-duty brass inserts permanently integrated into the structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a sophisticated, architectural tribute to an engineer, architect, or builder, utilizing a classic drafting blueprint motif with elegant metallic accents.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features an off-white/cream canvas enveloped by a complex, continuous wrap of blue architectural drafting lines, structural grids, and technical diagrams. The cylindrical form is visually dynamic, featuring a spiraling, raised metallic gold trim line that mimics the unwrapping of a rolled blueprint or parchment.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It perfectly continues the off-white and blue architectural drafting motif around its circumference and is framed at both the top and bottom edges by smooth, metallic gold trim rings.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with an integrated, recessed circular lid featuring a prominent, raised 8-point compass star motif finished in metallic gold.
