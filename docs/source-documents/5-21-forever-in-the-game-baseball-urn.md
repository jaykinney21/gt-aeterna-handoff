Technical Design Specification: "Forever in the Game" Baseball Urn
SKU: LN-SPT-DIAMOND
Series: Lifestyle & Novelty / Sports Legend Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring a dedicated, decorative pedestal base that operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, supporting a classic, permanently sealed amphora-style vessel above.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A highly stabilized, circular pedestal design providing a wide footprint to securely anchor the tall vessel.
External Base Dimensions: 7.6” (193 mm) Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 2.8” (71 mm) Base Height.
The Decorative Vessel: The upper sculptural assembly is a sleek, broad-shouldered jar with an integrated, permanently attached dome lid. The vessel alone measures 10.0” (254 mm) in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the permanently attached lid) reaches 13.2” (335 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the base. The engineered interior cavity yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The underside of the base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a deeply immersive, photorealistic tribute to America's pastime, utilizing a vibrant 360-degree stadium canvas framed by classic baseball stitching motifs.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body of the vase features a continuous, high-resolution graphic depicting a dramatically lit baseball stadium at night, complete with glowing stadium lights and a blurred crowd in the stands.
The Front Narrative: Centered against the stadium background is an action scene of a batter in the follow-through of a powerful swing, with a baseball suspended mid-air. In the foreground rests home plate, serving as the foundation for the presentation plaque.
The Rear Narrative: The back of the vessel continues the sweeping stadium scene, featuring a solitary black baseball bat standing vertically on home plate, with a pitcher visible in the distance on the mound.
The Lid: The domed lid (1.4" in height) is integrated and permanently attached to the vessel. It features a smooth black finish, bordered at the bottom rim by a prominent red baseball stitching pattern.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base is finished in a smooth, matte black coating. It is accented horizontally by two distinct bands of thick, red 3D baseball stitching wrapping around its circumference.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). This CAD-modeled design is engineered for rapid, high-fidelity production of the thick-walled storage base and vessel without minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The central vessel requires a premium, high-resolution wrap or meticulous airbrushing to capture the photorealistic stadium depth and lighting. The lid and base utilize a premium matte or satin black finish, with the raised stitching patterns hand-painted in a vibrant red enamel. The complete assembly is sealed under a protective clear coat.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the threaded brass inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the bottom cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front face of the vessel, resting gracefully on the graphic of home plate, is a framed presentation plaque featuring classic serif typography. The standard formatting reads:
"Forever in the Game"
"John Stevenson" (Placeholder Name)
"1954 - 2023" (Placeholder Dates)
Personalization Integration: Because the vessel canvas is highly detailed and graphic-driven, direct mechanical engraving is not recommended.
Customization Note: For specific families, the digital CAD/graphic file can be updated prior to manufacturing to integrate the deceased's specific name and dates natively onto the home plate plaque. Alternatively, the plaque space can be left blank during production to accommodate a custom-printed vinyl decal applied by the funeral home distributor.
