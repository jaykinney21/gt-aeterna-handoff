Technical Design Specification: "Forever In Motion" Dance Tribute Urn
SKU: LN-ART-MOTION
Series: Lifestyle & Novelty / Performing Arts Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring a dedicated, decorative pedestal base that supports a classic, amphora-style vessel.
The Vessel: A broad-shouldered, curved jar form engineered to serve as the primary decorative canvas, capped with a low-profile, removable domed lid.
External Vessel Height: 9.0” (229 mm) Height (Vessel only).
Overall Diameter: 7.0” (178 mm) Maximum Diameter.
The Base: A separate, circular pedestal base provides a formal display foundation and houses the primary access point.
Base Dimensions: 7.0” (178 mm) Overall Diameter x 2.2” (56 mm) Height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the lid) reaches exactly 11.2” (284 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the base. The engineered interior cavity yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The underside of the base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, high-contrast tribute to the performing arts and the celebration of life, utilizing high-relief silver sculpting set against a deep black canvas.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body is finished in a smooth, matte black. It is heavily structured by matching geometric, diamond-patterned metallic silver bands wrapping around the upper chest of the urn and the perimeter of the pedestal base.
The Front Narrative: The primary focal point features detailed, high-relief silver figures of two dancers in mid-leap—one adorned with a feathered headdress and one trailing a long, fluid ribbon.
The Front Plaque: Integrated seamlessly into the top tier of the base, directly below the dancers, is a silver-framed rectangular presentation plaque with black sans-serif typography reading:
"FOREVER IN MOTION"
"• A DANCE THROUGH LIFE •"
The Rear Narrative: The back of the vessel features a large, beautifully sculpted metallic silver infinity symbol (lemniscate), representing eternal spirit and enduring memory.
The Lid: The domed black lid is formally separated from the main body by a thin, elegant metallic silver trim ring.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). The design relies on thick, durable resin walls to securely contain the ashes while allowing for the crisp, high-fidelity detailing required for the geometric bands and the dynamic dancers.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The vessel requires a premium matte or satin black base coat, while the raised decorative elements, geometric bands, and typography demand precise masking and finishing with a brilliant metallic silver enamel.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the threaded brass inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the bottom cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: The integrated "FOREVER IN MOTION" plaque on the front acts as the permanent thematic anchor of the piece.
Personalization Integration: Because the front canvas is highly detailed with structural framing and raised elements, personalization for families (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) is best applied to the rear of the vessel.
Customization Note: The smooth matte black space on the rear of the pedestal base, or the negative space directly beneath the silver infinity symbol, serves as an ideal canvas for custom-cut, high-adhesion silver vinyl lettering to perfectly match the existing metallic accents.
