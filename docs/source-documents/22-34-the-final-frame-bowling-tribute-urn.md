1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a functional, two-part architectural approach where the structural rectangular base operates as the primary high-capacity ash chamber, allowing the upper spherical element to serve as a monumental, decorative centerpiece.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, rectangular plinth design providing a wide, stable footprint to support the sphere.
External Dimensions: 10.0” Width x 7.0” Depth x 4.5” Height.
Internal Dimensions: 9.25” L x 6.25” W x 3.25” H.
The Decorative Finial: Resting securely atop the base is a large, 8.5” diameter sphere styled as a bowling ball, bringing the total structural height to 13.0”.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access located beneath the rectangular base. The engineered compartment provides an internal volume of 200+ cubic inches. The access panel is a custom-cut, rectangular metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, colorful tribute to the sport of bowling, utilizing deep marbled textures and formal gold banding.
The Bowling Ball Sphere: The upper finial is bisected by a prominent, recessed gold band featuring the engraved text "THE FINAL FRAME". The upper hemisphere displays a dynamic, swirling marbled pattern blending deep blues, teals, and gold flakes. The lower hemisphere is finished in a solid, mottled navy blue.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber mirrors the color palette of the sphere. The primary vertical walls are finished in solid navy blue with gold trim lines highlighting the architectural steps. The flat top surface of the base (beneath the sphere) features the same blue/teal/gold marbled pattern as the top of the ball.
The Pin Motifs: The front plaque zone is creatively framed by two small, high-relief, sculpted white bowling pins with classic red neck stripes.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Marbled Finish: The complex blue/teal/gold swirling pattern on the upper hemisphere and the base top requires a specialized hydro-graphic dipping process or an advanced multi-axis UV digital print to ensure a seamless, organic flow of the colors.
Paint Application: The solid navy sections are finished with a rich, satin clear coat. The central text band and architectural trim lines are meticulously color-filled and painted with a brilliant metallic gold enamel.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing to ensure the bottom access panel remains secure without risking stripped threads.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Primary Band: The bold text "THE FINAL FRAME" on the equator of the sphere acts as the permanent, standard title for this design.
The Modular Plaque System: Individual personalization is integrated directly onto the front face of the rectangular base, elegantly flanked by the 3D bowling pins.
Plaque Details: Features a uniquely shaped, stepped brass presentation plaque. The prototype highlights the standard fill-in-the-blank engraving template:
"Name: [Blank Line]"
"Born: [Blank Line]"
"Date Deceased: [Blank Line]"
Manufacturing Note: Distributors and funeral homes can easily swap out this default template plate for customized, laser-engraved brass or acrylic plaques to honor the individual bowler.
