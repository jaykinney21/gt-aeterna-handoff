2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Internal Volume: The primary storage compartment is housed securely within the circular pedestal base, engineered to provide a full adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Access Method: Secure, discreet bottom-loading access is located through the underside of the circular pedestal base.
Closure Hardware System: The bottom assembly utilizes a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate crafted from brushed stainless steel or aluminum.
Flush Fit Integration: The rectangular metal access plate is engineered to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip on the bottom of the urn.
Security Anchors: The access plate is securely fastened using four countersunk machine screws. These screws thread directly into four pre-modeled, pre-installed brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed structural walls to ensure maximum pull-out strength and a permanent seal.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The upper sculpture features a classic, rounded cookie jar profile finished in a warm, textured tan/light brown. Standing at the front of the jar is a highly detailed, 3D-sculpted figure of a grandmother wearing a floral dress, holding her hand to her head in a searching gesture with her glasses resting atop her head.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base matches the warm tan finish of the vessel. It features a continuous, decorative border wrapping around its circumference consisting of alternating sculpted chocolate chip cookies and pink hearts. The base is framed by thin, brown trim lines at the top and bottom edges.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a matching tan lid. It is whimsically adorned with a 3D-sculpted baby/toddler figure sitting next to a sculpted oversized chocolate chip cookie and a central round knob handle.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Narrative (Vessel Front): Mounted to the front of the cookie jar, right beside the grandmother figure, is a square plaque featuring playful, serif typography that reads: "Baby, Can you help Grandma find her glasses?"
Personalization Integration (Base Rear): The rear face of the pedestal base features a rectangular presentation plaque specifically designated for bespoke personalization.
Standard Formatting: The rear base plaque accommodates custom text, shown with the standard layout: "Forever In Our Hearts" followed by localized names and dates (e.g., "Name Here", "Jan 01, 1950 - Dec 31, 2024").
