Security Anchors: The access plate is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-modeled brass inserts.

Base Details: The base is decorated with a centered cross emblem and ornate scrollwork.
Narrative Text: The front bears the inscription: "His grace is sufficient". The back features a cross emblem, wheat-stalk motifs, and the citation "2 Corinthians 12:9".
4. Material & Finishing Protocols

Finish Details: Features a premium, ceramic-like finish.
Manufacturing Note: All dimensions are approximate, and slight variations may occur due to the nature of 3D printing.
