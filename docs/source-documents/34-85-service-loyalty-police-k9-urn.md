2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring a broad, circular pedestal base that operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, supporting a detailed, multi-element sculptural figurine above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The deep pedestal base functions as the sole hollow storage chamber. It is engineered to easily accommodate the full 200+ cubic inch requirement for one set of adult cremated remains, keeping the center of gravity low to stabilize the upper figurine assembly.
Access Method: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular base via a custom-cut, heavy-duty metal access plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal plate is secured utilizing four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass threaded inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls to guarantee maximum pull-out strength over time.
Environmental Seal: The bottom opening is engineered with a precision silicone gasket. When the stainless steel plate is secured, it compresses the gasket, ensuring a permanent, airtight, and moisture-resistant seal suitable for indoor or outdoor display.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a solemn, dignified tribute to law enforcement professionals and their K9 partners, utilizing classic memorial bronze finishing paired with striking symbolic accents.
The Central Canvas (The Figurine): The primary focal point is a highly detailed, 3D-sculpted police officer in full uniform, captured holding a lapel radio. Seated attentively beside the officer's left leg is a fully sculpted K9 partner (German Shepherd).
The Base Canvas: The wide circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It features a rich, dark navy blue finish with a subtle marbleized or stone-like texture.
Structural Accents: The navy blue base is formally structured by two distinct, recessed metallic gold accent rings at the top and bottom. The sides of the base are adorned with raised, metallic gold laurel leaf motifs.
The Diorama Floor: The top surface of the pedestal base, acting as the ground for the figures, is prominently decorated with a large, recessed metallic gold star bisected by a striking "Thin Blue Line" enamel fill.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing. The digital CAD file is fully optimized for High-Resolution 3D Printed Resin. This specific occupational design is perfectly suited for on-demand industrial 3D printing pipelines—such as Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) or Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) through manufacturing partners like JawsTec or Sculpteo. This allows for the rapid, high-fidelity production of the thick-walled storage base alongside the highly complex, multi-part geometry of the officer and K9 with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a premium multi-stage finishing protocol. The officer and K9 figures require a specialized faux-bronze finishing technique, utilizing dark shading washes and metallic highlighting to simulate cast metal. The base requires a hydro-graphic wrap or specialized painting application for the navy marble effect. The accent rings, laurel leaves, and the top star demand precise masking and finishing with a brilliant metallic gold enamel, completed with a vivid blue epoxy fill for the center line.
