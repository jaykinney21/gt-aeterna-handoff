2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a robust, dual-component architecture where a secure, wide-stance circular pedestal base functions as the primary ash storage foundation, supporting a soft-edged, "squircle" (rounded square) profiled vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The continuous hollow storage compartment is engineered to span both the interior of the expanded 8.50-inch pedestal base and the upper vessel. It is specifically designed to safely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches while maintaining a highly stable, low center of gravity.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts permanently embedded within the 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a serene, scenic tribute utilizing an earthy palette of creams and bronzes, contrasted by vivid celestial accents to depict a peaceful desert or rolling hill landscape.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features a smooth, matte cream finish representing an open sky. The lower half of the vessel is enveloped by a deeply textured, sculpted relief finished in metallic bronze, simulating rolling dunes or hills. Emerging from behind the dunes on the right side is a dimensional, bright orange setting/rising sun. Floating above the landscape is a recessed, dimensional blue crescent moon. The top rim is accented with a delicate bronze knot pattern.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base provides a stabilizing foundation. It is finished in matching metallic bronze and features a continuous, 360-degree relief of architectural archways (reminiscent of a bridge or aqueduct) with heavily textured interiors. The upper rim of the base is banded with a continuous double-stripe of vibrant blue and orange. The front center archway perfectly frames a secondary blue crescent moon.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a flat-top, soft-square (squircle) lid finished entirely in metallic bronze, framed by a continuous geometric rim pattern.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for advanced additive manufacturing utilizing high-strength 3D printed polymer resins. The design relies on thick structural walls to support the heavy-duty brass inserts, the flush-mount bottom closure system, and the detailed architectural relief on the base.
The Paint & Finish: This piece requires a precision multi-stage finishing protocol. The sky canvas demands a smooth, soft-touch matte cream finish. The sculpted dunes, archways, and lid require a rich, antiqued metallic bronze enamel application. The celestial elements (sun and moons) and the base banding require crisp, high-gloss applications of vivid orange and deep blue enamels.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative (Vessel Front): Integrated directly into the smooth cream sky on the front of the vessel is elegant, dark serif typography reading:
"Love reaches beyond the horizon."
Secondary Narrative (Vessel Rear): The corresponding smooth cream space on the rear of the vessel features matching typography reading:
"We'll meet again. Somewhere beyond sight."
Personalization Integration (Base Rear): The rear of the circular pedestal base replaces the center archway motif with a raised, bronze-framed presentation plaque specifically designated for bespoke personalization.
Standard Formatting: Features custom localized names and dates printed in dark typography (e.g., "Name Here / Jan. 1, 1950 / Dec. 31, 2024").
Customization Note: For on-demand production, the customized text can be natively integrated into the base's geometry prior to manufacturing, or applied post-production by 

the distributor using high-adhesion custom-cut vinyl lettering.
