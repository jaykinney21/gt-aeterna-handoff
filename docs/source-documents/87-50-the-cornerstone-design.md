2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a robust, dual-component monumental architecture where a secure, wide-stance circular pedestal base functions as the primary ash storage foundation, supporting a heavily faceted, architectural vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The continuous hollow storage compartment is engineered to span both the interior of the expanded 7.50-inch pedestal base and the upper vessel. It is specifically designed to safely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches while maintaining a stable, low center of gravity.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal access plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into four pre-installed, heavy-duty brass inserts permanently integrated into the structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal with maximum pull-out strength.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a grounded, earthy tribute to a foundational family figure, utilizing rough faux-stone textures contrasted by smooth river rocks and rich metallic bronze accents.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features a complex, heavily faceted geometric surface designed to replicate rough-hewn stone or chiseled granite, finished in a warm, earthy taupe/grey. Centered on the front is a recessed, bronze-finished rectangular relief plaque depicting a detailed, touching image of a large adult hand securely holding a small child's hand.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. The outer vertical wall of the base features a continuous, highly tactile 360-degree relief of tightly packed, multi-colored river rocks (shades of grey, brown, and charcoal). The base is formally framed at both the top and bottom edges by smooth, thick metallic bronze trim rings.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a slightly domed, circular lid that mirrors the tightly packed river rock texture of the base, bordered by a prominent metallic bronze rim.
Dimensional Accents: Small, raised purple hearts are strategically placed to anchor the typography on both the front and rear of the vessel.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for advanced additive manufacturing (such as high-strength 3D printed polymer resins) or heavy-duty cast composites. The design relies on thick structural walls to support the heavy-duty brass inserts, the flush-mount bottom closure system, and the deep relief sculpting of the river rocks.
The Paint & Finish: This piece requires a precision multi-stage finishing protocol. The primary faceted surfaces require a matte, textured finish to accurately simulate natural stone. The individual river rocks on the base and lid require careful, multi-tonal hand-finishing to create a realistic, organic color variance. The central relief plaque, the base rims, and the lid rim require a rich, antiqued metallic bronze enamel application. The small heart accents are finished in a subtle purple enamel.
