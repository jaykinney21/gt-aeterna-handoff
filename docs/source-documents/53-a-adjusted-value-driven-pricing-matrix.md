Strategic Rationale for the Adjusted Counts

Targeting Typical Family Dynamics: The $950 tier now explicitly caters to a standard nuclear family layout (e.g., one miniature urn for the spouse's mantle, two wearable/keychain pieces for the children). This prevents families from feeling forced to buy extra pieces a la carte just to cover immediate family members.

Maximizing the Premium Anchor: The $1,495 tier jumps to 6 included keepsakes. This targets extended families or generational sharing. While the perceived retail value of 6 items easily exceeds the $900 price difference from the base tier, your actual 3D manufacturing cost on those 6 small items remains highly controlled, protecting your net margin.

Controlled Capping: By restricting the Companion tier to only one miniature urn (which typically has a higher volumetric print cost and perceived value than a keychain), you maintain a clear incentive for larger families to upgrade to the Legacy tier if they want multiple display pieces for different households.
