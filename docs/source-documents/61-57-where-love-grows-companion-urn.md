2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, side-by-side companion architecture where an extra-wide, shared pedestal base operates as a dual-chamber secure ash storage foundation.
Internal Volume Strategy: The heavy base features a deep, continuous internal architecture split into two distinct chambers, each capable of holding a standard adult capacity (200+ cubic inches). Thick, reinforced walls ensure the durability of both chambers.
Access Method: Secure bottom-loading access is located on the underside of the base, featuring two separate, circular brushed aluminum access plates.
Hardware & Closure: Both metal plates are secured utilizing four countersunk stainless steel machine screws. These thread directly into heavy-duty brass threaded inserts molded directly into the 3D-printed structural walls to guarantee maximum pull-out strength over time.
Environmental Seal: Each bottom opening features a precision-fit silicone gasket. When the aluminum plates are tightened, the gaskets compress to ensure a permanent, airtight, and moisture-resistant seal suitable for indoor or outdoor display.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a highly detailed, hand-painted diorama celebrating enduring love and natural beauty, utilizing high-relief woodland character sculpting.
The Shared Base Canvas: The wide base represents a vibrant forest floor, heavily textured to simulate grass and moss, and dotted with small, hand-painted pink and white flowers. Small 3D-sculpted squirrels rest on the outer edges of the base.
The Twin Vessels: Rising from the base are two distinct tree trunk sculptures featuring deep, realistic wood bark textures.
The Front Narrative: Spanning the gap between the two trunks is a sculpted branch holding two vibrant blue "love birds" leaning into one another, crowned by three bright red hearts.
The Rear Narrative: The back of each tree trunk features a deeply carved "sweetheart" wood detail, traditionally framing personalized initials.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing. The complex, multi-part diorama is fully optimized for High-Resolution 3D Printed Resin. This CAD file is engineered specifically for your industrial, on-demand printing pipelines, allowing for the rapid production of the heavy companion base and detailed woodland elements with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a highly specialized, multi-stage, hand-painted finishing protocol. The trunks require layered brown washes to achieve realistic bark depth, while the diorama base requires meticulous dry-brushing for the grass and precision detailing for the tiny floral accents and woodland creatures. The complete assembly is sealed under a premium protective clear coat to preserve the vivid colors.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front face of the textured wood-look base are two gold-toned presentation plaques flanked by a carved wooden heart. They feature classic black serif typography reading:
Left Plaque: "HEART TO HEART, ALWAYS"
Right Plaque: "WHERE LOVE GROWS"
Personalization Integration: The rear of the tree trunks acts as a highly thematic personalization zone. The sculpted heart outlines are designed to mimic carvings in a tree.
Customization Note: The digital CAD file can be natively updated prior to 3D printing to deeply emboss specific initials (e.g., "J+M") directly into the resin bark on the back of each trunk, offering families a deeply bespoke, permanent romantic tribute.
