2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Architecture: This design utilizes a monumental architecture featuring a wide, circular pedestal base supporting a sleek, egg-shaped vessel above.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum). The plate is designed to sit completely flush within a recessed lip.
Hardware & Closure: The metal plate is secured utilizing four countersunk stainless steel machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass threaded inserts permanently embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, ensuring a precision-sealed, secure closure with maximum pull-out strength.
Internal Volume Strategy: The pedestal base itself provides a calculated structural cavity (7.00" internal usable diameter x 2.25" usable height, yielding approximately 86.59 cubic inches). This continuous internal chamber extends seamlessly up into the main hollow vessel to comfortably achieve the overall 200+ cubic inch capacity required for one set of adult cremated remains.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a bold, high-contrast tribute to streetwear and sneaker culture, utilizing dynamic color-blocking and distinct simulated material textures.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body features sweeping, curved panels in a vibrant blue, black, and white colorway. The blue and black panels feature a deeply modeled, highly tactile "pebbled leather" texture, while the white panels feature a distinct, stippled texture contrast.
The Base Canvas: The wide circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It is finished in the textured bright white, formally framed by thick, smooth blue accent rims at both the top and bottom edges.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with an integrated, flat-top circular lid finished entirely in the textured pebbled blue matching the main body.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing. The digital CAD file is fully optimized for industrial 3D printing. This design is perfectly suited for on-demand manufacturing pipelines leveraging Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) or Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) to rapidly produce the thick-walled storage base and highly detailed surface textures with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a specialized multi-stage finishing protocol. The deep textures (pebbled leather and stippling) are natively printed into the polymer structure, requiring precise, multi-color masking and finishing in matte black, bright white, and vibrant blue enamels. The complete assembly must be sealed under a premium protective clear coat to preserve the vivid colorway and premium handcrafted look.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative (Front): Integrated directly onto the front face of the textured white base is a raised, gold-framed rectangular presentation plaque with a black background. It features classic gold typography reading:
"RISE ABOVE EXPECTATIONS"
Personalization Integration: The rear of the pedestal base features an unblemished, raised gold-framed presentation plaque matching the front, but left entirely blank. This space is specifically designated as the primary personalization zone, ideal for custom-cut gold vinyl lettering or a mechanically engraved metal insert applied by the funeral director.
