Honoring Their Legacy: A Guide to Your Aeterna Keepsake
Please accept our deepest sympathies. We know this is a delicate moment, and we have designed this process to be as simple, secure, and peaceful as possible. Please take your time and follow these steps to safely transfer a small portion of your loved one’s ashes into your Aeterna keepsake.
Your Kit Includes:
Precision Micro-Screwdriver
Stainless Steel Micro-Funnel
Tamper Pin
Permanent Thread-Locking Fluid
Aeterna Polishing Cloth
Step 1: Prepare Your Space Find a quiet, well-lit space. We recommend placing a clean, creased piece of paper on your table. This will catch any precious material that may miss the funnel, allowing you to easily return it to the primary urn.
Step 2: Open the Receptacle Locate the threaded micro-screw on your jewelry or keepsake (typically flush on the bottom or side). Using the provided precision screwdriver, gently turn counter-clockwise to remove the screw. Place the screw in a safe, visible spot.
Step 3: Transfer with Care Insert the narrow end of the micro-funnel into the chamber opening. Using a small spoon, place a very small amount of ashes into the funnel. Gently use the tamper pin to guide the ashes through the funnel and into the chamber. Note: Do not fill completely to the top; leave a small amount of space to ensure the screw can sit completely flush.
Step 4: Secure the Seal Before replacing the screw, apply a single, tiny drop of the provided thread-locking fluid onto the threads of the screw. Carefully insert the screw back into the piece and turn clockwise until it is flush and snug. Avoid over-tightening. The fluid will cure over the next 24 hours, creating a permanent, waterproof seal.
Step 5: Final Polish Use the Aeterna microfiber cloth to gently polish the exterior of your piece, removing any fingerprints or dust. Your loved one’s physical legacy is now safely secured.
