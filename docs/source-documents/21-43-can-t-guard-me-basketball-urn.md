Technical Design Specification: "Can't Guard Me" Basketball Urn
SKU: LN-SPT-HARDWOOD
Series: Lifestyle & Novelty / Sports Legend Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring a dedicated, decorative pedestal base that operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, supporting a classic, broad-shouldered amphora-style vessel above.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A highly stabilized, circular pedestal design providing a wide footprint to securely anchor the tall vessel.
External Base Dimensions: 7.2” (183 mm) Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 2.8” (71 mm) Base Height.
The Decorative Vessel: The upper sculptural assembly is a sleek, flared jar with an integrated, low-profile dome lid. The vase section alone measures exactly 10.0” (254 mm) in height.
The Lid: A low-profile, removable dome lid measuring 1.2" (30 mm) in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the lid) reaches exactly 12.8” (325 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the base. The engineered interior cavity yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The underside of the base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, high-contrast tribute to the sport of basketball, utilizing a sleek matte black canvas heavily accented with vibrant, 3D-sculpted red detailing and character art.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body is finished in a smooth, matte black coating. It is enveloped by continuous, sweeping, raised red lines mimicking the structural seams of a stylized basketball.
The Front Narrative: Centered prominently on the front chest of the vessel is a high-relief, 3D-sculpted figure of a basketball player in mid-dribble. The entire figure and the basketball are finished in a brilliant, matching red enamel.
The Rear Narrative: The back of the vessel focuses on the continuous, sweeping red seam lines wrapping around the unblemished black canvas.
The Lid: The domed lid continues the visual motif, featuring a black top with raised red radiating lines meeting at a central point, formally framed by a thick red trim ring at its base.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base is finished in matching matte black, heavily structured by flowing, wavy 3D red lines framing the central presentation plaques.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). Fully optimized for your industrial catalog, this CAD-modeled design is perfectly suited for on-demand manufacturing pipelines leveraging Multi Jet Fusion or Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) through partners like JawsTec or Sculpteo. This allows for the rapid production of the thick-walled storage base and high-fidelity surface details with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The main vessel body, lid, and base require a premium matte or satin black finish. The raised decorative seam lines, the structural bands, and the intricate 3D player figure demand precise masking and finishing with a vibrant red enamel.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the threaded brass inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the bottom cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front face of the pedestal base is a raised, red-framed rectangular presentation plaque featuring bold, red sans-serif typography reading:
"CAN'T GUARD ME"
Personalization Integration: The rear of the pedestal base is specifically designed to function as the primary personalization zone. It features a matching, empty red-framed plaque space, perfectly continuing the design symmetry.
Customization Note: Funeral home distributors can easily apply custom-cut, high-adhesion red vinyl lettering (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) to this blank rear plaque. Alternatively, the CAD file can be updated prior to 3D printing to incorporate raised or recessed custom typography directly into the base geometry.
