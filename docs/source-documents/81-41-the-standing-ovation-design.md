2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Access Method: Secure bottom-loading access is located through the underside of the pedestal base.
Closure Hardware System: The hardware assembly requires a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate made of brushed stainless steel or aluminum.
Flush Fit: The rectangular metal access plate sits flush within a precisely recessed lip.
Security: The plate is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into four pre-modeled brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed walls.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body is sculpted to resemble heavy, drawn red theatrical curtains that are accented with gold stars and formally tied back with gold tassels.
The Front Stage: The opening between the front curtains reveals a glowing gold background featuring a black silhouette of a cheering, applauding audience.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base is finished in a deep red color and bordered by metallic gold trim rings on both its top and bottom edges.
Base Embellishments: Centered on the front of the pedestal base are prominent, dimensional gold comedy and tragedy theatrical masks, flanked symmetrically by gold laurel wreaths.
The Lid: The top of the vessel is capped with a domed circular lid adorned with gold stars and crowned with a central spherical gold finial.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Narrative (Vessel Front): Displayed prominently on the gold background above the audience silhouette is the text: "THANK YOU FOR A LIFETIME OF LAUGHTER AND STANDING OVATIONS." positioned above a small heart.
Secondary Narrative (Vessel Rear): The back of the main vessel features an illuminated circular gold cutout with the text: "You made the world brighter with your smile, your stories, and your spirit." anchored by a small heart.
Personalization Integration (Base Rear): The rear face of the pedestal base features a rectangular gold presentation plaque specifically designated for bespoke personalization.
Standard Formatting: The base plaque accommodates custom names and dates, displaying standard placeholder text formatting as "Name Here", "Jan. 1, 1950", and "Dec. 31, 2024", anchored by a small heart.
