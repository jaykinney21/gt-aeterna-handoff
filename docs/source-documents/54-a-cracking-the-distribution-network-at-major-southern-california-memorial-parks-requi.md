The B2B Memorial Counselor Strategy

Bypass Corporate Procurement: Don't just email the central buying office. Build relationships directly with family service counselors. They sit across the table from grieving families and need unique, comforting solutions to offer when a standard brass vase falls flat.

The Zero-Risk Margin Incentive: Pitch the $950 and $1,495 bundles at a wholesale rate to the cemetery. Because you require no minimum order quantities, the cemetery captures a high-margin retail markup without dedicating a single dollar to inventory or warehouse space.

The Physical "Trojan Horse": Drop off a flawless, weighted 10-inch prototype (such as the Pilot or Bred Legacy design) for their consultation room. The ceramic-like finish and emotional weight will sell the concept far better than a digital catalog.

Direct-to-Family Interception

Hyper-Local Geo-Targeting: Run highly specific search ads within a tight radius of these parks, stretching out through the Inland Empire. Target niche keywords like "Inglewood Park columbarium urn size" or "Forest Lawn niche requirements."

Compliance as a Service: Publish beautifully designed, grief-conscious guides on your website detailing the exact niche dimensions for these specific locations. When families search for the rules, they find Aeterna as the effortless, compliant, and premium solution.
