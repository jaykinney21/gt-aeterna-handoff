Aeterna’s packaging must mirror the fine-art positioning of your primary $500 to $1,500 custom urns, ensuring these companion pieces deliver a premium, comforting unboxing experience for the family.

Presentation Packaging Architecture

Exterior Presentation: Soft-touch matte black or midnight blue rigid two-piece boxes. The top lid must feature a subtle, blind-debossed or gold-foil stamped Aeterna logo.

Interior Cushioning: Custom-routed, high-density EVA foam inserts lined with premium plush velvet to cradle the jewelry or miniature keepsake securely during transit.

Digital Suite Onboarding: A heavy-stock textured card resting on top of the product containing instructions on how to scan the piece's micro-QR code to activate and populate the complimentary digital memorial biography.

Care Elements: A branded microfiber polishing cloth to maintain the PVD and 18k gold finishes.

Memorial Filling & Transfer Kit (Mandatory Inclusion)

Micro-Funnel System: A polished stainless steel or aluminum micro-funnel and tamper pin specifically sized for the 0.10 cc to 0.25 cc chambers.

Sealing Agent: A single-use micro-tube of industrial-grade thread-locking fluid (e.g., Loctite 242) to permanently secure the countersunk closure screw after the family transfers the ashes.

Instructions: A sensitive, clearly illustrated step-by-step guide detailing how to safely handle the cremated remains, fill the chamber, and torque the hermetic seal.

Drop-Shipping & Fulfillment Logistics

Strict White-Labeling: The manufacturing partner must operate entirely invisibly. No vendor logos, return addresses, or packing slips can be included inside or on the exterior shipping carton. All shipping labels must display Aeterna as the sender.

Transit Protection: The rigid presentation box must be suspended within a corrugated crush-proof exterior mailer (minimum 32 ECT) utilizing eco-friendly void fill to prevent transit damage.

API Integration & SLA: Upon completion of the on-demand fabrication (5-7 days), the supplier must pack and tender the shipment to the carrier within 24 hours, automatically pushing the tracking data back to your sales platform via API.
