Technical Design Specification: "Mom's Tough Love" Urn
SKU: LN-FAM-TOUGHLOVE
Series: Lifestyle & Novelty / Family Heritage Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a classic, monumental cylindrical architecture. Unlike bottom-loading novelty designs, this highly narrative vessel features a deep, continuous interior chamber accessed from the top, providing a highly stable, flat-bottomed foundation.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the base to the top of the lid's knob handle) reaches exactly 13.0” (330 mm).
Maximum Diameter: The broad center of the vessel measures 8.0” (203 mm) across.
Base Architecture: The vessel rests on a flat, stable circular base measuring 5.5” (140 mm) in diameter, featuring a smooth base with a round rim. It is engineered to accommodate an optional non-slip protective pad to safeguard display surfaces.
Weight Profile: The thick-walled construction results in a substantial, premium heirloom feel, with an approximate empty weight of 2.5 lbs (1.1 kg).
2. Access & Volumetrics
Access Method: Secure top-loading access via a smooth, wood-look domed lid featuring a central knob handle. The lid measures 5.5” (140 mm) in diameter.
Internal Capacity: The deep, continuous internal chamber yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches, engineered to comfortably accommodate one set of adult cremated remains.
Closure & Security: The upper rim of the vessel and the interior of the lid feature precision-machined threads designed for a tight, permanent seal.
Permanent Sealing: The threading architecture includes a dedicated sealing groove, specifically engineered to capture and hold permanent cyanoacrylate adhesive or silicone sealant applied by the funeral director.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a highly narrative, humorous, and affectionate tribute to maternal discipline, utilizing a 360-degree, high-relief sculpted diorama wrapping the entire vessel.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body acts as a continuous 3D canvas depicting a classic kitchen scene, complete with sculpted wooden cabinetry and countertops in the background.
The Front Narrative: Centered prominently on the front is a sculpted figure of a stern mother wearing a floral apron, holding a wooden rolling pin. She is looking down at a sculpted figure of a young boy holding his hands up defensively.
The Speech Bubble: Positioned above the figures is an integrated, raised speech bubble featuring bold black typography reading:
"BOY I BOUGHT YOU IN THIS WORLD AND I WILL TAKE YOU OUT!"
The Rear Narrative: The back of the vessel continues the 3D scene seamlessly, showing the backs of the mother and son figures against the kitchen cabinet background, highlighting details like the tied bow of the mother's apron.
The Base Plaque: Integrated directly onto the front of the smooth, wood-look base is a raised, sculpted presentation banner featuring classic typography reading:
"MOM'S 'TOUGH LOVE'"
"- AIN'T NOBODY LIKE HER"
4. Material & Manufacturing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly outlined in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed High-Resolution Resin). The digital CAD file relies on thick walls for structural strength and durability, making it fully suitable for both indoor and outdoor memorial display.
Manufacturing Pipeline: This CAD-modeled design is optimized for on-demand industrial 3D printing workflows, allowing for the rapid, high-fidelity production of both the precision threads and the highly complex 3D character relief with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece requires a meticulous, multi-stage finishing process. While the base and lid utilize a faux-wood finishing technique, the 3D figures, clothing, cabinetry, and speech bubble demand a highly detailed, hand-painted finish to bring the characters and the kitchen environment to life. The complete assembly is sealed under a premium protective clear coat.
