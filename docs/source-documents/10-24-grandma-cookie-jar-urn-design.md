Technical Design Specification: "Grandma Cookie Jar" Urn
1. Dimensional & Volumetric Data
Overall Dimensions (Urn with Base): 10.75 in (273 mm) Height x 9.00 in (229 mm) Overall Diameter.
Urn Dimensions (without Base): 8.00 in (203 mm) Height x 7.25 in (184 mm) Maximum Diameter.
Base Dimensions (External): 2.75 in (70 mm) Height x 9.00 in (229 mm) Diameter.
Base Internal Depth: 2.50 in (64 mm).
Storage Capacity: 200+ cubic inches, designed for one set of adult cremated remains.
2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Internal Storage: The primary storage chamber is housed securely within the circular pedestal base.
Access Method: Features secure, dignified, bottom-loading access through the underside of the circular pedestal base.
Closure Hardware System: Utilizes a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits flush within a recessed lip.
Security Anchors: The access plate is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-modeled brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed walls.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
Vessel Aesthetic: The upper vessel is designed as a cookie jar, featuring a 3D-sculpted grandmother figure with her glasses on her head, and a small child standing beside her.
Base & Lid Details: The pedestal base has a warm, wood-look finish with pink floral and heart accents. The lid features a sculpted cookie top.
Narrative Text: The front of the vessel features the text: "Baby, Can you help Grandma find her glasses?". The base features the text: "How I wonder what you are".
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Fabricated using 3D printed resin with a ceramic-look finish.
Finish Details: Features hand-painted details with warm wood and pink accents.
Manufacturing Note: Dimensions are approximate, as minor variations may occur due to 3D printing and finishing.
5. Typography & Personalization
Personalization (Base Rear): The rear face of the pedestal base includes a designated area for bespoke text, formatted for the name, date of birth, and date of death.
