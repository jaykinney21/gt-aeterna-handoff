1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, high-capacity architectural approach specifically engineered as a companion urn. The structural circular base operates as a dual-chamber ash receptacle, allowing the upper sculpture to serve as a deeply emotional centerpiece honoring two individuals.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, circular plinth design providing a wide, stable footprint to support the heavy sculpture.
External Dimensions: 10.0” Overall Exterior Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 4.0”.
Internal Compartments (Companion Capacity): The interior is bisected by a central dividing wall, creating two separate, equal-sized storage chambers.
Dimensions per Chamber: 4.5” Usable Width x 4.0” Usable Height.
Volume Yield: Each side provides 200+ cubic inches of space, resulting in a Total Usable Volume of 400+ cubic inches. This comfortably holds two sets of adult cremated remains.
Access & Hardware: Bottom-loading access is located beneath the circular base. A single, large custom-cut square brushed metal plate covers the access point to both chambers. It sits flush and is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts for a dignified, secure seal.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a poignant, romantic tribute to enduring love, utilizing sweeping organic lines and striking material contrasts.
The Sculpture: A high-relief, fully realized 3D abstract sculpture of a couple locked in a deep, comforting embrace. The sweeping, stylized figures cradle two vibrant, textured red hearts between them.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber is finished in a warm, dark-toned woodgrain. The base is beautifully detailed with small, 3D-sculpted red hearts flanking the personalization plaques on both the front and rear faces.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for Additive Manufacturing utilizing High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites to achieve the complex curves and dual-chamber base.
The Wood Finish (Base & Figures): The printed PLA base and sweeping figures must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process—such as an advanced hydro-graphic dip or meticulous hand-painted faux-bois texturing—to perfectly simulate a highly polished, natural burl wood finish with deep, flowing grain lines.
The Heart Accents: The two large central hearts and the four smaller base hearts require a specialized finish. They are painted in a deep crimson red with a subtle marbled/mottled texture, sealed under a premium high-gloss clear coat to simulate polished resin or blown glass.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing to ensure the metal access panel remains completely secure.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Dual Plaque System: Personalization is integrated directly onto the curved front and rear faces of the circular base, elegantly bracketed by the small red hearts.
Front Branding: Features an elegant, brushed-brass presentation plaque with notched corners, secured with faux-rivet tacks. The standard engraving features classic serif typography reading:
"The Embrace"
Rear Plaque: Features a matching brushed-brass plaque on the back face with the standard engraving:
"Together Forever"
"Two Hearts, One Love"
Manufacturing Note: Because these plates mount to a curved surface, replacement plaques utilized by funeral home distributors for custom laser-engraving (to add the names and dates of both individuals) must be pre-rolled to perfectly match the 10.0" diameter radius of the base to ensure a seamless, flush mount.
