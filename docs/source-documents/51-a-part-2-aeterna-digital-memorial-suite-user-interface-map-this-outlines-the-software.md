Part 2: Aeterna Digital Memorial Suite – User Interface Map This outlines the software flow when a family member or friend scans the micro-QR code on the back of the keychain or pendant.
Phase 1: Activation & Onboarding (First-Time Scan by Family)
Scan & Welcome: Scanning the QR code opens a secure, elegant web app on their phone. Screen reads: "Welcome to Aeterna. Begin a Legacy."
Account Creation: Family member creates a secure admin account (Email/Password or Apple/Google Sign-In).
Link Keepsake: The system automatically pairs that specific QR code’s unique ID to the new digital profile.
Initial Setup: Guided prompts to upload a hero portrait, enter the name, and input "Sunrise" (birth) and "Sunset" (passing) dates.
Phase 2: The Visitor View (The Public Memorial Page) This is what friends and extended family see when they are allowed to scan the piece.
Hero Section: A beautiful, full-bleed vertical portrait of the individual. As the user scrolls up, the image subtly fades into a dark, elegant background.
The Header: The individual's full name in elegant typography, with their dates, and a custom epitaph (e.g., "A Life of Flight" or "Fought the Good Fight").
The Biography (Legacy Story): A clean, readable text block for the obituary or life story.
Media Gallery: A masonry-style grid where visitors can tap to view high-resolution photos or play videos celebrating the person's life.
Tribute Wall: A moderated digital guestbook where friends can leave text notes, light a virtual candle, or share a memory.
Phase 3: The Family Admin Dashboard (Private UI) Accessed by the family via a secure login.
Profile Editor: Easy tools to update the biography, add tags, or change the hero image.
Media Manager: Drag-and-drop interface to upload, crop, and organize photos and videos.
Privacy & Access Controls:
Public Mode: Anyone who scans the QR code can view the memorial.
Protected Mode: Scanning the QR code prompts for a 4-digit PIN (shared only with close family).
Tribute Moderation: Toggle the ability to approve comments before they appear on the Tribute Wall.
Linked Keepsakes Tracker: A dashboard showing how many Aeterna physical items (urns, keychains, necklaces) are currently linked to this specific digital profile.
