Technical Design Specification: "Royal Flush" Legacy Urn
1. Dimensional & Volumetric Data
Overall Dimensions (Urn with Base): 10.75 in (273 mm) Height x 9.50 in (241 mm) Overall Diameter.
Urn Dimensions (Vessel): 8.00 in (203 mm) Height x 7.25 in (184 mm) Maximum Diameter.
Base Dimensions (External): 2.75 in (70 mm) Height x 9.50 in (241 mm) Diameter.
Base Internal Depth: 2.25 in (57 mm).
Storage Capacity: 200+ cubic inches, designed for one set of adult cremated remains.
2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Internal Storage: The primary storage chamber is housed securely within the circular pedestal base.
Access Method: Features secure, bottom-loading access through the underside of the circular pedestal base.
Closure Hardware System: Utilizes a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits flush within a recessed lip.
Security Anchors: The access plate is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-modeled brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed walls.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
Vessel Aesthetic: The upper vessel features a cream/white finish adorned with a sculptural "Royal Flush" poker hand (Ace, King, Queen, Jack, 10 of spades) and a stack of sculpted poker chips at the base.
Base & Lid Details: The pedestal base is finished in a dark, premium wood-look material with gold trim. The lid features a central ornate gold emblem surrounded by symbols of playing card suits.
Narrative Text: The front base plaque bears the text "A LIFE WELL PLAYED" and "Royal Until the End".
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Fabricated using 3D printed resin with a premium finish.
Finish Details: Features hand-painted details with gold accents and plaques.
Manufacturing Note: Dimensions are approximate, as minor variations may occur due to 3D printing and finishing.
5. Typography & Personalization
Personalization (Base Rear): The rear face of the pedestal base includes a designated area for bespoke text, formatted for the name, date of birth, and date of death.
