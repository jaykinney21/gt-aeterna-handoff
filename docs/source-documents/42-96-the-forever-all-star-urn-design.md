Access Method: Secure, discreet bottom-loading access is located through the underside of the circular pedestal base.

Flush Fit Integration: The rectangular metal access plate is engineered to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip on the bottom of the urn.
Security Anchors: The access plate is securely fastened using four countersunk machine screws. These screws thread directly into four pre-modeled, pre-installed brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed structural walls to ensure maximum pull-out strength and a permanent seal.

3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The upper sculpture features a dynamic, sports-inspired motif designed to resemble a classic athletic sneaker. The primary body features a smooth black finish, contrasted by a textured red front "tongue" panel. It includes realistic, 3D-modeled white shoelaces threaded cleanly through metallic silver eyelets.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base is finished in a deep black with a rich red top surface. It features a horizontal red stitched-style band running around its circumference and is formally bordered by metallic gold trim rings at both the top and bottom edges.
Base Embellishments: Centered on the front of the pedestal base is a prominently raised, dimensional metallic gold heart framed within a gold circle, flanked by two smaller gold heart accents.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a gently domed lid featuring a textured red top surface, a metallic gold outer rim, and an ornate, centrally located loop finial.
