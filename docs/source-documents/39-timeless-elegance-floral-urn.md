1. General Form & Architecture 
This design utilizes a classic, monumental amphora-style architecture. Unlike bottom-loading novelty designs, this traditional vessel features a deep, continuous interior chamber accessed from the top, providing a highly stable, flat-bottomed foundation. 
● Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the base to the top of the domed lid) reaches exactly 12.0” (305 mm). 
● Maximum Diameter: The broad shoulders of the vessel measure 7.5” (191 mm) across. 
● Base Architecture: The vessel rests on a flat, stable base measuring 5.0” (127 mm) in diameter, engineered to accommodate an optional non-slip protective pad to safeguard display surfaces. 
● Weight Profile: The thick-walled construction results in a substantial, premium feel, with an approximate empty weight of 2.2 lbs (1.0 kg). 
2. Access & Volumetrics 
● Access Method: Secure top-loading access via a smooth, domed lid measuring 4.2” (107 mm) in diameter. 
● Internal Capacity: The deep, continuous internal chamber yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches, engineered to comfortably accommodate one set of adult cremated remains. 
● Closure & Security: The upper rim of the vessel and the interior of the lid feature precision-machined threads designed for a tight, permanent seal. 
● Permanent Sealing: The threading architecture includes a dedicated sealing groove, specifically engineered to capture and hold permanent cyanoacrylate adhesive or silicone sealant applied by the funeral director. 
3. Visual Elements 
The aesthetic is a refined, timeless tribute utilizing a striking high-contrast canvas of deep black accented by intricate gold linework. 
● The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body is finished in a smooth, matte or satin black. It is heavily enveloped by a complex, continuous floral and mandala-inspired motif that wraps 360 degrees around the vessel's chest and lower bowl. 
● Structural Accents: The intricate floral patterns are framed by multiple crisp, horizontal metallic gold pinstripes that segment the neck, shoulder, and base of the urn. ● The Lid: The domed lid provides a smooth, unblemished black visual resting space, bordered by a prominent metallic gold rim at its base.
