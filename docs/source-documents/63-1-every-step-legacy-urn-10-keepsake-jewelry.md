2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a reengineered, dual-component architecture where a secure circular pedestal base functions as the primary ash storage foundation, supporting a dynamic, modern sculptural vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The continuous hollow storage compartment is engineered to span both the interior of the pedestal base and the upper vessel. It is designed to securely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal access plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits entirely flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into heavy-duty brass inserts permanently integrated into the 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a sophisticated, modern tribute to an athlete or sneaker enthusiast, utilizing sweeping lines, athletic textures, and rich black and bronze color-blocking.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features a dynamic, asymmetrical form split by a sweeping, curved metallic bronze divider. The front-right facing section is a smooth, matte black finish. The rear-left facing section features a deeply modeled, stippled texture replicating athletic apparel or shoe tread, accented by a vertical, bronze "lacing" or stitch pattern.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It is finished in matte black and features three distinct horizontal bands of the raised athletic tread texture wrapped around its circumference. The base is framed at both the top and bottom edges by smooth, metallic bronze trim rings.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a flat-top, pill-shaped lid that mirrors the textured black finish of the rear vessel, formally bordered by a metallic bronze rim.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for advanced additive manufacturing utilizing high-strength 3D printed polymer resins. The design relies on thick structural walls to support the heavy-duty brass inserts, the flush-mount bottom closure system, and the sweeping geometry of the upper sculpture.
The Paint & Finish: This piece requires a precision masking and multi-stage finishing protocol. The smooth sections demand a flawless, soft-touch matte black finish, while the textured sections require careful detailing to ensure the dimpled pattern remains sharp. The sweeping divider, the vertical lacing, the text, and the base rims require a rich, brilliant metallic bronze enamel application.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative (Vessel): Integrated directly into the smooth matte black surfaces of the upper vessel is elegant bronze serif typography, anchored by small bronze hearts:
Front Face: "before the crowds there was you"
Rear Face: "every step i took began beside you"
Base Presentation Plaques: Both the front and rear of the textured pedestal base feature raised, bronze-framed presentation plaques specifically designated for thematic text and bespoke personalization.
Front Base Plaque: "FOREVER IN OUR HEARTS"
Rear Base Plaque (Standard Formatting): Features custom localized names and dates (e.g., "JOHN MICHAEL ANDERSON / MAY 15, 1965 - APRIL 20, 2024").
Customization Note: For on-demand production, the customized rear text can be natively integrated into the base's geometry prior to manufacturing, or applied post-production by the funeral director using a custom-engraved metal insert or high-adhesion vinyl lettering.
