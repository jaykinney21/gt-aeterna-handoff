1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring a dedicated, decorative pedestal base that operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, supporting a classic, permanently sealed amphora-style vessel above.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A highly stabilized, circular pedestal design providing a wide footprint to securely anchor the tall vessel.
External Base Dimensions: 7.6” (193 mm) Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 2.7” (68 mm) Base Height.
The Decorative Vessel: The upper sculptural assembly is a sleek, broad-shouldered jar with an integrated, permanently attached dome lid. The vessel alone measures 10.0” in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the permanently attached lid) reaches 12.7” (323 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the base. The engineered interior cavity yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The underside of the base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, high-contrast tribute to the sport of tennis, utilizing a sleek matte black canvas heavily accented with sharp metallic silver detailing and geometric patterns.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body is finished in a smooth, matte black coating.
The Front Narrative: Centered prominently on the front chest of the vessel is a crisp, metallic silver silhouette of a female tennis player in the follow-through of a powerful swing.
The Rear Narrative: The back of the vessel remains an unblemished, smooth black canvas, emphasizing the clean lines of the amphora shape.
The Lid: The domed lid is integrated and permanently attached to the vessel. It features a high-contrast, black and white checkered or woven geometric pattern, visually referencing the grip tape or intricate stringing of a tennis racket.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base is formally structured by heavy, raised metallic silver borders. Between the borders, the base features a continuous, deeply embossed diamond-lattice pattern in silver, mimicking the geometry of a tennis net.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: The primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). Fully optimized for your industrial catalog, this CAD-modeled design is engineered for on-demand manufacturing pipelines, allowing for the rapid production of the thick-walled storage base and vessel without minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The main vessel body requires a premium matte or satin black finish, while the raised decorative net pattern, the lid, and the central silhouette demand precise masking and finishing with a brilliant metallic silver enamel.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the threaded brass inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the bottom cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated seamlessly into the front face of the diamond-lattice base is a silver-framed rectangular presentation plaque featuring classic black serif typography reading:
"FOREVER IN MOTION"
The plaque is flanked by two faux-rivet details.
Personalization Integration: Because the base utilizes a heavily textured, embossed silver surface, direct mechanical engraving on the base is not recommended.
Customization Note: The smooth matte black space on the rear of the main vessel serves as an ideal canvas. Funeral home distributors can easily apply custom-cut, high-adhesion silver vinyl lettering (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) to perfectly match the existing metallic silver silhouette on the front.
