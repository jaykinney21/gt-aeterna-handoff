# Questions to ask

Historical extracted project source. Preserve its design and prompt details as source intent; current October 4 worksheets and the repository README govern later decisions. Dimensions, prices, product claims and suggested tolerances in this source are unverified until checked. Private sharing links and personal contact details are omitted.

Questions to ask:

• What's your direct cremation rate?
• Do you offer partnership/referral pricing for coordination services?
• What information do you need from me to process a family?
• How do you prefer to receive referrals? (Phone, email, online form)
• Do you handle transportation, or do I coordinate with a separate transport service?
• Turnaround time from intake to cremation?
• How are remains returned to families?
