Technical Design Specification: Black & Gold Legacy Urn

1. Dimensional & Volumetric Data

Overall Dimensions (Urn with Base): 10.75 in (273 mm) Height x 7.50 in (191 mm) Overall Diameter.

Urn (with Lid, without Base): 8.00 in (203 mm) Height x 7.25 in (184 mm) Maximum Diameter.

Lid Diameter: 4.20 in (107 mm).

Base External Dimensions: 2.75 in (70 mm) Height x 7.50 in (191 mm) Base Diameter.

Base Internal Storage: 2.25 in (57 mm) Internal Depth x 7.50 in (191 mm) Diameter.

Internal Capacity: 200+ Cubic Inches (Standard adult capacity, engineered for one set of cremated remains).

2. Engineering & Structural Architecture

Access Method: Bottom-loading access through the underside of the circular pedestal base for secure and discreet placement.

Closure Hardware System: Custom-cut rectangular metal plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) seated flush within a recessed perimeter lip.

Security Fasteners: Four countersunk machine screws threaded directly into pre-modeled, pre-installed brass inserts embedded in the 3D-printed walls.

Internal Storage Placement: The primary 200+ cu. in. containment chamber is integrated directly within the base structure.

3. Visual & Sculptural Elements

Vessel Aesthetic: Lustrous high-gloss black finish adorned with intricate, hand-embossed floral and botanical scrollwork in antique gold.

Band Detailing: Triple horizontal decorative borders wrapping the neck, upper shoulder, and lower taper.

Lid Top View: Stepped black concentric rings framing a gold center medallion surrounded by a circular vine pattern.

Base Front Inscription: Inset rectangular plaque with ornate corner flourishes displaying: "LOVE BLOSSOMS FOREVER".

Base Perimeter: Circumferential filigree and gold leaf blossom reliefs continuing along the sides and rear.

Collection Sentiment: "Timeless Beauty. Forever Remembered."

4. Material & Finishing Protocols

Core Substrate: High-detail 3D-printed resin with a reinforced structural core.

Surface Finish: Multi-layer, premium high-gloss black coating with hand-painted metallic gold filigree, embossed accents, and protective clear lacquer.

5. Typography & Personalization

Base Rear Plaque: Gold-framed memorial plate configured with dedicated fields for bespoke typography:
