2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
Architecture: This design utilizes a monumental architecture featuring a wide, circular pedestal base supporting a sleek, egg-shaped vessel above.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum). The plate is designed to sit completely flush within a recessed lip.
Hardware & Closure: The metal plate is secured utilizing four countersunk stainless steel machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass threaded inserts permanently embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, ensuring a secure closure and maximum pull-out strength.
Internal Volume Strategy: While the base itself provides a calculated structural cavity (7.00" internal diameter x 2.25" usable height), the continuous internal chamber extends up into the main vessel to comfortably achieve the overall 200+ cubic inch requirement for one set of adult cremated remains.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a bold, high-contrast tribute to streetwear and sneaker culture, utilizing iconic color-blocking and distinct material textures.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body features sweeping, dynamic panels in the classic "Bred" (Black and Red) and white colorway. The black and white panels feature a deeply modeled, stippled "elephant print" texture, while the red panels and lid feature a classic pebbled leather texture.
The Base Canvas: The wide circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It is finished in the textured black elephant print, framed by thick, smooth red accent rims at both the top and bottom edges.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with an integrated, flat-top circular lid (6.75" diameter) finished entirely in the textured pebbled red.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing. The digital CAD file is fully optimized for industrial 3D printing. This design is perfectly suited for on-demand manufacturing pipelines leveraging Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) or Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) to rapidly produce the thick-walled storage base and highly detailed surface textures with absolutely no minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a specialized multi-stage finishing protocol. The deep textures (elephant print and pebbled leather) are natively printed into the polymer structure, requiring precise, multi-color masking and finishing in matte black, bright white, and vibrant red enamels. The complete assembly must be sealed under a premium protective clear coat to preserve the vivid colorway.
