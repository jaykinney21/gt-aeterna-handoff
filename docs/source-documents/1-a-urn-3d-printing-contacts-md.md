Tier-aware email strategy
Tier 1 (bureaus, lines 1–8): Use the structured cold-quote template (size in inches+mm, internal volume, material/finish + alternative, quantity, soft deadline, all 3 files attached, ask for unit price + lead time + shipping to CA + MOQ). These eight should all reply with a real quote.

Tier 2 (urn specialists, lines 9–12): Don't attach files in email #1. Send a short qualifying email instead:

"I'm an artist making creative one-off urns. I have STL and STEP files ready. Do you print from customer-supplied files, or only design in-house? Happy to share files once I know we're a fit."

If they say yes → send the full Tier-1 template. If they say no → drop them or ask if they'd consider a collab where you supply the design vision.

Tier 3 (Firebird Bronze): Treat like Tier 1, but mention "lost-wax bronze casting from a 3D-printed wax pattern, single piece, ~[size]" in the first line so it routes to the right person.


Notes & gotchas
California shipping: You're in Fontana, CA. JawsTec (ID), Xometry (MD network), Firebird (OR), PrintAWorld (NY), and In The Light Urns (Visalia, CA — could be a 3-hour drive for pickup) are your fastest US options. China-based (WeNext, FacFox) typically 7–14 days delivered with DHL.
Sculpteo and Shapeways: Both EU. Tariff/customs handling differs — Sculpteo includes EU-side, Shapeways' new ownership has only been ~16 months so confirm US import handling in your first email.
Shapeways caveat: Their old marketplace and shops did not come back, only the manufacturing service. So if anyone references "the old Shapeways," that part is gone.
Foreverence reality check: They're famous, but their model is "send us photos, we design and print." If you want them to print your design, that's a non-default request and they may say no. Worth one polite email to find out.
