1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, two-part architectural approach where the structural pedestal base operates as the primary high-capacity ash chamber, while the upper element serves as a monumental, decorative sculpture.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, square plinth design providing a wide, stable footprint for the tall sculpture.
External Dimensions: 8.5” Width x 8.5” Depth x 3.0” Height.
Internal Dimensions: 7.25” L x 7.25” W x 2.5” H.
The Decorative Finial: Resting securely atop the base is a highly detailed, 9.5” tall sculpted figurine of a jazz musician and an oversized saxophone, bringing the total structural height to 12.5”.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access located beneath the square base. The compartment provides an engineered internal volume of 210+ cubic inches. The access panel is a custom-cut, rectangular metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a soulful, deeply expressive tribute to a musician's life, utilizing traditional statuary aesthetics with a striking, dual-tone finish.
The Sculpture: A high-relief, fully realized 3D figurine of a musician in a tailored suit and fedora, passionately playing a tenor saxophone. He is leaning back against a massive, stylized, oversized saxophone that serves as the structural backbone of the finial.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber is finished in a deep, rich woodgrain with a stepped upper edge, creating a classic, formal foundation that resembles a stage for the performer.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Wood Finish (Base): The printed PLA base must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process—such as hydro-dipping or hand-painted faux-bois texturing—to perfectly simulate a natural, dark-stained wood aesthetic.
The Dual-Tone Metallic Finish (Sculpture): The PLA print must be finished to mimic traditional cast metals, utilizing a dual-tone approach. The musician figure is finished in a dark, oxidized bronze with a hand-rubbed antiquing wash to highlight the folds of his suit. The massive structural saxophone he leans against is finished in a brilliant, contrasting brass/gold enamel to catch the light.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the countersunk access panel can be securely opened and closed without stripping the plastic threads.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Dual Plaque System: Personalization is integrated directly onto the flat vertical faces of the square wooden base.
Front Branding: Features a rectangular, brushed-brass presentation plaque secured with faux-rivet tacks. The standard engraving features classic black typography reading: "JAZZ & SOUL ETERNAL".
Rear Personalization: Features a matching rectangular brass plaque on the back face. The prototype highlights the template layout for custom engraving: "FOR NAME / SUNRISE ❊ SUNSET".
Manufacturing Note: These flat mounting zones allow funeral home distributors to easily unscrew and swap out the default plates for localized, custom laser-engraved brass plaques to honor the individual musician's legacy.
