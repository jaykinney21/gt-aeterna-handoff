Margin Strategy & Positioning

Perceived Value Optimization: Grouping physical keepsakes with the digital suite creates a high perceived value that justifies the premium tier without drastically increasing your on-demand 3D printing and fulfillment hard costs.

Anchor Pricing: The $1,495 tier serves as a psychological anchor. It establishes the brand's fine-art market position while making the middle tier appear as the most logical, high-value choice for families wanting to share remains.

Frictionless Upselling: Families purchasing the foundation tier can seamlessly purchase additional keychains or bracelets via the digital suite portal months later when extended family members request their own connected memorials.

Cost Segregation: Because there are no minimum order quantities on the printed jewelry components, you hold zero inventory risk on the $1,495 bundle until the exact moment a family pays upfront.
