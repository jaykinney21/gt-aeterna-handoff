1. General Form & Architecture
This finalized design utilizes a highly functional, two-part architectural approach where the structural base operates as the primary high-capacity ash chamber, allowing the upper amphora vessel to serve as a deeply detailed, monumental centerpiece.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, circular plinth design providing a stable, grounded footprint.
External Dimensions: 10.0” Overall Exterior Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 4.6”.
The Decorative Finial: Resting securely atop the base is a classic, broad-shouldered amphora-style vase with a flat lid. The vase measures 10.0" in height. The overall structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the lid) reaches 14.6”.
Access & Volumetrics (Capacity Achieved): Bottom-loading access is located beneath the circular base. The engineered internal circular cavity measures 6.0” Diameter x 4.6” Height.
Volume Yield: This provides exactly 203 cubic inches of usable space, fully satisfying the minimum 200 cubic inch requirement for a standard adult.
Hardware: The access panel is a custom-cut, square brushed metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts in four places for a clean, secure, and seamless look.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, deeply symbolic tribute to ancient Egyptian culture, mythology, and the concept of eternal life, utilizing rich lapis lazuli tones and gold accents.
The Upper Vessel (Front): The primary vase features a light sand/papyrus textured background covered in classical hieroglyphs. The focal point is a highly detailed, colorful illustration of an Egyptian woman (reminiscent of a goddess figure) kneeling and pouring a continuous stream of water from a traditional jar, symbolizing the flow of eternal life.
The Upper Vessel (Rear): The back of the vessel features a prominent, vibrant winged scarab beetle holding a sun disk—a classic symbol of rebirth and regeneration—positioned above a large gold Ankh.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber provides a brilliant contrast, finished in a deep, textured lapis lazuli blue. It is beautifully detailed with a continuous, repeating perimeter pattern of alternating gold Ankhs (the key of life) and delicate gold hearts. The base is formally framed by recessed gold trim lines along both the top and bottom edges.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Stone/Papyrus Finish (Vase): The upper vessel is finished with a specialized satin/matte clear coat to simulate aged limestone or papyrus parchment. The complex hieroglyphs and colorful character artwork require advanced 360-degree UV rotary digital printing or a highly precise hydro-graphic wrap seamlessly applied to the curved surface.
The Lapis Finish (Base): The printed PLA base must undergo a multi-step finishing process to simulate painted wood or textured lapis stone. The repeating Ankh motifs, hearts, and perimeter trim lines must be meticulously hand-painted or color-filled with a brilliant metallic gold enamel.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the bottom brushed metal access panel remains completely secure without risking stripped plastic threads over time.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Plaque System: Personalization is integrated directly onto the curved front face of the circular blue base.
Plaque Details: Features an elegant, brushed-brass presentation plaque with notched/scalloped edges, secured with faux-rivet tacks. The standard engraving features classic serif typography flanked by two Ankh symbols, reading:
"☥ ENDLESS WATERS ☥"
Manufacturing Note: Because this plaque mounts to a curved surface, replacement plaques utilized by funeral home distributors for custom laser-engraving (to add localized names and dates) must be pre-rolled to perfectly match the 10.0" diameter radius of the base to ensure a seamless, flush mount against the blue texture.
