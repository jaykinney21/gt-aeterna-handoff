1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a monumental, rectangular chest/casket form factor. This shape maximizes internal capacity while providing broad, flat canvases for deep-recessed sculptural storytelling.
Form Factor: A substantial rectangular chest featuring a flat lid and clean, sharp architectural lines.
External Dimensions: 10.0” Width x 7.25” Depth x 7.25” Height.
Internal Dimensions: 9.25” L x 6.25” W x 6.75” H.
The Lid: The flat top features a large, contrasting dark rectangular inset panel (simulating black slate or acrylic), framed by a deeply carved, repeating geometric "star" or cross-stitch border pattern.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access located beneath the chest. The engineered compartment provides an ample internal volume of 200+ cubic inches, suitable for one adult. The access panel is a custom-cut, rectangular metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a powerful, emotional tribute that bridges historical legacy with modern, urban reality.
The Front Narrative (The Journey): The front face features a deeply recessed cavity that serves as a diorama-style frame. Inside this recess is a high-relief, 3D-sculpted element depicting a person's legs in mid-stride, walking across a raised threshold. The figure wears dark denim jeans and classic black-and-white canvas sneakers.
The Front Typography: Integrated seamlessly next to the sculpted legs within the recessed panel is a profound, deeply engraved quote: "I walk in the prayers the ancestors prayed."
The Rear Canvas: The back of the chest remains free of sculptural elements, showcasing the clean, unbroken woodgrain to serve as the primary personalization zone.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Wood Finish (Main Body): The printed PLA chest must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process—such as hydro-dipping or hand-painted faux-bois texturing—to perfectly simulate a rich, medium-dark walnut or mahogany wood finish.
The Sculpture Finish: The interior recessed diorama requires meticulous hand-painting. The jeans are finished in a matte navy/black to simulate denim fabric, while the classic sneakers are detailed with bright white laces, rubber toe caps, and satin black canvas.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the countersunk access panel can be securely opened and closed by families or funeral directors without stripping the plastic threads.
4. Typography & Personalization
Front Engraving: The foundational quote on the front must be deeply CNC-milled or laser-engraved into the PLA and color-filled with a crisp, matte black enamel to ensure high legibility against the wood-tone background.
The Rear Plaque System: Personalization is anchored to the rear face of the chest.
Plaque Details: Features a large, ornate brushed-brass presentation plaque with elegantly scalloped edges.
Customization Template: The default engraving template includes a classic "Tree of Life" motif at the top center, followed by clean, serif typography reading: "Name Here / Date of Birth / Date of Death". Distributors can easily replace this standard plaque with customized, locally laser-engraved brass or acrylic plates for the families they serve.
