2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a robust, dual-component architecture where a secure, wide-stance circular pedestal base functions as the primary ash storage foundation, supporting a classic amphora-style vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The continuous hollow storage compartment is engineered to span both the interior of the expanded 7.75-inch pedestal base and the upper vessel. It is specifically designed to safely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches.
Access Method: Secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal access plate.
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into four pre-installed, heavy-duty brass inserts permanently integrated into the structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal with maximum pull-out strength.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a striking, tech-inspired tribute utilizing a deep black canvas with a subtle stardust/speckled texture, brought to life by raised, metallic bronze circuit board motifs.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features a sweeping, curved profile finished in matte black with a delicate, speckled pattern. Intricate, raised metallic bronze "circuitry" lines and nodes trace dynamically across the surface. The front face centers around a glowing, raised bronze heart motif where the circuit lines converge.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base provides a heavy, stabilizing foundation. It matches the speckled black finish of the upper vessel. The outer vertical wall of the base features a continuous, wrapping pattern of the metallic bronze circuitry. The base is framed at both the top and bottom edges by smooth, thick bronze trim rings.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a flat-top, stepped circular lid finished in the matching speckled black. The center is anchored by a prominent, raised metallic bronze lightning bolt, formally bordered by a bronze outer rim.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for advanced additive manufacturing (such as high-strength 3D printed polymer resins) or heavy-duty cast composites. The design relies on thick structural walls to support the heavy-duty brass inserts and the flush-mount bottom closure system.
The Paint & Finish: This piece requires a precision multi-stage masking and finishing protocol. The primary black surfaces require a specialized textured clear coat or natively printed texture to achieve the speckled, "stardust" feel. The sweeping circuit lines, the central heart, the lid's lightning bolt, and the base trims require a rich, brilliant metallic bronze enamel application.
