## 1. Brand Overview & Positioning

### Brand 1: Graceful Tributes Online

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Service brand — cremation coordination & tribute events |
| **Website** | graceful-tributes.vercel.app |
| **Audience** | At-need families (just experienced a loss) + Pre-need families (planning ahead) |
| **Demographics** | All ages, all income levels, primarily Los Angeles metro area |
| **Tone** | Warm, caring, dignified, comforting, hopeful |
| **Philosophy** | *"Honor the Vessel. Celebrate the Spirit and Soul of Your Loved One."* |
| **Brand Colors** | Sage Green `#9CAF88` · Warm Cream `#F5F1EB` · Soft Terracotta `#D9A384` |
| **Visual Style** | Soft natural light, organic textures, nature imagery, warm gradients, serif + humanist sans-serif typography |
| **Emotional Register** | Empathy-first. Every post should feel like a gentle hand on the shoulder. |

### Brand 2: From Dust Co.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Product brand — 3D-printed urns as art, keepsakes, home decor |
| **Products** | "Vessels" — designer urns that double as art pieces |
| **Audience** | Millennials & Gen-X, ages 35–55, $140K+ household income, design-conscious, culturally engaged |
| **Tone** | Cool, artistic, modern, philosophical, edgy but respectful |
| **Tagline** | *"From Dust We Create."* |
| **Brand Colors** | Near Black `#1a1a1a` · Warm Brass `#C9A962` · Warm Cream `#F5F1EB` |
| **Visual Style** | High-contrast, gallery-quality photography, dark backgrounds, dramatic lighting, modern sans-serif typography, architectural settings |
| **Emotional Register** | Reverent confidence. Think: an art gallery that happens to honor the dead. |

## 3. Platform Strategy

### Graceful Tributes Online — Platform Breakdown

#### Instagram (PRIMARY — highest priority)
- **Role:** Emotional storytelling hub, visual portfolio of services, community building
- **Why:** Visual medium perfect for tribute event photography, flower arrangements, venue setups. Families share and tag memorial content here naturally.
- **Content Mix:** 40% Reels, 30% Carousel posts, 20% Stories, 10% Single-image posts
- **Key Features to Use:** Guides (for planning resources), Highlights (service categories, FAQs, testimonials), Collab posts with venues/florists, Close Friends (for grieving families support group)
- **Growth Strategy:** Local hashtags, geotags for LA venues, collaboration with grief counselors and hospice organizations
- **Goal:** 2,500 followers in 6 months

#### Facebook (SECONDARY — high priority)
- **Role:** Community hub, resource center, family engagement, events promotion
- **Why:** Older demographics (50+) are heavy Facebook users and are the primary decision-makers for funeral services. Facebook Groups create supportive communities.
- **Content Mix:** 30% Link posts (to blog/resources), 25% Video, 25% Photo galleries, 20% Community engagement posts
- **Key Features to Use:** Facebook Group ("Graceful Tributes Community" — grief support + planning tips), Events (for celebration of life workshops), Messenger (for direct inquiries), Reviews
- **Growth Strategy:** Local community groups, LA-area targeting, partnership with hospice and elder care organizations
- **Goal:** 1,500 followers + 500 group members in 6 months

#### TikTok (GROWTH — medium priority)
- **Role:** Education, myth-busting, humanizing the industry, reaching younger planners
- **Why:** Gen Z and young millennials are increasingly involved in end-of-life planning for parents/grandparents. The "death positive" movement thrives on TikTok.
- **Content Mix:** 60% Educational/informative, 25% Behind-the-scenes, 15% Trending audio with relevant messaging
- **Key Features to Use:** Duets (with relevant grief/wellness creators), Stitches, Series (multi-part educational content)
- **Growth Strategy:** Death-positive community, trending sounds with tasteful adaptations, "Things no one tells you about..." hooks
- **Goal:** 5,000 followers in 6 months (TikTok grows fastest)

#### Pinterest (SUPPLEMENTARY — lower priority)
- **Role:** Inspiration boards for celebration of life planning, SEO-rich traffic driver
- **Why:** People actively search Pinterest for event planning, memorial ideas, sympathy gifts. High-intent traffic.
- **Content Mix:** 80% Curated idea pins, 20% Original content pins linking to website
- **Key Features to Use:** Idea Pins (step-by-step planning guides), Boards organized by theme, Rich Pins with website metadata
- **Growth Strategy:** SEO-optimized pin descriptions, consistent pinning schedule, group boards
- **Goal:** 1,000 followers, 50K monthly impressions in 6 months

#### Twitter/X (MINIMAL — lowest priority)
- **Role:** Industry thought leadership, grief awareness advocacy, newsjacking relevant cultural moments
- **Why:** Limited direct ROI for this service category, but useful for brand authority and press visibility.
- **Content Mix:** 40% Thought leadership threads, 30% Retweets/community engagement, 30% Resource sharing
- **Goal:** 500 followers in 6 months. Low-effort, high-quality posting only.

### From Dust Co. — Platform Breakdown

#### Instagram (PRIMARY — highest priority)
- **Role:** Visual showcase, brand world-building, product launches, artist collaboration spotlights
- **Why:** Design-conscious millennials live on Instagram. The aesthetic nature of the products is tailor-made for the platform. This is your digital gallery.
- **Content Mix:** 35% Reels (process videos, reveals), 30% Carousel (design deep-dives, philosophy), 25% Stories (polls, behind-the-scenes, daily engagement), 10% Single-image (hero product shots)
- **Key Features to Use:** Shopping tags (when available), Highlights (Collections, Process, Philosophy, Press), Collab posts with designers/artists, Broadcast Channel ("The Dust Studio" — insider updates)
- **Growth Strategy:** Design community hashtags, art/architecture influencer collabs, feature on design blogs, gallery partnerships
- **Goal:** 5,000 followers in 6 months (broader product appeal drives faster growth)

#### TikTok (PRIMARY — highest priority, tied with Instagram)
- **Role:** Process videos, satisfying 3D-printing content, philosophical provocations, going viral
- **Why:** 3D printing content is HUGELY popular on TikTok. The "oddly satisfying" angle + death-positive cultural moment = viral potential. This is your discovery engine.
- **Content Mix:** 40% Process/making-of, 25% Product reveals, 20% Philosophical/thought-provoking, 15% Trending sound adaptations
- **Key Features to Use:** TikTok Shop (when eligible), Series, Live (studio sessions)
- **Growth Strategy:** 3D printing community, design TikTok, art TikTok, #deathtok crossover
- **Goal:** 10,000 followers in 6 months

#### Pinterest (SECONDARY — high priority)
- **Role:** Interior design inspiration featuring Vessels, gift idea positioning, evergreen traffic
- **Why:** Pinterest users actively search for home decor, unique gifts, and art pieces. Products can be positioned as "modern home decor" and "meaningful keepsakes" — both huge Pinterest categories.
- **Content Mix:** 70% Product-in-context lifestyle pins, 20% Process/behind-the-scenes, 10% Blog content pins
- **Key Features to Use:** Product Pins (link to shop), Idea Pins (styling guides), Boards organized by collection, aesthetic, and room
- **Growth Strategy:** SEO for "modern urns," "unique home decor," "memorial art," "3D printed art"
- **Goal:** 3,000 followers, 100K monthly impressions in 6 months

#### Facebook (SECONDARY — medium priority)
- **Role:** Community building, longer-form storytelling, customer reviews, targeted ads platform
- **Why:** Facebook's ad platform is the most sophisticated for targeting the 35–55 demographic with specific income and interest parameters.
- **Content Mix:** 30% Product storytelling, 25% Behind-the-scenes video, 25% Customer stories, 20% Design/art culture shares
- **Key Features to Use:** Shop, Reviews, Groups ("Designing Remembrance" — art + memorial culture), Ads Manager
- **Growth Strategy:** Targeted ads to design-conscious LA residents, interior design group engagement
- **Goal:** 2,000 followers in 6 months

#### Twitter/X (SUPPLEMENTARY — medium priority)
- **Role:** Design world engagement, philosophical micro-content, press/media relations, brand personality
- **Why:** Design Twitter is a real community. Philosophical one-liners and provocative questions about death/design perform well. Good for press pickup.
- **Content Mix:** 40% Philosophical/provocative tweets, 30% Design community engagement, 20% Product teasers, 10% Press/media
- **Growth Strategy:** Engage with design accounts, participate in design conversations, quote-tweet art/design content with brand perspective
- **Goal:** 2,000 followers in 6 months

## 4. Content Pillars

### Graceful Tributes Online — 5 Content Pillars

#### Pillar 1: 🕊️ Honoring Stories
**What:** Sharing stories of lives well-lived, tribute event highlights, and family testimonials  
**Why:** Emotional connection builds trust. Families choose providers they feel *understand* their grief.  
**Content examples:**
- "Celebrating [Name]" tribute event photo galleries (with family permission)
- Video testimonials from families
- "Their story, in their family's words" quote graphics
- Anniversary remembrance posts
- "What made them unforgettable" community prompt posts

**Frequency:** 2–3x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 2: 📋 Planning & Guidance
**What:** Educational content about cremation, celebration of life planning, pre-need preparation, and navigating the process  
**Why:** Reduces anxiety and positions Graceful Tributes as the knowledgeable, approachable expert. SEO and save-worthy content.  
**Content examples:**
- "5 things to know about direct cremation" carousel
- "Celebration of life vs. traditional funeral" comparison
- "Pre-planning checklist" downloadable/saveable post
- "Questions to ask when choosing a cremation provider" Reel
- Monthly "Ask Us Anything" Q&A session (Lives or Stories)

**Frequency:** 2x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 3: 🌿 Comfort & Healing
**What:** Grief support content, healing quotes, mindfulness practices, and community support  
**Why:** Positions the brand as a companion through the grief journey, not just a transactional service provider. Builds long-term community.  
**Content examples:**
- Grief affirmation quote graphics (branded with sage green/terracotta palette)
- "Gentle reminders for hard days" carousel series
- Grief counselor Q&As and expert collaborations
- Seasonal grief content (holidays, birthdays, anniversaries)
- Book/podcast recommendations for those grieving

**Frequency:** 2–3x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 4: 🌸 Celebration Inspiration
**What:** Event design ideas, venue showcases, personalization tips, DIY tribute elements  
**Why:** Helps families imagine what's possible and drives event bookings. Highly shareable and saveable content.  
**Content examples:**
- Event setup reveals and walkthroughs
- Theme ideas for celebration of life events (music lovers, gardeners, foodies, etc.)
- Venue partner spotlights (LA locations)
- "How to personalize a tribute on any budget" tips
- Seasonal/holiday-specific celebration ideas

**Frequency:** 1–2x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 5: 💜 Community & Culture
**What:** Team introductions, behind-the-scenes, community involvement, cultural sensitivity, and industry thought leadership  
**Why:** Humanizes the brand, shows diversity and inclusion, builds trust through transparency.  
**Content examples:**
- "Meet our team" features
- Behind-the-scenes of event preparation
- Cultural spotlight series (how different cultures honor their loved ones)
- Community partnerships and sponsorship highlights
- "Why we do this work" founder/team motivation stories

**Frequency:** 1–2x per week across platforms

### From Dust Co. — 5 Content Pillars

#### Pillar 1: 🏛️ The Art of the Vessel
**What:** Product showcases, collection reveals, design deep-dives, and the intersection of art and remembrance  
**Why:** Core product content. Positions Vessels as art objects worthy of display, not something to hide in a closet.  
**Content examples:**
- Hero product photography (dramatic lighting, gallery settings)
- Collection launch teasers and reveals
- "Design story" behind each Vessel — inspiration, meaning, process
- Vessels styled in beautiful home interiors
- Side-by-side: Vessel as art piece vs. Vessel as memorial — same object, dual purpose

**Frequency:** 3–4x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 2: ⚙️ The Process
**What:** Behind-the-scenes of 3D printing, material science, design iteration, and craftsmanship  
**Why:** Process content is some of the most engaging content on social media. "How it's made" satisfies curiosity and builds perceived value.  
**Content examples:**
- 3D printer timelapses (the #1 most viral content type for this brand)
- Material exploration videos (what we print with and why)
- Design sketches → 3D model → printed product progression
- Failed prints and iterations (humanizing, relatable)
- "From file to finished" full process Reels

**Frequency:** 2–3x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 3: 💀 Philosophy of Dust
**What:** Thought-provoking content about mortality, legacy, what we leave behind, and reframing death  
**Why:** Differentiates From Dust Co. from every other urn company. Creates a brand world that's about IDEAS, not just products. Drives shares and saves.  
**Content examples:**
- Philosophical quote graphics on near-black backgrounds with brass typography
- "From Dust" essay-style carousel posts exploring mortality
- Historical/cultural death practices around the world
- Interviews with philosophers, death doulas, grief therapists
- "What would you want your Vessel to say about you?" engagement prompts

**Frequency:** 1–2x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 4: 🎨 Design Culture
**What:** Broader design world content — architecture, contemporary art, design trends, material innovation, collaborations  
**Why:** Embeds From Dust Co. in the design conversation. Attracts followers who care about design (the target audience) even before they need a Vessel.  
**Content examples:**
- "Design we admire" curated shares of architecture, sculpture, ceramics
- Artist/designer collaboration spotlights
- Design trend analysis (how memorial culture is evolving)
- Gallery and museum content (when Vessels are exhibited)
- "Objects that matter" series — celebrating design objects with emotional significance

**Frequency:** 1–2x per week across platforms

#### Pillar 5: 🖤 Human Stories
**What:** Customer stories, why people choose a particular Vessel, the emotional connection between object and memory  
**Why:** Social proof with emotional depth. Shows that real people choose these products and find meaning in them.  
**Content examples:**
- Customer spotlight videos (with permission): "Why I chose this Vessel for my mother"
- Unboxing reactions
- "Where they placed it" — photos of Vessels in customers' homes
- Letters to loved ones (community-submitted, anonymous if preferred)
- "One year later" — how the Vessel became part of daily life

**Frequency:** 1x per week across platforms

## 18. Crisis & Sensitivity Guidelines

### Content Sensitivity Rules

#### Always:
- ✅ Get **written permission** before sharing any family's story, photos, or testimonials
- ✅ Use **trigger warnings** when discussing graphic aspects of death/cremation
- ✅ Offer **resources** (hotlines, counselors) when posting about grief
- ✅ Be **culturally sensitive** — research thoroughly before posting about other cultures' death practices
- ✅ Allow **comments to stay** even when critical (unless abusive) — censoring grief is never okay
- ✅ Respond to negative comments with **empathy**, not defensiveness
- ✅ Review all content through a **sensitivity lens** before posting
- ✅ Use **inclusive language** — avoid assumptions about family structure, religion, gender, or cultural background

#### Never:
- ❌ Use **real names or images** without explicit, written consent
- ❌ Post **exploitative** or sensationalized grief content for engagement
- ❌ Use **humor** about death in Graceful Tributes content (From Dust Co. can be philosophical/edgy but never flippant)
- ❌ **Newsjack** celebrity deaths to promote services
- ❌ Post **promotional content** on nationally recognized days of mourning without extreme care
- ❌ Use **guilt** or **fear** as marketing tactics ("Don't leave your family with this burden" is fear-based — avoid)
- ❌ Share **pricing** comparisons that shame other providers
- ❌ Post anything that could be interpreted as **disrespectful** to any culture, religion, or tradition

### Crisis Response Protocol

#### If a post receives backlash:
1. **Pause** — Don't respond immediately. Assess the nature of the complaint.
2. **Evaluate** — Is it valid? Did we make a mistake? Or is it a misunderstanding?
3. **Respond** (within 2 hours) — Acknowledge the concern publicly. Be human.
4. **Take offline** if needed — "We'd love to discuss this further. Please DM us."
5. **Learn** — Document what happened and update guidelines.
6. **Remove** the post if it was genuinely harmful (don't just hide it).

#### If a follower shares a crisis (suicidal ideation, acute grief):
1. **Respond immediately** with care: "We hear you. You're not alone."
2. **Share resources:** 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call/text 988), Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741)
3. **Do NOT** try to be a therapist. Direct to professionals.
4. **Follow up** via DM if they seem open to it.
