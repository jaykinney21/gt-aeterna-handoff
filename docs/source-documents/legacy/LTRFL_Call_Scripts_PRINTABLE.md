# 🔥 CREMATORY CALLS

**Your Opening:**

> "Hi, this is Priscilla Kinney. I spent 35 years at Inglewood Park Cemetery, and I'm launching a cremation coordination service called Laid to Rest for Less.
>
> I help families arrange affordable direct cremations, and I'm looking for crematory partners who can offer wholesale rates for consistent volume.
>
> Who would I speak with about a partnership arrangement?"

**When you reach the right person:**

> "Great! Here's what I'm proposing: I handle all family coordination, permits, transportation arrangements, and paperwork. You provide the cremation services at a partner rate.
>
> I'm projecting 10-20 cremations per month initially. What kind of partnership pricing could you offer?"

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] What's your direct cremation rate?
- [ ] Do you offer wholesale/partner pricing?
- [ ] What's included (container, filing, etc.)?
- [ ] Turnaround time?
- [ ] Do you handle transport or do I arrange that?

# 🏛️ VENUE CALLS

**Your Opening:**

> "Hi, I'm Priscilla Kinney with LTRFL. We help families plan celebration of life events - beautiful gatherings to honor loved ones.
>
> I'm building a network of preferred venues, and [VENUE NAME] looks perfect for the kind of events we coordinate.
>
> Who handles private event bookings?"

**When you reach the right person:**

> "We're looking for venue partners who can offer our clients a great experience. Our events are typically 2-4 hours, 50-150 guests, with catering.
>
> Would you be interested in being a preferred venue partner? We'd feature you on our website and refer families directly."

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Private room capacity?
- [ ] Rental rate for 2-4 hours?
- [ ] Is catering required or can we bring our own?
- [ ] AV equipment available?
- [ ] Any referral or partner discount?

---

# 🍽️ CATERER CALLS

**Your Opening:**

> "Hi, I'm Priscilla Kinney. I help families plan celebration of life events, including the repast - the gathering after a memorial.
>
> I'm building a list of trusted caterers I can recommend. Does [BUSINESS] cater memorial events?"

**When they say yes:**

> "Wonderful! I'd love to add you to our preferred caterer list. We'd refer families directly to you.
>
> What's your typical per-person pricing for groups of 50-100? And do you offer any referral arrangement?"

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Per-person pricing (50-100 guests)?
- [ ] Minimum order?
- [ ] Setup and cleanup included?
- [ ] Delivery or on-site service?
- [ ] Referral fee or partner discount?

**Questions to ask:**

- [ ] Rate for 1-2 hour service?
- [ ] Travel fees?

- [ ] How far in advance to book?

# 🚫 HANDLING OBJECTIONS

**"We don't do wholesale rates"**
> "I understand. Even without a special rate, I'd love to refer families to you if you're a good fit. Can I get your standard pricing so I know what to tell families?"

**"Send me an email"**
> "Absolutely! What's the best email? And what's your name so I can address it properly?"

**"We're not interested"**
> "No problem at all. Thank you for your time. If anything changes, I'm at [your number]."

**"What's your company again?"**
> "LTRFL - Laid to Rest for Less. We help families with affordable cremation and celebration of life services. I spent 35 years at Inglewood Park Cemetery before starting this."
