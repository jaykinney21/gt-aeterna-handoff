## 1. NATURE OF PARTNERSHIP

This Agreement is between **Graceful Tributes** (a cremation coordination and referral service) and the undersigned **Vendor** (a licensed funeral establishment/crematory).

**Vendor** remains **solely responsible** for all licensed funeral and cremation services, including but not limited to:
- Filing death certificates with appropriate authorities
- Obtaining cremation permits and authorizations
- Processing all legal documentation
- Body transportation and handling
- Cremation services
- Returning cremated remains to families
- All services requiring a funeral director's license

**Graceful Tributes** acts **only as a coordination and referral service**, providing:
- Family intake and information gathering
- Logistics coordination between families and Vendor
- Customer service and family communication
- Memorial tribute planning and coordination
- Referrals of qualified families to Vendor

**Graceful Tributes does NOT:**
- Provide any licensed funeral or cremation services
- File death certificates, permits, or legal documents
- Handle or transport deceased persons
- Operate as a funeral home or funeral establishment

**Graceful Tributes agrees to:**

1. **Gather Family Information** via intake form:

   - Next of kin contact information
   - Physician information (for death certificate)

2. **Coordinate Logistics**:
   - Send complete intake information to Vendor
   - Coordinate timing and scheduling with family
