# CORRECTED CREMATION COORDINATION CALL SCRIPTS
## LEGALLY COMPLIANT VERSION - Coordination Service Only

---

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT LEGAL DISTINCTION**

**What You ARE:**
- A cremation **coordination service**
- A referral service connecting families with licensed crematories
- An administrative coordinator who gathers information

**What You ARE NOT:**
- A licensed funeral director
- A funeral home
- Someone who files permits, death certificates, or cremation authorizations

**This language matters for regulatory compliance!**

---

# 🔥 CREMATORY CALLS (CORRECTED)

**Your Opening:**

> "Hi, this is Priscilla Kinney. I spent 35 years at Inglewood Park Cemetery, and I'm launching an online cremation **coordination service** called Graceful Tributes.
>
> We help families navigate the cremation process by **coordinating** with licensed crematories like yours. I handle the family intake, **coordinate transportation arrangements**, and help families with tribute planning — while your licensed staff handles all the actual cremation services, permits, death certificates, and legal paperwork.
>
> I'm looking for crematory partners who can offer partnership rates for consistent referrals. Who would I speak with about a partnership arrangement?"

**When you reach the right person:**

> "Here's how it works: I serve as the family's **coordinator**. I'll:
> - Gather all family information via intake form
> - **Coordinate** transportation logistics with your staff
> - Help families choose memorial options
> - Send you complete intake information so your licensed staff can file all the necessary paperwork
>
> Your licensed funeral directors handle everything that requires a license:
> - Filing death certificates
> - Obtaining permits
> - Cremation authorization
> - Body transport and cremation
> - Returning remains to family
>
> I'm projecting 10-20 referrals per month initially. What kind of **partnership pricing** could you offer for coordinated referrals?"

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] What's your direct cremation rate?
- [ ] Do you offer partnership/referral pricing for coordination services?
- [ ] What information do you need from me to process a family (intake form requirements)?
- [ ] How do you prefer to receive referrals? (Phone, email, online form)
- [ ] Do you handle transportation, or do I coordinate with a separate transport service?
- [ ] Turnaround time from intake to cremation?
- [ ] How are remains returned to families?
- [ ] Do you provide a basic container, or do families choose their own?

## 📋 **YOUR INTAKE PROCESS (What You Actually Do)**

When a family contacts you:

1. **Gather Information** (Your intake form collects):
   - Deceased person's full name, date of birth, social security number
   - Date, time, place of death
   - Next of kin information
   - Physician information (for death certificate)
   - Family preferences (type of container, timeline, special requests)

2. **Coordinate with Crematory** (You send them):
   - Completed intake form with all family information
   - Family contact details
   - Any special instructions or timeline requests

3. **Crematory Handles** (Licensed staff does):
   - File death certificate with county
   - Obtain cremation permit
   - Process cremation authorization (legal document signed by next of kin)
   - Coordinate body pickup/transport
   - Perform cremation
   - Return remains to family

4. **You Coordinate** (Your role):
   - Family communication and support
   - Logistics coordination (scheduling, timing)
   - Memorial vessel selection and delivery
   - Tribute event planning (if applicable)
   - Follow-up with family

**YOU = COORDINATOR**  
**CREMATORY = LICENSED SERVICE PROVIDER**

## 🚨 **KEY COMPLIANCE PHRASES TO USE**

**Say this:**
✅ "I coordinate with licensed crematories"
✅ "I gather family information and send it to your licensed staff"
✅ "Your funeral directors handle all the legal paperwork"
✅ "I act as the family's coordinator and point of contact"
✅ "I help families navigate the process while you handle the licensed services"

**DON'T say this:**
❌ "I handle all permits and paperwork"
❌ "I arrange transportation" (say "coordinate transportation" instead)
❌ "I file death certificates"
❌ "I provide cremation services"

## 📞 **REVISED "HANDLING OBJECTIONS" RESPONSES**

**"Are you a funeral home?"**
> "No, we're a coordination service. We partner with licensed crematories who handle all the actual cremation and legal paperwork. We're here to make the process easier for families by coordinating everything."

**"Are you licensed?"**
> "We don't need to be - we're a coordination and referral service. All licensed services (cremation, permits, death certificates) are handled by our partner crematories who are fully licensed funeral establishments. We coordinate, they execute."

**"Who files the death certificate?"**
> "Your licensed funeral director does! We gather all the family information they need, and your staff files all the legal paperwork. We're the coordinators, you're the licensed professionals."

**"This sounds like we're just doing the work..."**
> "You're doing what you do best - the licensed cremation services. We're bringing you consistent referrals and handling the family communication, so you can focus on the service. We make your job easier by having families fully informed and prepared before they contact you."

## 🎯 **WHY CREMATORIES WILL WANT TO PARTNER WITH YOU**

**Your value proposition:**

1. **Pre-qualified families** - They already know costs, have made decisions, ready to move forward
2. **Complete intake** - All information gathered, less back-and-forth
3. **Consistent referrals** - You're bringing them volume
4. **Better family experience** - You handle the emotional/coordination side, they handle the technical side
5. **Less marketing needed** - You're doing their marketing for them

**This is a win-win!**
