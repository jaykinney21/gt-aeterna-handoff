# AETERNA Themed Urn Catalog — Product Angles

**Purpose:** First structured product reference for AETERNA. Fills the "Product DNA — GAP" warning in `aeterna-content-system/SKILL.md` for the 8 themed urns in the initial launch catalog. Use this as the source of truth for Product-type posts featuring these SKUs.

**Source:** Jay dropped 10 product spec sheets on 2026-06-04 for the launch plan. 8 were fully visible. 2 are TBD — see "Unconfirmed products" at the bottom.

**Pattern per product:** Each entry is a *trojan horse* — the cultural angle names the person being remembered, the lead line is the post hook, the visual close lands on the object. **Never lead with the product. Lead with the kind of person who'd pick it.**

---

## 1. Converse Sneaker Urn
- **Spec:** Yellow/black/blue high-top sneaker design. 10.9" tall, 6.4" base, 200+ cu in. Gold pedestal base, dome lid, silicone gasket seal.
- **Cultural angle:** The young person who never owned anything but Chucks and a denim jacket.
- **Lead line:** *"He grew up in a pair of Chucks. We put his other pair on the mantel."*
- **Audience:** Gen X / Millennial parents losing a child or young adult. Sneakerhead community.
- **Why it works:** Most scroll-stopping design. The first product a non-memorial customer will save.

## 2. Basketball / Tire-Tread Urn
- **Spec:** Black body with red rubber sole, basketball-pattern lid. 10.3" tall.
- **Cultural angle:** The Sunday ritual. The pickup game. The gym that was church.
- **Lead line:** *"He never missed a Sunday. We made sure the trophy was right where the schedule was."*
- **Audience:** Sports community, ages 40-65 remembering a spouse/parent. AAU and church-league families.

## 3. "I'm Outta Here" Graffiti Urn
- **Spec:** Bone/concrete with hand-painted street art, "I'm Outta Here" graffiti on the front. 10.8" tall.
- **Cultural angle:** The kid who couldn't sit still, even in stillness.
- **Lead line:** *"The kid who couldn't sit still, even in stillness."* (Use the same line twice — it's the anchor.)
- **Audience:** Urban grief, youth memorial, street-art-adjacent.
- **⚠️ Risk:** The "I'm Outta Here" text reads as funny, not sad. **Beta-test the copy with 2-3 grieving-family readers before launch.** Test via IG Story poll or DM round.

## 4. "Endless Waters" Egyptian Urn
- **Spec:** Hieroglyphic-style urn with Egyptian woman relief on front, winged scarab on back, ankh-pattern blue base. "Endless Waters" plaque. 10" urn + 4.6" base, total 14.6" tall.
- **Cultural angle:** Heritage grief. The matriarch whose children scattered her ashes next to her grandmother's in Accra.
- **Lead line:** *"For the keepers of culture. Endless Waters."*
- **Audience:** Black grief, immigrant grief, heritage-rooted loss. Highest cultural specificity in the catalog.

## 5. "The Embrace" Companion Urn
- **Spec:** Hand-carved wood couple embracing, two red hearts in the cradle, "The Embrace" + "Together Forever / Two Hearts, One Love" plaques. 10" base. **400+ cu in — designed for two sets of remains.**
- **Cultural angle:** The two who went together. The "we had to plan two" reality.
- **Lead line:** *"For the ones who didn't get to go together."*
- **Audience:** Spouses, life partners lost close together. Highest emotional gut-punch in the catalog.

## 6. "Eternal Bond" Mother/Son Heart Urn
- **Spec:** Black heart with carved relief scene ("I'll Always Love My Mama" with two figures embracing), "she's my favorite girl" on back, "Eternal Bond" + "Love Never Fades" plaques. 7.5" wide, 4.3" base.
- **Cultural angle:** The mother-loss piece. The most universal grief line we own.
- **Lead line:** *"I'll always love my mama."* (then the back: *she's my favorite girl*)
- **Audience:** The 35-65 child losing a parent. The single most universal product in the catalog.
- **Why it leads launch:** Lowest creative risk, widest audience. Sets the emotional register for the rest of the catalog.

## 7. Paramedic Figurine Urn
- **Spec:** Male paramedic sitting on a stretcher, red medical bag beside him, "Paramedic" on back of uniform, "Thank You For Your Service" plaque. 10" figure + 4.3" base.
- **Cultural angle:** First-responder grief. The community that gives everything and gets generic memorials.
- **Lead line:** *"He spent his whole life running toward people. We made sure the last place he sat was his."*
- **Audience:** First-responder families, fire/EMS community, badge-of-honor memorial tradition.
- **Bonus:** First-responder grief communities are HIGHLY networked on FB — strong organic amplification potential.

## 8. "Jazz & Soul Eternal" Saxophone Player Urn
- **Spec:** Bronze saxophone player figurine (fedora, suit, sax) on dark wood base, "Jazz & Soul Eternal" + customizable "For Name / Sunrise • Sunset" plaques. 9.5" figure + 3" base, 12.5" total.
- **Cultural angle:** Musician grief. The note they never got to play.
- **Lead line:** *"The note he never got to play."*
- **Audience:** Musician community, jazz/blues/soul listeners, creative-life loss.

## Posting rules for this catalog (brand-safety, campaign-level)

These are *campaign choices* (what to do, what not to do) and are distinct from the *voice rules* in the main SKILL.md (how things sound). Both apply.

- **Never lead with price.** LTRFL's old lane. Off-brand. The rebrand exists to leave "For Less" behind.
- **No "RIP" / "in loving memory" / "thoughts and prayers" / "at this difficult time" copy.** Funeral-home-generic filler.
- **No celebrity-death tie-ins.** If a public figure dies, AETERNA goes silent that week. Not negotiable.
- **No engagement bait.** No "tag someone who's lost a parent." Grief is not a hashtag game.
- **Mother's Day / Father's Day = careful.** Lead with connection, not product.
- **Graffiti urn (#3) needs beta-test copy validation before launch.**
- **Testimonial copy needs explicit family consent** and a documented sign-off path through gbrain-aterna.
- **Holiday weeks = no product-led posts.** Connection-only, or silence.
