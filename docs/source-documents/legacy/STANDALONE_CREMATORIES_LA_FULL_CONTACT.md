## ✅ **WHAT MAKES THESE DIFFERENT FROM CEMETERIES:**

## 💡 **PARTNERSHIP PITCH FOR STANDALONE CREMATORIES**

**Why they should partner with you:**

1. **You're not a competitor** - You don't offer burial, cemetery, or full funeral services
2. **You bring them volume** - 10-20 referrals/month
3. **Pre-qualified families** - Already informed, ready to proceed
4. **Complete intake** - All information gathered, ready to file
5. **You handle customer service** - They focus on the cremation, you handle family communication
6. **Win-win model** - They get referrals, you get partnership pricing

**Your value to them:**
"I'm bringing you qualified families with complete intake forms. You get consistent referrals without marketing costs, and I handle the family coordination so your staff can focus on what they do best — the licensed cremation services."
