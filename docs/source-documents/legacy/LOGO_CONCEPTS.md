# Logo Concepts for From Dust Collective & Graceful Tributes

## FROM DUST COLLECTIVE
**Brand Vibe:** Artistic, Elegant, Modern Memorial Art

### Concept A: "Dust to Art"
- **Imagery:** Abstract dust particles swirling and forming into an elegant urn shape
- **Style:** Watercolor/artistic with gold gradient accents
- **Typography:** Elegant serif font (like Cormorant or Playfair Display)
- **Colors:** Gold (#D4AF37), Sage Green (#9CAF88), Cream (#F5F1EB)
- **Tagline Option:** "Memorial Art Reimagined"

### Concept B: "Sacred Geometry"
- **Imagery:** Geometric line art forming a vessel/urn with sacred geometry elements (circles, triangles)
- **Style:** Minimalist line art with gold foil effect
- **Typography:** Modern serif (like Cinzel or Bodoni)
- **Colors:** Gold gradient, Black, White
- **Tagline Option:** "Where Art Meets Memory"

### Concept C: "Botanical Vessel"
- **Imagery:** Elegant urn silhouette with delicate botanical elements (leaves, stems) growing from it
- **Style:** Sophisticated illustration, hand-drawn feel
- **Typography:** Elegant sans-serif (like Futura or Avenir)
- **Colors:** Gold, Sage Green, Warm Gray
- **Tagline Option:** "From Dust We Come"

### Concept D: "Minimalist Dust"
- **Imagery:** Simple urn icon with flowing dust/particle trail transforming into abstract art shapes
- **Style:** Ultra-minimalist, modern
- **Typography:** Clean geometric sans-serif (like Montserrat or Poppins)
- **Colors:** Gold, Black, White
- **Tagline Option:** "Celebrate Life Through Art"

## GRACEFUL TRIBUTES
**Brand Vibe:** Modern, Elegant, Compassionate, Professional

### Concept A: "Embracing Hands"
- **Imagery:** Two elegant hands gently forming a vessel/protective embrace
- **Style:** Modern line art illustration
- **Typography:** Clean, sophisticated sans-serif (like Lato or Raleway)
- **Colors:** Sage Green, Gold accent, Soft Gray
- **Tagline Option:** "Honor the Vessel. Celebrate the Spirit."

### Concept B: "Flowing Grace"
- **Imagery:** Abstract flowing lines suggesting movement, grace, and continuity (like a gentle wave or ribbon)
- **Style:** Modern, organic curves with gold gradient
- **Typography:** Elegant sans-serif with custom ligatures
- **Colors:** Gold gradient, Sage Green, Cream
- **Tagline Option:** "Cremation & Celebration Services"

### Concept C: "Memorial Arch"
- **Imagery:** Stylized arch or doorway representing passage/transition, with subtle floral accent
- **Style:** Modern architectural with warmth
- **Typography:** Modern serif (like Cormorant or Libre Baskerville)
- **Colors:** Warm Brass, Sage Green, Soft Charcoal
- **Tagline Option:** "Affordable. Dignified. Meaningful."

### Concept D: "Heart & Vessel"
- **Imagery:** Simple heart shape integrated with vessel/urn silhouette (abstract, not literal)
- **Style:** Clean, modern, compassionate
- **Typography:** Professional sans-serif (like Source Sans Pro)
- **Colors:** Sage Green primary, Gold accent, White
- **Tagline Option:** "Family-First Cremation Services"
