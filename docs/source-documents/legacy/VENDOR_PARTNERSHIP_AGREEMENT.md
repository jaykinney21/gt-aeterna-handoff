## 5. COORDINATION & COMMUNICATION

**Graceful Tributes agrees to:**
- Provide families with Vendor's contact information and service details
- Coordinate scheduling and logistics between family and Vendor
- Communicate family preferences, special requests, and any necessary details
- Act as primary point of contact for the family throughout the process

**Vendor agrees to:**
- Respond to Graceful Tributes' inquiries within **_____ hours** (business days)
- Provide professional, compassionate service to all referred families
- Honor the agreed-upon pricing for all Graceful Tributes referrals
- Communicate any issues, delays, or concerns promptly to Graceful Tributes

## 6. QUALITY STANDARDS

**Vendor agrees to:**
- Maintain all necessary licenses, permits, and insurance required by law
- Provide services in a professional, timely, and compassionate manner
- Comply with all applicable health, safety, and industry regulations
- Treat all families with respect, dignity, and cultural sensitivity
- Address any complaints or concerns promptly and professionally

**Graceful Tributes agrees to:**
- Refer only qualified, pre-screened families to Vendor
- Provide accurate information about family needs and preferences
- Handle any disputes or issues collaboratively with Vendor
