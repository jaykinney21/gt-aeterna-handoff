# NAME OPTIONS — Rebranding from "Laid to Rest for Less"

**Prepared for:** Priscilla Kinney
**Prepared by:** Susan, Business Consultant — Kinney Family Advisory
**Date:** February 2026

---

## Why Rename?

"Laid to Rest for Less" positions the business as a **budget/discount** service. While affordability is a genuine benefit, Priscilla's vision is much larger:

- She serves **ALL families**, not just budget-conscious ones
- The philosophy ("we're just vessels passing through this place") is spiritual, meaningful, and beautiful
- The 3D-printed "Vessel" concept elevates the brand to artisan/premium territory
- Insurance assignment means families don't always pay out-of-pocket — price is not the lead message
- "For Less" invites comparison with cheap competitors rather than positioning as a category creator

The new name should:
✅ Appeal to all income levels and demographics
✅ Evoke warmth, celebration, spirit, and dignity
✅ Work with the "Vessel" concept and philosophy
✅ Be modern, memorable, and easy to say/spell
✅ Have strong domain name and social media availability
✅ Not sound "funeral-y," morbid, or clinical
✅ Stand out from the sea of "Memorial," "Eternal," "Forever" funeral brands

## 15 NAME SUGGESTIONS

### Tier 1: Top Recommendations ⭐

#### 1. **Vessel & Vine**
- **Tagline:** *Honor the Spirit. Celebrate the Journey.*
- **Why it works:** "Vessel" is your core concept (the body as a vessel, the urn as a Vessel). "Vine" evokes growth, connection, family roots, and the continuity of life. It's warm, organic, and memorable.
- **Domain to check:** vesselandvine.com, vesselvine.com
- **Social handles:** @vesselandvine
- **Vibe:** Modern, nature-inspired, sophisticated

#### 2. **Passage Cremation & Tribute Co.**
- **Tagline:** *From Dust We Come. In Love We Remember.*
- **Why it works:** "Passage" captures the philosophy perfectly — we're just passing through. It's dignified, non-denominational, and evokes journey and transition rather than ending.
- **Domain to check:** passagetribute.com, passagecremation.com
- **Social handles:** @passagetribute
- **Vibe:** Elegant, philosophical, universal

#### 3. **The Vessel Collective**
- **Tagline:** *Beautiful Vessels for Beautiful Lives.*
- **Why it works:** Puts "Vessel" front and center. "Collective" sounds artistic, community-driven, and modern. Positions the business as creative/artisan rather than funeral industry.
- **Domain to check:** thevesselcollective.com, vesselcollective.com
- **Social handles:** @thevesselcollective
- **Vibe:** Artisan, creative, community

#### 4. **Homeward Cremation & Celebration**
- **Tagline:** *Your Spirit's Journey Home.*
- **Why it works:** "Home" resonates across all cultures and faiths — the spirit going "home." It's warm, comforting, and non-clinical. Works for cremation (ashes returned home) and tribute services (homegoing traditions in Black culture).
- **Domain to check:** homewardcremation.com, homewardtributes.com
- **Social handles:** @homewardcremation
- **Vibe:** Warm, spiritual, culturally resonant

## Top 3 Recommendation

### 🥇 **Vessel & Vine** (Score: 29/30)
Best overall. Captures the Vessel concept, feels warm and organic, works as a lifestyle brand, and immediately differentiates from every other cremation company. Beautiful name for a beautiful vision.

### 🥈 **The Vessel Collective** / **Dust & Spirit Co.** / **Terra & Light** (Tie: 26/30)
The Vessel Collective is strongest for the artisan/creative angle. Dust & Spirit is boldest and most philosophical. Terra & Light has the best multicultural appeal.

### 🥉 **Passage Cremation & Tribute** / **Embers & Light** / **Soulprint** (Tie: 25/30)
All excellent choices. Passage is most professional/investor-ready. Embers & Light is most poetic. Soulprint is most innovative and ties perfectly to 3D printing.

## Sub-Brand Strategy

Consider using a **two-tier naming structure:**

**Primary brand:** Vessel & Vine *(or chosen name)*
**Sub-brands by service tier:**
- **Vessel & Vine Tributes** — Full service celebrations of life
- **Vessel & Vine Express** — Direct cremation (fast, simple, dignified)
- **The Vessel Collection** — Retail 3D-printed urns/art pieces
- **Vessel & Vine Pre-Plan** — Pre-need monthly enrollment

This keeps "Laid to Rest Express" energy for the budget tier while the parent brand speaks to everyone.
