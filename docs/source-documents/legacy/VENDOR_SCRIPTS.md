## 🔥 SCRIPT 1: Crematory Partnership

### For Priscilla (Warm, Personal)

> "Hi, this is Priscilla Kinney. I'm reaching out because I'm launching a new cremation coordination service called **Laid to Rest for Less** here in Los Angeles.
>
> I spent over 35 years at Inglewood Park Cemetery, and I've seen firsthand how families struggle with the cost of end-of-life services. My company helps families coordinate **affordable direct cremations** - and I'm looking for crematory partners who share our commitment to dignity at a fair price.
>
> I'd love to discuss a **partnership arrangement** - we'd bring you consistent volume, handle all the family coordination, permits, and paperwork, and you'd provide the cremation services.
>
> Who would be the right person to discuss a wholesale or partnership rate with?"

**If they ask about volume:**
> "We're projecting 10-20 cremations per month initially, with growth potential as we establish our referral network."

**If they ask about pricing:**
> "We're looking for a partner rate that allows us to offer families a complete package under $2,500 while ensuring everyone is fairly compensated."

## 💡 TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### For Priscilla
1. **Lead with your experience** - 35 years at IPC is powerful credibility
2. **Emphasize "partnership"** not "vendor" - people want relationships
3. **Be specific about what you offer them** - referrals, consistent volume, less hassle
4. **Follow up within 48 hours** with an email summary

### For Virtual Assistant
1. **Be professional but warm** - these are relationship businesses
2. **Gather info first** - don't sell, just learn and qualify
3. **Get the decision-maker's name and email** - critical for follow-up
4. **Note everything** - even small details help Priscilla personalize follow-ups
