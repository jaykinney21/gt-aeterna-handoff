## 💡 **Pricing Strategy Tips**

### **1. Start Higher Than You Think**
- You can always offer discounts
- Can't raise prices on existing customers easily
- Your experience is VALUABLE

### **2. Bundle Intelligently**
- People buy "packages" more than à la carte
- Bundle your time into the package price
- Makes the value clearer

### **3. Offer Payment Plans**
- $4,995 sounds big
- "$416/month for 12 months" sounds manageable
- No-interest plans are your competitive advantage

### **4. Upsell Gently**
- "Most families choose the Signature Package because..."
- "For just $X more, you also get..."
- Never push, but guide toward better value

### **5. Know Your Bottom Line**
- **Absolute minimum per cremation:** $2,195
  - Your cost: $1,650
  - Your minimum profit: $545 (after overhead)
- Below this, you're working for free
