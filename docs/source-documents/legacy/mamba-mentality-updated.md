# 6. THE SPORTS LEGEND COLLECTION

## 6.1: "The Legacy" — Basketball Tribute Vessel (UPDATED)
*Previously: "The Mamba Mentality" — revised to remove licensed/trademarked references*

### Design Intent
A sculptural tribute to basketball greatness, featuring a symbolic snake protecting a regulation-style basketball. Designed as a customizable tribute piece — customers can personalize colors and inscription text to honor their loved one's basketball passion.

### Geometry & Composition
- **Core Object:** A textured sphere resembling a basketball
- **Sculptural Element:** A large, textured snake coils around the basketball. The snake's body wraps around the base and sides, with the head resting aggressively near the top of the ball.

### Color & Material Palette
- **Basketball:** Standard orange with black seam lines and pebbled leather texture *(DEFAULT — customizable to team colors upon request)*
- **Snake:** Matte Black finish with highly detailed, diamond-shaped scale texture. The eyes should be painted red or have small ruby-colored gems inserted.

### Base Specification
- **Style:** A circular, tiered plinth base (2-3 tiers) with a dark bronze or antique brass finish
- **Inscription:** Gold lettering on the front face:
  - Line 1: FOREVER A LEGEND
  - Line 2: *(Customizable — customer provides name/message)*
- **Default display text for marketing:** "FOREVER A LEGEND / REST IN PEACE"

### Access & Assembly
- **Loading:** Bottom-loading via a circular wooden or metal panel
- **Securement:** The panel is secured with four Phillips-head screws

### Customization Options (Upsell)
- **Basketball colors:** Can be changed to any team color combination (+$25)
- **Snake color:** Black (default), or custom finish (+$35)
- **Inscription text:** Two lines, customer-provided (included in base price)
- **Eye gems:** Standard painted red (default) or genuine gemstone insert (+$50)
