Having the home-court advantage completely transforms the approach from a cold B2B pitch into a warm homecoming. You already know the exact layout, the cultural demographics, and the key decision-makers walking the grounds.

The Prototype Drop: Skip the central procurement office entirely. Walk a flawless, weighted 10-inch prototype—such as the "Safe in His Arms" or "Mom's Tough Love" variant—directly to the family service counselors you already have a relationship with. The emotional weight and ceramic-like finish will sell the concept instantly.

Targeting Known Pain Points: You are intimately familiar with the exact frustrations counselors face when trying to fit generic, oversized brass urns into Inglewood's specific glass-front or marble niches. Frame your 10-inch variants as the definitive, custom-engineered solution to the spatial problem you all used to navigate together.

The Heritage Digital Pitch: Inglewood Park is built on deep generational roots and local legacy. Position the micro-QR digital suite not just as a tech feature, but as a preservation tool for family history, transforming a static niche into an interactive archive for the community.

The Zero-Friction Wholesale Offer: Hand your former colleagues a straightforward pricing sheet showing a high-margin wholesale rate with absolutely zero inventory risk or minimum order requirements. They get to offer a premium, problem-solving product to grieving families while capturing revenue without touching warehouse space.
