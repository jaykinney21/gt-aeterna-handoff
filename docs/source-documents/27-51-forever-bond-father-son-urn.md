Technical Design Specification: "Forever Bond" Father & Son Urn
SKU: HC-FAM-FATHERSON
Series: Family & Memorial / Scenic Tribute Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture where the low-profile circular pedestal base operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, supporting a classic, highly illustrative amphora vessel above.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A highly stabilized, circular pedestal design providing a wide footprint to anchor the vessel.
External Base Dimensions: 7.0” Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 2.2” (56 mm) Base Height.
The Decorative Vessel: The upper sculptural assembly is a classic, broad-shouldered vase measuring 10.0” in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the domed lid) reaches 12.2” (310 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access is located underneath the circular pedestal base. The engineered interior cavity within the base yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, square metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a deeply emotional, photorealistic tribute to the enduring connection between a father and son, utilizing a continuous 360-degree panoramic canvas.
The Front Narrative (The Vessel): The primary focal point on the vase features a highly detailed, painted illustration of two male figures (father and son) sitting side-by-side with their arms around each other's shoulders. They are seated on a textured, multi-colored stone wall, looking out over a sprawling city skyline and harbor during a vibrant, golden sunset.
The Rear Narrative (The Vessel): The back of the vessel continues the sweeping panoramic scene. The stone wall wraps around the bottom edge, overlooking the tranquil bay, winding coastal roads, and the illuminated city skyline resting beneath the dramatic orange and purple sunset clouds.
The Lid: The domed lid continues the celestial motif, featuring a view of the vibrant sunset sky, gracefully accented with painted yellow flowers resting near the rim.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base is finished to simulate a rich, dark wood grain. The base is structured with layered, tiered edges to provide a formal foundation for the artwork above.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: The primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). As this CAD-modeled design is optimized for your master collection database, it is fully compatible with on-demand additive manufacturing services such as JawsTec or Sculpteo, allowing for rapid, high-fidelity production of the thick-walled storage base and the vessel without minimum order requirements.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece requires a specialized multi-stage finishing process. The vessel requires a premium, high-resolution graphic wrap or meticulous airbrushing to capture the photorealistic sunset gradient and the intricate details of the figures and city. The tiered base utilizes a dark faux-bois (wood-look) finishing process. The complete assembly is sealed under a premium matte or satin clear coat to ensure long-term durability and UV protection.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the brass threaded inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls during manufacturing is critical, ensuring the metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer base.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front curved face of the dark wood-look base is an elegant, gold presentation plaque with classic serif typography reading:
"• FOREVER BOND •"
Personalization Integration: Because the base utilizes a smooth wood-grain finish, personalization for families (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) is easily achieved.
Customization Note: Funeral home distributors can apply custom-cut, high-adhesion gold vinyl lettering directly to the rear curved face of the base, matching the existing front plaque, or a secondary engraved brass plate can be mechanically mounted to the rear.
