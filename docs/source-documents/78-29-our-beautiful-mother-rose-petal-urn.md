1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, diorama-style monumental architecture where the substantial circular pedestal base operates as the primary ash storage chamber, allowing the complex upper floral sculpture to serve as a high-impact, decorative tribute.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A heavy, circular pedestal design providing a wide, heavily stabilized footprint to support the solid upper blossom.
External Base Dimensions: 7.5” Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 3.0” Base Height.
The Decorative Sculpture: The upper sculptural assembly features a fully bloomed rose and resting leaves, measuring 7.0” in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the highest petal tip) reaches exactly 10.0” (254 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access is located underneath the circular pedestal base. The engineered interior cavity yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security. A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, square metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
Hardware Assembly: The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a soft, deeply elegant tribute to a mother's enduring love, utilizing classical floral iconography set upon an ornate, vintage-style foundation.
The Central Canvas (The Rose): The primary focal point is a highly detailed, 3D-sculpted pink rose blossom with overlapping, textured petals. It is cupped at the bottom by a ring of intricately sculpted, deep green leaves.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base features a smooth, ivory or off-white foundation. The side wall of the base is heavily decorated with classical, raised gold scrollwork and filigree, intertwined with additional green leaves that overlap from the top rim. The upper and lower rims of the base are accented with solid gold trim.
The Central Plaque: Nestled into the front center of the green leaves, resting just above the base, is a prominent, gold heart-shaped presentation plaque.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: The primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). Because this CAD-modeled design is optimized for your master collection, it is fully compatible with on-demand production services such as JawsTec or Sculpteo, allowing for rapid, high-fidelity manufacturing without minimum order requirements.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The rose petals require meticulous, layered hand-painting to capture the subtle gradient washes of soft pink and white. The base utilizes an antiqued ivory finish with gold metallic enameling on the scrollwork. The complete assembly is sealed under a premium, high-gloss clear coat to simulate fine porcelain.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the brass threaded inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls during manufacturing guarantees that the metal access plate can be tightened securely by a funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: The gold heart-shaped plaque on the front of the design serves as the primary personalization zone.
Standard Typography: The default configuration features elegant, black italicized serif typography reading:
"Our Beautiful Mother"
Customization Note: For specific families, this heart plaque can be provided as a blank gold brass piece for custom mechanical engraving (Name, Sunrise/Sunset dates), or the digital CAD file can be updated to integrate custom raised typography prior to the final 3D printing phase.
