1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, two-part architectural approach where the substantial pedestal base operates as the primary high-capacity ash chamber, allowing the upper element to serve purely as a monumental, decorative sculpture.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, wide circular/oval plinth design providing a stable, reverent footprint.
External Dimensions: 11.5” Diameter x 4.0” Height.
Internal Dimensions: 10.25” L x 10.25” W x 2.75” H.
The Decorative Finial: Resting securely atop the base is a highly detailed, 7.5” tall sculpted figurine of praying hands, bringing the total structural height to 11.5”.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access located beneath the base. The engineered compartment provides an internal volume of 200+ cubic inches, easily accommodating a standard adult. The access panel is a custom-cut, square brushed metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a deeply comforting, traditional tribute utilizing classic religious statuary motifs.
The Sculpture: A high-relief, fully realized 3D sculpture of hands folded in prayer. The hands rest upon a small, rectangular stepped pediment. The front face of this pediment features intricate, low-relief sculptural detailing of a classic Christian cross flanked by blooming roses.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber features a smooth, curved profile with a stepped upper rim, serving as a dignified foundation for the statuary above. The back of the base is intentionally kept free of plaques or hardware to showcase the clean, unbroken wood finish.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Wood/Bronze Finish: The printed PLA components (both the base and the sculpture) must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process. The schematics specify a rich "wood look finish." This is achieved via advanced hydro-dipping or meticulous hand-painted faux-bois texturing, followed by a dark antiquing wash that settles into the deep recesses of the folded hands and the floral cross detailing to simulate aged, carved hardwood or a dark patina bronze.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the countersunk access panel can be securely opened and closed by families or funeral directors without stripping the plastic threads over time.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Single Plaque System: Personalization is integrated directly onto the curved front face of the cylindrical base, keeping the rear profile completely clean.
Front Branding: Features a brushed-brass presentation plaque with scalloped capsule-style edges, secured with faux-rivet tacks. The standard engraving utilizes classic typography reading: "faith carried you home".
Manufacturing Note: Because this plaque mounts to a curved surface, replacement plaques utilized by funeral homes for custom laser-engraving (to add the individual's name and dates) must be pre-rolled to perfectly match the 11.5" diameter curve of the base to ensure a seamless, flush mount.
