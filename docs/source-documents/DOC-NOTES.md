THE MASTER MEMORIAL CATALOG & OPERATIONAL PLAYBOOK
SECTION 1: BRAND STORY & MANUFACTURING PROCESS
Our Story: A Mission Born From Love
Our journey began not as a business venture, but from a deeply personal place: the need to create a truly special, one-of-a-kind urn to comfort a dear friend following the tragic passing of their son. In that moment of profound grief, standard, mass-produced options felt entirely inadequate. We realized that every individual contains multitudes, and they deserve a final resting place that authentically reflects their unique spirit, their passions, and their truest self.
That single act of love evolved into our core philosophy. We believe that honoring a life means getting the smallest details right. True personalization goes far beyond simply engraving a name on a plaque. It means understanding that for a dedicated left-handed bowler, the exact mechanics of their release and the distinct, specialized soles of their sliding shoes matter. It means capturing the precise fold of a gentleman's pocket square, or the authentic, tactile seams of a well-worn basketball. Because we live and breathe these everyday passions ourselves, we are relentlessly dedicated to translating them perfectly into lasting, tangible tributes.
Our Process: Where Innovation Meets Craftsmanship
We bridge the gap between cutting-edge manufacturing technology and traditional, handcrafted artistry to create heirloom-quality memorials.
* Precision 3D Modeling: Every collection begins in the digital realm. Our designs are digitally sculpted with pinpoint accuracy by expert CAD developers. This allows us to achieve incredibly intricate reliefs and ensure flawless structural integrity across all our niche-compatible variants and companion jewelry.
* High-Resolution 3D Printing: We utilize advanced resin printing technology to build a durable, robust foundation. This on-demand, scalable manufacturing process allows us to capture the finest topographical details—from the smooth curves of a classic fedora to the rich, tactile woodgrain of a bowling lane.
* Artisan Hand-Finishing: While technology builds the foundation, human hands bring the legacy to life. Each vessel is meticulously hand-painted by skilled artisans. We layer high-contrast colors, apply metallic gold and silver accents, and utilize specialized finishing techniques to achieve premium matte, ceramic, or high-gloss aesthetics.
* Secure & Dignified Assembly: Every urn is engineered with dignity in mind, featuring secure, flush-mounted brushed metal access plates, pre-installed brass hardware, and airtight silicone seals to permanently protect your loved one's legacy.
SECTION 2: ENGINEERING STANDARDS & PACKAGING SPECIFICATIONS
Master Technical & Structural Baseline
* Wall Thickness: Standard nominal thickness of 3.0 mm to 4.0 mm across all main vessel bodies; 4.0 mm to 5.0 mm for load-bearing base structures.
* Fillet Radii: Minimum internal radius of 2.0 mm on all internal structural intersections to prevent stress cracks under load.
* Print Tolerances: Global printing accuracy of $\pm$0.15 mm with a +0.2 mm clearance offset for bottom access plates.
* Primary Hardware: Bottom-loading access secured by a custom-cut brushed metal plate (stainless steel or aluminum), 4x countersunk M3 machine screws, and pre-modeled straight-wall boss channels with embedded brass inserts and silicone O-ring channels.
Typography & Font Pairings
* Primary Display Typeface (Hero Text): Cinzel or Trajan Pro (minimum 1.2 mm raised extrusion for paint-holding capability).
* Secondary Typeface (Dates & Personalization): Montserrat (Medium or Semi-Bold weight).
Packaging & Foam Tolerances (10-Inch Variant Baseline)
* Master Outer Carton: 12.0 x 12.0 x 15.0 inches (305 x 305 x 381 mm) standard double-wall corrugated shipping box.
* Primary Foam Insert: High-density EVA foam (minimum 45 kg/m³ density) with a die-cut central cavity maintaining a 2.5-inch protective buffer on all sides.
* Keepsake Boxing: Standard 3.5 x 3.5 x 1.5-inch two-piece presentation boxes with velvet-flocked, die-cut foam inserts.
