2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This model utilizes a robust, dual-component architecture where a secure circular pedestal base functions as the primary structural foundation and lower storage tier, supporting the main amphora-style vessel above.
Internal Volume Strategy: The spacious interior chamber spans the interior of the expanded 7.75-inch pedestal base and the upper vessel, engineered with durable wall construction to comfortably and safely hold a minimum of 200 cubic inches.
Vessel-to-Base Connection: As detailed in the cross-section cutaway, the upper vessel attaches to the base via a secure, threaded interface equipped with a silicone gasket to prevent moisture intrusion.
Access Method: Secure ash insertion is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the circular pedestal base via a custom-cut rectangular metal access plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum).
Hardware & Closure: The metal access plate sits entirely flush within a precisely engineered recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws that thread directly into heavy-duty brass inserts permanently integrated into the structural walls, guaranteeing a lasting, tamper-resistant seal.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a bold, modern tribute to urban youth culture and streetwear, utilizing a faux-stone canvas adorned with vibrant, hand-drawn graffiti elements.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main upper body features a textured, speckled cream/sand background simulating a concrete or plaster wall. The front vividly depicts an illustration of a boy viewed from behind, wearing a backward cap, a tan backpack, and sagging blue jeans revealing red plaid boxers.
The Graffiti Elements: Below the illustration is a prominent, stylized graffiti tag reading "I'm OUTTA HERE" in bold orange with black outlines. The upper right shoulder of the urn features a stark, black spray-paint drip effect. The rear and sides of the vessel are accented with additional orange graffiti tags and stylized, loose-line floral doodles in black, orange, and blue.
The Base Canvas: The wide, circular pedestal base perfectly continues the textured cream background. It is wrapped continuously with the stylized black, orange, and blue graffiti flowers. The base is formally framed at both the top and bottom edges by smooth, contrasting metallic trim rings.
The Lid: The vessel is capped with a stepped, flat-top circular lid that mirrors the textured cream finish. The center is anchored by a black spray-paint drip, surrounded by a radial pattern of the stylized graffiti flowers.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: Engineered for advanced additive manufacturing (such as high-strength 3D printed polymer resins) or heavy-duty cast composites. The design relies on thick structural walls to support the threaded interface, the heavy-duty brass inserts, and the flush-mount bottom closure system.
The Paint & Finish: This piece requires a precision finishing protocol. The primary surfaces require a textured, matte finish to accurately simulate the urban wall canvas. The complex character illustration and graffiti elements require a high-resolution hydro-graphic wrap or specialized permanent UV-decal application. The top and bottom base rims require a smooth, metallic enamel finish.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Narrative (Front): Anchored by the permanent artwork "I'm OUTTA HERE".
Personalization Integration (Rear Vessel): The rear of the main vessel features a distinct, framed rectangular plaque area designated for bespoke personalization, designed to look like a framed stencil or sticker on the wall.
Standard Formatting: Features custom localized names and dates printed in classic serif typography over the cream background (e.g., "Your Loved One / January 1, 1956 / December 31, 2024"), anchored by a small black heart.
Customization Note: For on-demand production, the customized rear text can be natively integrated into the graphic wrap prior to manufacturing, or applied post-production by the distributor using high-adhesion custom-cut vinyl lettering.
