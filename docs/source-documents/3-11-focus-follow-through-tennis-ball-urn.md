1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, monumental architecture featuring an exceptionally wide, dedicated decorative pedestal base that operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber. This heavy, low-center-of-gravity foundation supports a classic amphora-style vessel above.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A massive, highly stabilized circular pedestal design providing an oversized footprint to securely anchor the upper vessel.
External Base Dimensions: 10.5” (267 mm) Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 5.25” (133 mm) Base Height.
The Decorative Vessel: The upper sculptural assembly is a classic, broad-shouldered jar with a removable, low-profile dome lid.
Vessel Dimensions: The vase section alone measures exactly 10.0” (254 mm) in height with a maximum diameter of 7.0" (178 mm).
Lid Dimensions: The lid measures 4.5" (114 mm) in diameter.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the lid) reaches exactly 15.25” (387 mm).
Access & Volumetrics: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the massive base. The engineered interior cavity (measuring approximately 8.25" wide by 5.0" deep internally) yields a standard adult capacity of 200+ cubic inches.
Closure & Security: The underside of the base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
The underside also features four integrated rubber or felt feet positioned outside the access plate to protect delicate display surfaces.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a bold, pop-art inspired tribute to the sport of tennis, utilizing a high-contrast black canvas wrapped in a dynamic, transitioning geometric pattern of tennis balls.
The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The main body features a smooth black background. It is enveloped by a continuous, repeating pattern of overlapping circles. The upper half of the vessel features these circles fully colored as vibrant, optic yellow-green tennis balls. As the pattern descends down the vase, the balls transition into dark negative space, leaving only glowing yellow-gold outlines against the black canvas.
The Lid: The domed lid perfectly continues the visual motif, featuring an outer ring of optic yellow-green tennis balls surrounding a black center, bordered by a prominent metallic gold rim.
The Base Canvas: The oversized circular pedestal base provides a heavy, grounding contrast, finished in a smooth, solid matte black. It is formally structured by distinct metallic gold accent trims running along both the top and bottom exterior edges.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). Fully optimized for your industrial catalog, this CAD-modeled design is perfectly suited for on-demand manufacturing pipelines leveraging Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) or Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) to rapidly produce the exceptionally large, thick-walled 10.5-inch storage base and the intricate vessel without minimum order quantities.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished with a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The main vessel body and base require a premium matte or satin black base coat. The optic yellow-green tennis balls and gold outline patterns can be applied via a high-resolution hydro-graphic wrap or precision masking and enameling. The trims and base plaques demand precise finishing with a brilliant metallic gold.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the threaded brass inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the bottom cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front face of the black pedestal base is a raised, gold-framed rectangular presentation plaque (complete with four corner faux-rivets) featuring classic gold italicized serif typography reading:
"Keep your eye on the ball"
Personalization Integration: The sheer circumference of the 10.5-inch pedestal base provides ample, unblemished black surface area on the rear face, specifically designated as the primary personalization zone.
Customization Note: Funeral home distributors can easily apply custom-cut, high-adhesion gold vinyl lettering (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) to the rear face. Alternatively, a customized, identically shaped black and gold presentation plaque can be mechanically mounted to the rear to mirror the front aesthetic.
