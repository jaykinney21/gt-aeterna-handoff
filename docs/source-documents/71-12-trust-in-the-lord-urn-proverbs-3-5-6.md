Technical Design Specification: "Trust in the Lord" Urn (Proverbs 3:5–6)

1. Dimensional & Volumetric Data

Overall Dimensions: 13.00 in (330.2 mm) Height x 7.75 in (196.9 mm) Overall Diameter.

Urn (Vase) Height: 9.00 in (228.6 mm).

Urn Diameter: 7.75 in (196.9 mm).

Lid Diameter: 5.30 in (134.6 mm).

Base Dimensions (External): 4.00 in (101.6 mm) Height x 7.75 in (196.9 mm) Base Diameter.

Base Storage Compartment (Internal): 3.15 in (80.0 mm) Internal Height x 6.30 in (160.0 mm) Internal Diameter.

Capacity: 200+ Cubic Inches usable capacity (suitable for one set of adult cremated remains).

2. Engineering & Structural Architecture

Access Method: Bottom-loading access through the underside of the circular pedestal base.

Closure Hardware System: Custom-cut rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) designed to sit flush within a recessed lip.

Security Fasteners: Four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-modeled brass inserts.

Internal Storage Integration: The primary 200+ cu. in. compartment is engineered directly inside the lower pedestal structure.

3. Visual & Sculptural Elements

Vessel Aesthetic: Deep emerald green body with a fine crackle-textured ceramic-like glaze, detailed with gold mountain range silhouettes on the front.

Front Typography & Motif: Gold inscription reading "Trust IN THE LORD" centered below the mountain peaks.

Rear Scriptural Motif: Inscribed in gold with Proverbs 3:5–6: "IN ALL YOUR WAYS ACKNOWLEDGE HIM, AND HE WILL MAKE YOUR PATHS STRAIGHT." along with Proverbs 3:5–6 reference attribution and flourish details.

Pedestal Details: Features a centered Latin cross framed by ornate baroque acanthus leaf scrollwork along the front collar, with matching gold trim rings along the base tiers.

Lid Aesthetic: Domed lid matching the deep green crackle finish, crowned by a centered gold-accented finial knob.

4. Material & Finishing Protocols

Core Substrate: High-quality 3D-printed photopolymer resin.

Finish Details: Hand-finished to resemble high-gloss glazed ceramic with antique gold-leaf style typography, reliefs, and trim accents.

5. Typography & Personalization

Base Rear Plaque: Dedicated rectangular inset plaque framed in gold scrollwork for personalized memorial engraving (Name, Date of Birth, and Date of Passing, e.g., "John Doe / Jan. 1, 1950 / Dec. 31, 2030").
