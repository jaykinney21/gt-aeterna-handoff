Technical Design Specification: "Ancestral Legacy" African Heritage Urn
SKU: HC-CUL-HERITAGE
Series: Heritage & Culture / Ancestral Tribute Collection

1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, diorama-style monumental architecture where the substantial circular pedestal base operates as the primary secure ash storage chamber, allowing the highly complex upper sculptural elements to serve as a high-impact, decorative tribute.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A heavy, circular pedestal design providing a wide, heavily stabilized footprint to support the dynamic height and off-center elements of the upper sculpture.
External Base Dimensions: 9.5” Overall Base Diameter.
Base Exterior Height: 3.0” Base Height.
The Decorative Sculpture: The upper sculptural assembly (the figures and the panther) measures an impressive 10.0” in height.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the base to the top of the baby figure) reaches exactly 13.0”.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access is located underneath the circular pedestal base. The engineered interior cavity yields 200+ cubic inches of usable space, fully satisfying the standard capacity requirement for one adult. The empty weight of the unit is approximately 3.6 lbs (1.63 kg).
Closure & Security: The base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.

2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a powerful, deeply emotional tribute to ancestry, legacy, and family, utilizing highly detailed character sculpting and traditional cultural motifs.
The Central Canvas (The Figures): The primary focal point is a meticulously detailed, 3D-sculpted figure of a father holding a baby high above his head. Both figures are dressed in vibrantly colored, highly detailed traditional garments featuring complex geometric Kente cloth patterns in yellow, green, red, and blue.
The Ground Elements: Sitting gracefully on the base beside the father is a sculpted black panther, acting as a majestic guardian figure. The panther features striking red eyes. The diorama floor surrounding the figures is scattered with textured, golden/bronze sculpted leaves.
The Base Canvas: The circular pedestal base features a highly textured, dark brown faux-wood finish. The top and bottom rims of the base are accented with intricate, repeating gold geometric border patterns.
The Base Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front curved face of the pedestal base is elegant gold serif typography reading:
"behold the only thing greater than you"

3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). The design relies on thick, durable resin walls to contain the ashes securely within the base while supporting the substantial 10-inch vertical scale of the figures.
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece requires a multi-stage, highly detailed finishing process. The human figures demand meticulous hand-painting to capture lifelike skin tones and the complex, vivid geometry of the Kente cloth. The storage base utilizes a specialized faux-wood finishing process with masked metallic gold borders. The entire assembly is sealed under a premium matte or satin clear coat for long-term protection.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the brass threaded inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the base cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the polymer material.

4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: The prominent, integrated gold quote "behold the only thing greater than you" acts as the permanent emotional anchor of the piece.
Personalization Integration: Because the front face utilizes a heavily textured surface adorned with permanent typography and borders, traditional screwed-in presentation plaques may not sit completely flush.
Customization Note: Personalization for families (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) is best achieved via custom-cut, high-adhesion gold vinyl lettering applied directly to the matching wood-grain space on the rear curved face of the base, ensuring it complements the existing gold trims and typography.
