Technical Design Specification: "Forever In Our Field" Farm Diorama Urn
SKU: LN-RUR-FARM
Series: Lifestyle & Novelty / Rural Heritage Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, diorama-style architectural approach where the structural base operates as the primary high-capacity ash chamber, allowing the upper element to serve as a deeply detailed, monumental landscape.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A substantial, circular plinth design providing a wide, stable footprint for the sprawling diorama.
External Dimensions: 10.5” Overall Diameter.
Overall Structural Height: 11.0” (From base to the top of the barn/silo).
Internal Compartment (Usable Space): 6.5” Width x 6.5” Depth x 5.0” Height.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access is located beneath the circular base. The engineered compartment provides an ample internal volume of 200+ cubic inches, suitable for one adult. The access panel is a custom-cut, square brushed metal plate. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts for a clean, seamless look.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a deeply emotional, highly detailed tribute to rural life and agricultural heritage, utilizing vibrant colors and lifelike sculptural elements.
The Farm Diorama: The upper element is a sprawling, 3D-sculpted landscape featuring a classic red wooden barn with white trim and a towering silver silo. The barn doors are open, revealing a warm, illuminated interior light/lantern effect.
The Scenery & Livestock: The barn sits atop a lush, sculpted hill of green foliage and grass. A winding dirt path leads to the front, dotted with lifelike, hand-painted farm animals, including cows and a pig. A flock of dark silhouette birds is mounted on discrete wire supports, appearing to fly above the barn roof.
The Plinth Base: The structural ash chamber is finished in a warm, medium-toned woodgrain with a beautifully stepped upper edge, grounding the vibrant pastoral scene above it.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Wood Finish (Base): The printed PLA base must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process—such as hydro-dipping or hand-painted faux-bois texturing—to perfectly simulate a natural, rich wood finish.
The Diorama Finish: The intricate landscape demands meticulous hand-painting. The red barn requires a matte finish to simulate painted wood, while the silo is finished in a metallic silver/pewter. The foliage, dirt path, and livestock must be painted with high-contrast, lifelike colors and sealed with a premium protective clear coat.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the bottom metal access panel remains completely secure without risking stripped plastic threads over time.
4. Typography & Personalization
Front Engraving: Personalization is integrated directly into the curved front face of the wooden base. The design features deep, dark-filled typography reading:
"FOREVER IN OUR FIELD"
"Old MacDonald had a farm, E-I-E-I-O"
"And on his farm he had a cow, E-I-E-I-O"
This text is flanked by subtle, low-relief silhouettes of farm animals (a dog, a cow, etc.).
The Rear Plaque System: The back of the base features an ornate, brushed-silver presentation plaque designed with a rustic, vintage aesthetic.
Plaque Details: The silver plaque is beautifully detailed with a central tractor motif at the top, bordered by a sculpted rooster on the left and a sheep on the right. The central engraving template provides fill-in lines reading: "SUNRISE _______ / SUNSET _______".
