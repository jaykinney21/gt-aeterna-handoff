1. General Form & Architecture
This design moves away from the traditional vase or pedestal profile, utilizing a classic, monumental rectangular chest/casket form factor. This shape maximizes internal volume while providing broad, flat canvases for detailed bas-relief storytelling.
Form Factor: A substantial rectangular chest featuring heavily stepped, architectural molding along both the lid and the base to create a formal, grounded presence.
External Dimensions: 11.0” Width x 7.25” Depth x 7.25” Height.
Internal Dimensions: 10.25” L x 6.25” W x 6.75” H.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access located beneath the chest. The engineered compartment provides an ample internal volume of 200+ cubic inches, suitable for one adult. The access panel is a custom-cut, rectangular metal plate designed to sit perfectly flush within a recessed lip. It is secured using four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-installed brass inserts.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a deeply emotional, traditional tribute utilizing detailed, high-relief landscape sculpting.
The Front Narrative (The Family): The entire front face functions as a three-dimensional canvas. It features a bas-relief sculpted landscape depicting a family of five (two adults, three children) standing together on a textured shoreline. They are gazing out over rolling ocean waves toward a setting sun on the horizon, framed by sculpted clouds.
The Rear Narrative (The Horizon): The peaceful landscape continues onto the back face of the chest. The figures are removed, leaving a serene, unbroken view of the ocean, the setting sun, and the clouds, symbolizing eternal peace and the passage of time.
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered for Additive Manufacturing using High-Strength PLA (Polylactic Acid) or similar durable polymer composites.
The Cast Bronze / Antiqued Wood Finish: The printed PLA components must undergo a specialized multi-step finishing process to achieve the rich, traditional look shown in the prototype. A warm, metallic bronze or dark wood-tone base coat is applied, followed by a dark, hand-rubbed antiquing wash. This wash is crucial; it must settle into the deep recesses of the bas-relief waves, the clouds, and the figures to provide high-contrast depth and highlight the sculptural details.
Hardware Engineering: Heavy-duty brass threaded inserts must be heat-pressed or ultrasonically welded into the PLA base during post-processing. This ensures the countersunk access panel can be securely opened and closed by families or funeral directors without stripping the plastic threads.
4. Typography & Personalization
The Lid Canvas: Because the front and rear faces are dedicated entirely to the sculpted artwork, the primary personalization zone is integrated directly into the top viewing plane (the lid).
Personalization Details: The flat top of the lid features a large, recessed rectangular plaque zone surrounded by a stepped border. The default engraving layout includes classic, elegant typography reading: "IN LOVING MEMORY OF / [Blank Lines] / SUNRISE: / SUNSET:".
Manufacturing Note: This flat, recessed zone is perfectly engineered for custom personalization. Funeral home distributors can utilize this area to adhere customized laser-engraved brass or acrylic plates, or utilize a flat-bed UV printer to print custom family details directly onto the lid surface.
