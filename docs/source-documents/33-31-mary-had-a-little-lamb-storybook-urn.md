Technical Design Specification: "Mary Had a Little Lamb" Storybook Urn
SKU: HC-FAM-STORYLAMB
Series: Family & Memorial / Storybook Heritage Collection
1. General Form & Architecture
This design utilizes a highly functional, diorama-style monumental architecture where the substantial rectangular pedestal base operates as the primary ash storage chamber, allowing the complex upper sculptural figurines to serve as a purely decorative tribute.
The Base (The Actual Urn): A heavy, rectangular chest design styled as a closed, classical storybook, providing a wide, heavily stabilized footprint.
External Base Dimensions: 11.5” Overall Width x 8.0” Overall Depth x 3.75” Base Height.
The Decorative Figurine: The upper sculptural assembly features a large lamb and a small child resting on a contoured grassy platform.
Overall Dimensions: The total structural height (from the bottom of the book base to the top of the lamb's head) reaches exactly 10.0”.
Access & Volumetrics: Bottom-loading access is located underneath the rectangular storybook base. The engineered interior cavity yields 200+ cubic inches of usable space, fully satisfying the standard capacity requirement for one adult.
Closure & Security: The base features a highly engineered closure system designed for permanent security:
A specifically designed recessed lip accommodates a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) that sits completely flush.
The plate is secured with four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, heavy-duty brass inserts embedded within the thick 3D-printed structural walls, guaranteeing maximum pull-out strength.
2. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a gentle, deeply emotional tribute to innocence and cherished memories, utilizing classic nursery rhyme iconography set upon a vintage storybook foundation.
The Central Canvas (The Characters): The primary focal point is a highly detailed, 3D-sculpted oversized white lamb resting peacefully. Standing affectionately beside the lamb is a small girl in a pink and white dress holding a green book. They are set upon a textured green grassy knoll adorned with sculpted daisies, smooth stones, and pink rose vines.
The Rear Keepsake Details: The back of the grassy knoll features a sculpted, heart-shaped keepsake compartment with a removable lid. Inside rests a tiny sculpted scroll and a miniature gold heart pendant.
The Base Canvas: The rectangular pedestal base mimics a thick, classical storybook. The front face is textured to simulate stacked book pages, framed by ornate, classical relief scrollwork and pink roses at the corners. A sculpted pink ribbon marker with a gold heart charm hangs gracefully over the bottom edge.
The Base Narrative: The rear spine of the book base features crisp serif typography reading:
"Loved • Cherished • Never Forgotten"
3. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary construction is engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing (3D Printed Resin). The design relies on thick, durable resin walls to contain the ashes securely within the base while supporting the substantial scale of the solid figurines. The empty weight is approximately 3.2 lbs (1.45 kg).
The Paint & Finish: The entire piece is finished in a specialized "Ceramic-Look" coating. The characters and floral elements require meticulous hand-painting to capture the soft pastel tones. The storybook base is finished with antiqued ivory and subtle gold/brown washes within the recesses to simulate the aged, precious nature of an antique book. The entire piece is sealed under a premium matte or satin clear coat.
Hardware Engineering: The integration of the brass threaded inserts directly into the 3D-printed resin walls of the base cavity during manufacturing is critical. This ensures that the heavy metal access plate can be tightened securely by the funeral director without stripping the base material.
4. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: The integrated "Loved • Cherished • Never Forgotten" quote on the rear spine acts as the permanent emotional anchor of the piece.
Personalization Integration: The front face of the storybook base (the "pages" side) provides a large, flat, framed zone specifically dedicated to personalization.
Customization Note: Personalization for families (localized Names, Sunrise/Sunset dates) is best achieved via custom-cut, high-adhesion vinyl lettering applied directly to this blank front space, or by mounting a custom-engraved brass or silver presentation plaque securely within the ornate floral frame.
