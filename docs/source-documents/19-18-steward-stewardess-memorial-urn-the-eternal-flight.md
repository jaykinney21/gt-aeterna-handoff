Technical Design Specification: Steward / Stewardess Memorial Urn ("The Eternal Flight")

1. Dimensional & Volumetric Data

Overall Dimensions (Urn with Base): 12.25 in (311.2 mm) Height x 7.75 in (196.9 mm) Overall Diameter.

Urn (Vase) Dimensions: 9.00 in (228.6 mm) Height x 7.75 in (196.9 mm) Maximum Diameter.

Base Dimensions: 3.25 in (82.6 mm) Height x 7.75 in (196.9 mm) Diameter.

Storage Capacity: 200+ Cubic Inches (engineered to hold one set of adult cremated remains).

2. Engineering & Structural Architecture

Access Method: Bottom-loading access through the underside of the circular pedestal base.

Closure Hardware System: Custom-cut rectangular metal plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) seated flush inside a recessed retention lip.

Security Fasteners: Four countersunk machine screws threaded into pre-modeled brass inserts embedded into the base substrate.

Chamber Integration: Lower pedestal assembly houses the secure, sealed internal storage cavity.

3. Visual & Sculptural Elements

Vessel Aesthetic: Soft blush-pink celestial sky motif with cloud textures and silhouettes of an ascending commercial airliner.

Front Figurative Details: High-detail sculpted figures of cabin crew / flight attendants in classic navy blue uniforms standing poised upon cloud tiers.
Front Inscription: Base nameplate inscribed in script with "The Eternal Flight" accented by a gold-leaf border and heart motif.

Reverse Scriptural / Dedication Verse: Scripted memorial message reading: "Thank you for your service. Always remembered." accented by a golden heart emblem.

Collection Motto: "Heroes Behind Every Journey Is a Hero — An aviation collection honoring those who keep us flying, safe & connected."

4. Material & Finishing Protocols

Core Substrate: High-quality 3D-printed ceramic or composite resin.

Finish Details: Expertly crafted high-gloss ceramic-look finish with vibrant, durable, hand-painted details, soft pink marbling/cloud tones, and brushed gold trim on the base and lid collars.

5. Typography & Personalization

Personalization Area: The rear base or vessel body accommodates bespoke engraving for names, service dates, airline affiliations, or a custom farewell sentiment.
