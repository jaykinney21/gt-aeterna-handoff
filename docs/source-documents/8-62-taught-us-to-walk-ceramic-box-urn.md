2. Engineering & Structural Architecture
This design utilizes a classic, display-oriented rectangular box architecture featuring a slanted top viewing panel and a dedicated pedestal base.
Internal Volume Strategy: The interior of the rectangular box functions as a single, continuous hollow chamber, specifically engineered to accommodate the full 200+ cubic inch requirement for one set of adult cremated remains.
Access Method: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the rectangular pedestal base.
Hardware & Closure: The access port requires a custom-cut, rectangular metal base plate (brushed stainless steel or aluminum) designed to sit completely flush within a recessed lip.
Secure Fastening: The metal plate is secured utilizing countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into heavy-duty brass inserts that are permanently embedded within the thick ceramic walls, ensuring a secure and lasting closure without risking fractures to the ceramic material.
3. Visual & Narrative Elements
The aesthetic is a gentle, highly narrative tribute honoring a mother's guidance and love, utilizing soft pastel tones and delicate painted floral motifs.
The Form: The vessel takes the shape of a structured box with an angled, slanted roof that acts as the primary visual display canvas.
The Upper Narrative (Slanted Roof): The top angled panel features a detailed, painted scene depicting a kneeling mother holding the hands of two young daughters in a serene meadow setting, complete with a tree, flowers, and flying butterflies.
The Front Typography Canvas: The flat, lower front panel acts as a dedicated text zone, framed by delicate painted green vines and purple lavender sprigs. It features elegant black serif and script typography reading:
"She taught us to walk"
"and then let us Fly"
The Base & Trims: The main body of the urn is a soft, warm cream or off-white. The structure is accented by soft purple trims outlining the roofline, the separation point between the upper and lower panels, and the stepped pedestal base.
4. Material & Finishing Protocols
Core Construction: As explicitly noted in the schematics, the primary structural construction utilizes thick Ceramic Walls. The manufacturing process must accommodate the precision embedding of the brass inserts during the ceramic casting or firing phases to ensure structural integrity for the bottom-loading hardware.
The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a premium, smooth ceramic glaze. The intricate meadow scene and the framing floral vines require delicate, high-resolution decal application or hand-painted finishing prior to the final clear glaze firing, ensuring the artwork is permanently sealed and protected.
5. Typography & Personalization
Primary Branding & Narrative: The front face carries the permanent thematic anchor ("She taught us to walk and then let us Fly").
Personalization Integration: The tall, flat rear panel is specifically designated as the primary personalization zone. It is bordered by matching purple floral and vine motifs to maintain 360-degree aesthetic consistency.
Standard Typography Formatting: The rear panel accommodates custom text reading:
"Your love will guide us until we meet again" (Header)
A central purple floral divider
"Name Here" (Primary Name)
"Date Here - Date Here" (Sunrise/Sunset Dates)
Customization Note: For bespoke orders, the custom typography on the rear panel can be integrated into the ceramic glazing process or applied via permanent, high-adhesion memorial decals by the funeral director.
