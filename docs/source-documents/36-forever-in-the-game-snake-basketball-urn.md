2. Engineering & Structural Architecture 
This design utilizes a continuous volumetric strategy to maximize internal capacity while using the complex outer sculpture as a functional, stabilizing structural element. ● Internal Volume Strategy: The interior of the 7.0" basketball is completely hollow. This primary upper chamber passes directly down into a deep, continuous hollow chamber within the 8.5" pedestal base, accommodating the full 200+ cubic inch requirement. ● Structural Reinforcement: The snake sculpture is not merely decorative; it is engineered to wrap securely around the spherical urn, anchoring it firmly to the base and providing rigid structural stability and strength to the overall assembly. 
● Access Method: Primary secure access is bottom-loading, located on the underside of the base via a custom-cut stainless steel access plate. 
● Hardware & Closure: The metal plate is secured utilizing four countersunk machine screws. These thread directly into pre-modeled, brass threaded inserts embedded within the structural walls to guarantee maximum pull-out strength. 
● Environmental Seal: The bottom opening features a precisely engineered silicone gasket channel. When the stainless steel plate is secured, it compresses the gasket, ensuring a permanent, secure seal. 
3. Visual & Narrative Elements 
The aesthetic is a powerful, high-contrast tribute utilizing dynamic character sculpting and classic athletic colorways. 
● The Central Canvas (The Vessel): The primary focal point is a highly textured basketball finished in alternating, vibrant gold/yellow and deep purple panels, separated by standard black structural seam lines. 
● The Narrative Element: A heavily scaled, textured black snake coils tightly around the basketball in a figure-eight pattern. The snake's head rests atop the ball, featuring piercing, painted red eyes and a subtle tongue detail. 
● The Base Canvas: The wide circular pedestal base provides a classic foundation, shaped with a slight inward taper and finished to simulate a rich, dark wood grain. 4. Material & Finishing Protocols 
● Core Construction: Engineered utilizing advanced Additive Manufacturing. The complex, intertwining geometry of the snake and ball is fully optimized for High-Strength SLA Resin. This digital CAD file is engineered specifically for industrial, on-demand 3D printing pipelines, allowing for the rapid production of the highly detailed scales and thick-walled storage base with zero minimum order quantities. 
● The Paint & Finish: The piece demands a specialized multi-stage painting protocol. The basketball requires a stippled texture application under the purple and gold enamels to simulate composite leather. The snake requires a matte black finish with subtle dark-grey dry-brushing to highlight the 3D-modeled scales. The pedestal base utilizes a premium faux-wood finishing process.

● Primary Branding & Narrative: Integrated directly onto the front face of the faux-wood base is classic, gold serif typography reading:

The CAD file can also be natively updated prior to printing to incorporate deeply engraved team numbers, logos, localized names, or specific memorial dates directly into the base resin.
