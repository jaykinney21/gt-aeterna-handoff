## Commercial conditions that matter

Published terms make shipment dates estimates rather than guarantees. Special delivery/packaging requires arrangements and can incur charges. Their standard risk-of-loss basis is ex works unless otherwise agreed. Orders cannot be canceled after placement; specifications cannot then be changed. Nonconforming parts require notice within **three business days of delivery**, return within three business days of notice, and supplier confirmation; replacement is the stated remedy. The terms do not establish ordinary consumer-remorse returns. [Terms, sections 3, 5 and 6](https://www.jawstec.com/terms-and-conditions/)

Our implication: clarify transit insurance, damage claims and an end-customer inspection/replacement process before offering direct fulfillment. A three-day supplier claim window needs an explicit operational agreement; it is not a substitute for our customer-facing return obligations.

## Not established by the official pages reviewed

- Repeated delivery to a different end-customer address while retaining our billing account.
- Blind or branded packing slips, labels and outer cartons; suppression of supplier invoices/marketing.
- Decorative hand finishing matching approved urn samples.
- Retail presentation boxes, protective inserts, urn + keepsake + jewelry kitting, QR-card matching and storage of supplied packaging.
- Consumer return routing and replacement dispatch commitments.
- Native Amazon/Shopify integration, public fulfillment API or automated tracking synchronization.

Absence of public documentation is not a refusal; these are qualification questions.

2. Quote recurring **one-unit-per-customer production**, separate from 10/25/50-unit consolidated batches. Ask for a price-validity period and any setup fee charged on every order.

4. Provide production-to-carrier handoff time for the fully decorated product, variability, peak-season capacity, transit options and representative zone-based freight costs. ZIP [sample ZIP omitted] alone cannot price nationwide fulfillment.
5. Confirm approved-sample finish repeatability, closure assembly and QC evidence, damage responsibility, claim windows and return destination.
6. Ask whether ordering/tracking is manual, CSV or supported API; request documentation before building any integration.
