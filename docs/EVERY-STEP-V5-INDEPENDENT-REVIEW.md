# Every Step Legacy v5 — final independent art review

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

September 22, 2026. Reviewed actual final pass 20 front, rear, side, top and detail renders against document 63’s reference board. This evaluates visible resemblance and quality, not merely the presence of requested features.

## Result

**V5 is materially closer to the source and ready for another human image review. It is not an exact reconstruction or an approved final design.** The initially rejected inflated collar, triangular pinching, clipped bronze edges and cap-junction notches were corrected through successive render checks. The final upper blade is contracted inside the writing shell’s outer edge, restoring the source’s upright layered reading.

## Remaining source differences for the owner to judge

- The upper shoulder’s dark reveal still looks broader and deeper in the render than the compact recess in the reference. A shallow closing surface was added, but its dark appearance remains a visual interpretation.
- The hidden side return, cap orientation and exact curve tension cannot be uniquely recovered from the illustrated reference. V5 presents a consistent interpretation; it should not be described as traced to exact 3D fidelity.
- Texture distribution, bronze warmth, stitch rhythm and plaque proportions are closer but not identical. Their final aesthetic acceptance belongs to the owner, and physical finish cannot be approved from a render.

No new obvious pinching, clipped cap junction or lettering overlap was observed in the final reviewed views. That is an image-quality finding, not a comprehensive geometric intersection test.

## Comparison method

Approximate image-space source guides were body width/height ≈0.55, cap apparent height/width ≈0.49 and body/base width ≈0.88. Matching view angle and displayed urn height matter: unequal crops can make a correct change look worse or hide a proportion error. These image ratios are not engineering dimensions. The source’s “the crowds” width is roughly 38–40% of visible body width; widening the final inscription addresses the earlier 30–32% rendering.

No capacity, printable topology, closure, minimum-wall, material-process or manufacturing approval is included in this art review.
