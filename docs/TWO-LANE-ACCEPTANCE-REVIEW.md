## Every Step Legacy: digital candidate

Scope: document 63, original LN-SPT-EVERYSTEP, 11-inch version. Keep the separate 10-inch/150-cubic-inch proposal and jewelry CAD outside this model release. Retain the original athletic black/bronze interpretation rather than DOC NOTES' trail interpretation.

- Record assumptions prominently: provisional body width, wall thickness and printing process; fixed decorative top while bottom loading is the working interpretation. A supplier-review candidate may retain explicit assumptions; a manufacturing release cannot leave the closure or process unresolved.
- Keep the complete exterior, including cap, relief, text and bronze rims, within the source overall 11-inch height and 6.75-inch maximum width. The 9-inch upper vessel plus 2-inch base already uses the full height. A pill-shaped cap cannot be fully specified by its stated “diameter”; record its actual two axes and the source ambiguity.
- Supply front, rear, side, top, underside and section views with dimensions. Compare asymmetric silhouette, sweeping divider, tread, lacing, three base bands, pill cap and front/rear plaques to the source reference. Distinguish a stylized interpretation from exact reference reconstruction.
- Measure one connected, usable cavity after subtracting all walls, bosses, fasteners and closure intrusions. Report both cubic inches and liters; minimum target is 200 cubic inches. Do not reuse the envelope study's 226.49 cubic inches as the finished model result.
- Check minimum wall thickness, mesh closure/orientation, self-intersections, accidental internal shells and part collisions. State which checks were measured and which remain qualitative. Report final overall bounds including decorative geometry.
- Include a practical filling aperture and connected interior; review access for supports or powder removal with the selected process. Hidden trapped voids are not usable storage. Explain body/base joining if they are separate print parts.
- Model bottom plate, recess, screws, inserts and seal as individually identifiable components. Provide a preliminary hardware list, assembly order and closure section. Tolerances, insert installation and sealing compression require the supplier's process guidance before release.
- Preserve the exact source narrative on the front/rear and “FOREVER IN OUR HEARTS” base plaque; leave personal names/dates as an editable personalization area. Check actual relief/engraving size against process resolution and finishing thickness.
- Deliver editable source, regenerating script if used, separate candidate STL parts with explicit millimeter units, measurement/assumption report, previews and a file inventory. Supply STEP only where it is a meaningful solid mechanical export; do not present a converted faceted sculpture as original parametric CAD.

### Physical gates after digital delivery

Select material/process with the manufacturer; test closure coupon and insert retention, seal performance, fill access, stability with representative contents, finish adhesion/cleanability, assembly and protective packaging. Confirm measured capacity and dimensions on a full sample. Water resistance, tamper resistance, outdoor durability and universal niche fit remain unsupported until separately verified. Obtain the customer's actual niche opening/depth where fit matters. No sample order is implied by this review.
