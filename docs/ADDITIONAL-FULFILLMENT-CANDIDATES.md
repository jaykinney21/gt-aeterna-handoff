# Additional 3D manufacturing and fulfillment candidates

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

Research checkpoint: September 19, 2026. Official supplier sources checked; these are supplier claims, not verified production performance. No inquiries, account creations, uploads or orders were made in this research lane.

## Recommendation

Add **3D Print Joy** to the first qualification wave: its Ontario, California location is convenient for the [sample ZIP omitted] sample destination, and it explicitly offers printing, painting, assembly and branded customer fulfillment. **DC Additive Pros** is a second domestic fulfillment candidate. **Sculpteo** offers a stronger industrial-material comparison with documented direct-customer shipping and custom packing slips. Retain **Yorkshire3D** as a UK alternative, **Shop3D** principally for jewelry/accessories, and **Slant 3D** for smaller keepsakes or a future redesigned product.

None is yet proven to deliver our complete premium urn, companion keepsake and jewelry bundle at quantity one. A supplier's ability to print and ship is not confirmation of finish quality, usable cavity capacity, closure integrity, packaging or an acceptable customer return process.

### 1. 3D Print Joy

The supplier advertises dropship orders from Ontario, California, branded packaging, order tracking and an API. The claimed 24–48-hour turnaround is for most products, **not a quotation for a finished urn**. [Dropship service](https://playjoy3d.com/services/dropship)

Its large-format page specifies a **500 × 500 × 500 mm** envelope, 0.1–0.3 mm layers and PLA, ABS, PETG and TPU. That nominal envelope accommodates the original urn dimensions, subject to orientation and manufacturability. [Large-format capability](https://playjoy3d.com/capabilities/large-format)

Sanding, priming, painting, multi-part bonding and inspection are advertised as in-house work. Matte, gloss and textured finishes are offered. Detailed multi-color relief painting, metallic effects and long-term finish durability still need physical evaluation. [Post-processing](https://playjoy3d.com/capabilities/post-processing)

**Ask first:** Can you supply one fully finished adult urn repeatedly, with metal plate/inserts/gasket, accept externally supplied jewelry, pack one coordinated bundle, and ship under our seller identity? Request a local sample-review option, a firm painted-unit SLA, minimum charge, startup/packaging costs and damage remedy. Contact through its quote page or published phone **[phone omitted; check official contact page]**; do not guess the masked email.

### 2. DC Additive Pros

Its Build & Ship program explicitly offers quantity-one production and blind shipment under the customer's brand. Charges comprise manufacturing, per-order handling and destination/weight shipping. The page states 2–4 business days after file/quote approval, subject to queue, and continental-US delivery. Listed polymers include PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA and nylon. It flags file defects but does not redesign files; its summary limits liability and transfers carrier risk to the buyer. [Build & Ship](https://dcadditivepros.com/pages/build-and-ship)

The home calculator separately mentions a **$20 minimum order**, while the program says no minimum; resolve quantity minimum versus minimum charge in writing. Contact: **info@dcadditivepros.com**. [Home and calculator](https://dcadditivepros.com/)

**Ask first:** What is the guaranteed one-piece envelope for the chosen material? Can you paint artistic finishes and install/test closure hardware? Can you store and kit our jewelry/printed inserts? Clarify minimum charge, insured shipping, photo inspection, reprints, return address and whether quoted turnaround includes finishing. Request the per-order handling fee separately.

### 3. Sculpteo

The current API service page advertises order, quote and tracking automation, no minimum quantity and free partner API access. [API service](https://www.sculpteo.com/en/services/api-services/)

Its detailed order documentation explicitly permits shipment to customer addresses and agreed customized packing slips/invoices. It warns that orders may split across parcels. The detailed page uses an older authentication pattern, so verify the **current program and documentation**, not that legacy implementation. [Order documentation](https://www.sculpteo.com/es/developer/webapi/order/order/)

Current PA12 overview lists **300 × 300 × 590 mm SLS** and **274 × 370 × 380 mm MJF**, plus smoothing, painting and polishing. Other help pages give different maxima; obtain the exact selected material/finish envelope in the quote. [PA12 capability](https://pro.sculpteo.com/en/materials/pa12/)

July 2026 terms identify a minimum-order fee for orders below 50 in the listed currencies and potential import charges. Do not treat a single-part policy as zero minimum spend. [Terms](https://pro.sculpteo.com/en/terms/)

**Ask first:** Is customized direct-customer fulfillment currently available for a small US brand? Which factory ships our parts, and can all bundle components ship in one carton? Does painting include masked metallic/artistic detail or only uniform coatings? Request all-in shipping/import terms, packaging minimums and agreed seller identity. Contact: **contact@sculpteo.com**, published in the order documentation.

### 4. Yorkshire3D

The detailed program promises brand packaging/inserts, customer-facing tracking, personalization proofs and reprints for production faults or carrier damage. Prices are agreed per SKU; premium packaging and personalization are extra. It states no monthly fee or ongoing quantity minimum **after an onboarding sample run**. It also distinguishes retailer-owned customer service and change-of-mind returns. [White-label program](https://www.yorkshire3d.co.uk/solutions/dropshipping)

FDM options include PLA, PETG, TPU and ASA; standard dispatch is advertised at 3–5 days. A maximum build envelope was not established from the reviewed material. [FDM service](https://www.yorkshire3d.co.uk/services/fdm-3d-printing)

**Ask first:** Can the original adult bodies print without splitting, and can you apply premium painted finishes? What does the onboarding sample run cost and require? Quote delivered US consumer orders with customs treatment, tracking, insurance and a US returns option. Ask whether external jewelry can be stocked and consolidated. Use the [official contact route](https://www.yorkshire3d.co.uk/contact).

### 5. Shop3D

Its main site offers digital-file manufacturing, branded fulfillment, sample orders and jewelry metals including sterling silver and gold. Adult-urn dimensions/material suitability were not established. Contact: **info@shop3d.io**. [Shop3D](https://www.shop3d.io/)

Important qualification: the shipping page lists supplier-branded free packaging; unbranded packaging at **£19.99/month with 10 monthly orders**, branded packaging at **£149.99/month with 500**, and premium at **£349.99/month with 5,000**. This qualifies the main site's general no-monthly-fee/no-minimum marketing. Listed US shipping rates are not reliable adult-urn estimates without package size/weight confirmation. [Packaging tiers](https://www.shop3d.io/whitelabel-shipping-solutions/)

**Ask first:** Are those tiers current, and is there an affordable pilot exception? Can jewelry include a functional sealed ashes compartment, matching finish and chain? Can jewelry be shipped to our urn partner for combined packing? Adult urn production is a separate capability question; do not assume the jewelry program covers it.

### 6. Slant 3D / Teleport

Slant explicitly offers ecommerce-to-customer printing/packing/shipping, PLA/PETG and optional third-party components. Portals is its own storefront; Teleport connects an existing store. Its separate FBA service sends labeled inventory to Amazon, which is a different arrangement from printing a consumer order on demand. [Slant services](https://www.slant3d.com/), [FBA service explanation](https://www.slant3d.com/blog/14ddea6f-3249-41fb-93fe-0e311d5b64ff)

The published FAQ specifies **220 × 220 × 220 mm**, fixed print settings and no custom boxes; premium access supports a printed insert. Original bodies exceed 220 mm in at least one measured axis. Rotating a mesh might change its bounding box, but we cannot assume fit or reduce dimensions and preserve capacity. [Official FAQ](https://www.slantpod.com/support)

**Ask first:** Is there a larger/custom production path outside the standard plugin? Are finishing, metal plates and supplied jewelry possible? Otherwise qualify it for smaller keepsakes only. Use the [production contact form](https://www.slant3d.com/contact).

## Consistent questions for every qualifying supplier

Send the same five original-spec references and explicit component mapping. Request two separate offers: samples delivered to **[sample ZIP omitted], California, USA**, and repeat one-customer orders throughout the continental US. Shipping costs must follow destination and packed dimensions, not the sample ZIP alone.

1. Can you manufacture the original dimensions, with at least 200 cubic inches usable interior capacity (210 for the original sneaker), secure specified closure and no unapproved changes? Identify engineering corrections separately.
2. What polymer/process, wall design, painting/clearcoat and hardware would you propose? Provide a comparable finished sample photo and a priced finish coupon before committing to the full finish.
3. Is quantity one available **after approval**, with no batch requirement? Itemize setup, sample, repeated production, finishing, hardware, QC, packing, handling, storage and transport.
4. Can you receive separately manufactured jewelry, stock it, match components to a SKU and ship a single urn/keepsake/jewelry carton? Who replenishes and insures accessory stock?
5. Can every customer-visible carton, slip and insert identify our business, without supplier marketing? Obtain photographs of a proposed customer shipment before accepting the arrangement.
6. What is the committed order-to-dispatch time **including finishing and personalization**? What happens during backlog, a rejected print or an out-of-stock accessory?
7. Can you provide per-order photos and records of dimensions, closure fit, contents and packed condition? What is the approval standard for matching the reference finish?
8. What are the claim window, replacement/refund responsibility, insured-loss procedure, return address and remedy for latent defects? Separate manufacturing-origin issues from our file-origin issues.
9. Can initial orders use email/manual submission without paid integration? What exact data and tracking handoff are needed for Amazon merchant fulfillment later?

## Decision gates

- **Qualification reply:** written ability to meet dimensions, finish, quantity-one and branded fulfillment; explicit exclusions.
- **Engineering/finish sample:** resolve source-file/spec differences and approve a physical finish standard.
- **Full sample to [sample ZIP omitted]:** check cavity volume, closures, finish and packaging before retail claims.
- **Customer-style test shipment:** one complete bundle, correct paperwork and tracking, including damage/returns workflow review.
- **Commercial decision:** compare landed repeated-unit cost and dispatch reliability with manufacturing a small batch and using a separate US packer.

A complete order integration is unnecessary until a supplier passes these gates. No provider's general turnaround or size claim establishes that our finished urn is production-ready.
