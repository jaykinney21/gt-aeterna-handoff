## Minimum listing package

1. One approved physical product with measured usable capacity, dimensions, weight, accurate material/finish, closure instructions, and real sample photos.
2. SKU and product identifier or approved exemption; confirmed Amazon product type/category and any applicable eligibility requirements.
3. Actual supplier quote, replenishment lead time, packaging measurements, shipping costs, returns/replacement allowance, and fulfillment choice.
4. Accurate title, bullets, images, delivery promise, and support contact. Distinguish adult, pet, and keepsake capacities clearly. Do not reuse unsupported material, environmental, handmade, or branded-character claims from concept documents.
5. Verify the offer can be purchased and fulfilled before enabling ads. Obtain the actual Amazon fee estimate using the packaged item and selected category/fulfillment method. [Amazon revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.com/pricing/estimate).

Recommendation: use a small stocked batch and merchant fulfillment for the initial quality-learning cycle if shipping can be dependable. Consider FBA for a proven standardized SKU after package dimensions and damage rates are known. Custom designs are a different operational offer and should be added only after the standard SKU works.

## How to triage ads

Use Sponsored Products for the first single-SKU test. Automatic targeting can discover relevant searches/products; use search-term results to inform a small manual campaign. Keep branded and generic terms identifiable. Apply negative targeting to clearly irrelevant traffic. Amazon documents this discovery-and-refinement workflow. [Sponsored Products targeting guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/targeting-with-sponsored-products).

| Symptom | Check first | Typical response after diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| No impressions | Offer availability, eligibility, campaign status, budget, targeting relevance, bid | Resolve eligibility/offer issue before raising bids |
| Impressions but few clicks | Main image, title, price, relevance of search terms | Improve product presentation or narrow mismatched traffic |
| Clicks but no orders | Capacity clarity, actual material, closure, price, delivery promise, listing trust, attribution lag | Fix listing/offer or pause losing targets; do not scale clicks |
| Orders but poor margin | Fees, landed cost, fulfillment, returns, CPC and conversion rate | Reduce bids/costs or improve offer economics |
| Profitable orders | Enough conversion evidence, sample/return quality, stock cover | Increase budget gradually, watching contribution and inventory |

Record impressions, clicks, spend, orders, attributed sales, CPC, conversion rate, ACOS, total sales, and returns. Review delivery/budget frequently; judge search terms after attribution has had time to mature. Sparse launch data is uncertain. A suggested guardrail is to review a no-order target once its spend reaches roughly one allowable acquisition cost, and pause/reduce if relevance or conversion evidence does not justify continuing. This is a proposed control, not a universal Amazon rule.

## Work backward from unit economics

`Contribution before ads = selling revenue − product cost − freight/duties − packaging − Amazon fees − fulfillment − expected returns/replacements − other variable costs`

`Break-even ACOS = contribution before ads ÷ attributed selling revenue`

`Allowable ad cost per order = contribution before ads − desired contribution after ads`

`Planning CPC ceiling ≈ allowable ad cost per order × conversion rate`

Amazon defines ACOS as advertising spend divided by attributed sales and explains that break-even ACOS depends on margin. Include **all variable costs**, not only manufacturing, when setting your limit. [Amazon ACOS guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/acos-advertising-cost-of-sales).

Illustration only: a $150 sale with $105 of total variable costs leaves $45 before ads. Break-even ACOS is 30%. Keeping $15 after ads allows $30 acquisition cost, or 20% target ACOS. At an assumed 5% conversion rate, that suggests about $1.50 CPC. These are not forecasts, supplier quotes, or actual Amazon fee estimates. Fixed overhead still comes out of the remaining contribution.

Set the total experiment budget after actual quotes and listing readiness. The existing GT ad canvas and content agents are not proof of an Amazon Ads integration. Start with Seller Central/Campaign Manager and downloadable reports; an automated ad agent is unnecessary for validating the first SKU.
