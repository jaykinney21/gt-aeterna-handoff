# Xometry: urn manufacturing and fulfillment assessment

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

Researched September 19, 2026 using official Xometry sources. Sample destination: US ZIP **[sample ZIP omitted]**. Customer orders would have varying delivery addresses. Research only; no account changes, upload, outreach or purchase.

**Assessment:** credible candidate for samples and repeat manufacturing; **not yet a verified turnkey, white-label consumer fulfillment partner**. Ordering a single part and selecting a delivery address does not establish branded packing, accessory kitting, customer returns or Amazon-ready fulfillment.

## What is documented

| Topic | Public evidence and implication |
|---|---|
| One-off quantities | Most services have no minimum; one part is allowed. Price breaks depend on quantity/process. Each urn is a multi-part product, so request a finished assembly price rather than treating “one part” as one finished urn. [Minimum quantity](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/642-what-is-your-minimum-order-quantity) |
| Addresses | Customers can add/edit saved shipping addresses. This supports multiple destinations in the account, but does not confirm automated one-order-per-consumer fulfillment or splitting one manufacturing batch among consumers. [Account details](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/699-how-to-update-your-xometry-account-information) |
| Shipping | Domestic 3D-printed orders qualify for free two-day or ground options; checkout determines eligibility and charges. Freight exclusions apply. Confirm shipping for the complete finished urn and any overseas production duties separately; do not assume the ZIP92336 sample rate applies to all customers. [Shipping costs](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/651) |
| Carrier account | DHL/FedEx/UPS/USPS account billing can be requested, subject to account approval. [Carrier accounts](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/847-can-i-use-my-companys-shipping-account) |
| Artistic finish | MJF/SLS options include smoothing, dye and Cerakote; sanding/painting/plating or other custom finishes require manual review. SLA also permits custom polishing/painting/plating requests. These capabilities do not guarantee our exact multicolor, metallic artistic finish. [Finishes](https://www.xometry.com/finishes/) |
| Assembly | Assembly services are listed among value-added capabilities. Urn hardware, seal, closure installation and accessory kitting still need explicit acceptance and pricing. [Capabilities](https://www.xometry.com/capabilities/) |
| Tracking | Buyer dashboard provides line-item status, tracking and downloadable packing lists. [Order status](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/695) |
| Quality claims | RMA requests can be submitted up to six months after delivery; Xometry reviews evidence and determines return, remake or refund. Customer modifications/secondary finishing damage is excluded. This is a manufacturing quality process, not a consumer remorse-return service. [Return policy](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/653-xometrys-return-policy) |
| Pre-shipment exceptions | Standard inspections may allow minor nonconforming parts to ship for buyer review. Orders with other inspection types or a Certificate of Conformance receive contact about unresolved nonconformances before shipping. For direct consumer delivery, require explicit approval before any deviation ships. [Nonconformances](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/676-how-does-xometry-handle-non-conformances) |
| Lead time and cancellation | Shipment dates are estimates, not binding promises; custom orders generally cannot be canceled unless Xometry agrees. Special packaging/delivery arrangements can incur cost. Obtain a repeat-order production commitment before advertising a consumer delivery date. [Customer agreement, sections 3 and 5](https://www.xometry.com/service-agreement/) |
| API | Public developer documentation covers manufacturing partners' accepted jobs, job/line-item statuses and offers. It is not evidence of a buyer-side Amazon/Shopify order-placement API. [Developer documentation](https://developer.xometry.com/docs/getting-started) |

## Blind shipping is a specific unresolved issue

A publicly accessible Xometry partner packing poster instructs direct-ship partners to include Xometry coasters and apply an Xometry decal. The filename appears historical (2019), so it is not proof of today's universal practice, but it is evidence that **“direct shipping” can mean Xometry-branded supplier-to-buyer shipping, not our brand's white-label fulfillment**. Ask for a written exception and inspect a sample of the actual package before offering direct consumer delivery. [Partner packing poster](https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/340051/Partner%20Assets/Partner%20One-Pagers/20190905-Xometry-Shipping-Procedures-Poster-3DP-V2.pdf)

Not publicly established in the reviewed sources: our brand as seller/shipper on all paperwork; no supplier promotions; retail presentation box; multi-item urn/keepsake/jewelry kits; QR card insertion and matching; individualized engraving workflows; one-order-per-customer fulfillment fee; guaranteed repeat lead times; consumer return handling; a native Amazon/Shopify fulfillment connector.

## Exact quote questions to send

1. Quote one fully finished sample of each original design to ZIP92336, with CAD remediation priced separately from manufacturing, hardware, finish, assembly and packaging.
2. After sample approval, can you accept individual consumer orders for the identical design and ship each to a different US residential address? What fixed per-order/setup/fulfillment charges apply, and can pricing remain fixed for a stated period?
3. Can every parcel use our seller identity, packing slip and return instructions, without Xometry/manufacturer branding or prices? Please provide a photographed packing example and any limitations.
4. Can you supply/store presentation boxes, add our instructions/QR cards, and kit matching keepsakes/jewelry from separate sources? Quote setup, storage, minimum packaging purchases and per-kit fees separately.
5. What is the repeat-order time from approval/payment to carrier scan, including finish, personalization and QC? State realistic upper ranges, capacity limits, holiday exclusions and rush fees.
6. Can you provide final cosmetic photographs and closure/assembly checks before release, and hold all deviations pending our written approval?
7. How are lost parcels, damage, defects and remakes handled when the recipient is our customer? Who pays shipping and where are returns sent? Can you accommodate our consumer return process separately from manufacturing RMAs?
8. What manual, CSV, buyer API or storefront order options exist? How do tracking updates reach us, and what customer data is required?

## Recommendation

Keep Xometry in the original-five quote round for production-cost benchmarking and sample quality. Price two distinct operating modes: **single finished units sent directly to customers**, and **small batches sent to us/a fulfillment provider for final QC and packing**. Do not score it as a confirmed dropshipping provider until the questions above are answered and a complete sample parcel passes inspection.
