## 2. Scope Every Step Legacy from references to editable CAD and STL

**Source:** document 63, `_1. Every Step Legacy_ Urn+10_keepsake&jewelry.docx`, in the non-specific categories archive. SKU LN-SPT-EVERYSTEP. This is the abstract athletic vessel with a sweeping divider and tread/mesh textures; do not confuse it with Legacy Step, Legacy Ascend or other similarly named designs.

### Resolve the brief before generating geometry

- Original: 11-inch overall height, 6.75-inch maximum diameter, 200+ in³, continuous upper/base cavity and metal bottom access plate.
- Smaller proposal: 10-inch overall height, 6-inch overall diameter, 5.5-inch vessel diameter and **150+ in³**. This does not preserve the original capacity requirement.
- Smaller proposal lists a 7.5-inch body and 2-inch base, leaving 0.5 inch unexplained in the 10-inch total. Define the lid and assembly contribution.
- Original describes a pill-shaped lid but gives a circular-style diameter. Decide whether the lid is decorative/fixed or an additional functional opening alongside bottom access.
- Original finish is black/bronze; accessory notes use rose-gold tones. Select the urn finish and distinguish it from accessory variants.

**Proposed first modeling scope:** the original 11-inch / 200+ in³ vessel, with accessories and the smaller version deferred. This is a recommendation awaiting the eventual modeling brief, not a decision to start construction.

### Tools have different jobs

| Tool | Proposed role | Acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Tripo | Optional image-to-mesh experiment to establish the exterior silhouette | STL export is not proof of an engineered cavity, closure or editable solid CAD |
| Blender, already installed | Direct exterior modeling or cleanup of an AI mesh; geometry for relief, tread, text and presentation | Retain an editable .blend source and inspect real geometry, not just texture appearance |
| MeshLab | Optional mesh inspection, cleanup or simplification if a specific need arises | It is a mesh-processing tool, not the primary image-to-CAD generator |
| FreeCAD or build123d | Dimension-controlled cavity, closure interfaces, plate and other mechanical geometry | Preserve native/parametric source and STEP where appropriate; combine with the decorative mesh deliberately |

Tool-role references: [Tripo image-to-3D](https://www.tripo3d.ai/), [MeshLab](https://www.meshlab.net/), and the previously sourced [CAD research](HARDWARE-RESTART-RESEARCH.md). No tool subscription or installation is selected by this plan.

### Planned experiment

1. Freeze front/side/back references and dimensions, remove contradictory annotations from the working brief, and define the cavity/closure separately from the surface artwork.
2. Compare a small, capped Tripo exterior trial with a simple direct Blender model. Decide the attempt/cost cap before any paid generation. Judge silhouette accuracy, editability and cleanup effort; a good render alone is not a pass.
3. Engineer the internal vessel and access hardware to the chosen capacity and manufacturing process. Add real relief where the print needs texture; do not count a texture map as printable detail.
4. Check assembled dimensions, cavity volume after intrusions, walls, intersections, mesh integrity, closure fit and export scale. Keep printed tolerances provisional until agreed with a supplier.
5. Export separate component STLs, editable sources, appropriate STEP solids, assembly/section drawings and a revisioned validation record. A faceted STL converted to STEP is not a substitute for editable engineering geometry.

**Planned deliverable:** one documented, editable design candidate and manufacturing exports with known limitations. Physical prototyping is a subsequent task. No CAD has been created for this workstream yet.
