## Plaque comparison

The main model retains the specified front and rear plaques. A separate front comparison omits the plaque visually and restores the bead field behind it. This is a comparison view, not approval to delete the specified plaque.

## Remaining differences and limits

- The cap's isolated outline is fuller, but the front view still presents a more elongated face than the source illustration. Camera and cap inclination affect this difference.
- The source's core texture has stronger small specular glints. V7 has rounded irregular highlights but a more evident repeated row structure.
- Hidden depth and the under-cap return remain a 3D interpretation; the concept board does not uniquely determine them. No exact-match claim is warranted.

The final checked front, detail and rear views show no obvious new clipping, pinching, projecting shelf or lettering overlap. The requested changes are reviewable, but their presence is not equivalent to final owner approval. This report makes no capacity, wall-thickness, closed-solid, printability or manufacturing claim.
