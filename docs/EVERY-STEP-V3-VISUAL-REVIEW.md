## Source landmarks supplied before rendering

The reference front illustration places the body approximately within x137–264 and y70–299 on the 1024 × 682 board. Its base spans approximately x126–270. The shorter front shell shoulder begins near y133, around 27% below the tall top. Thus the visible body height/width is approximately 1.8, body/base width approximately 0.88 and tall cap width approximately 0.72 of maximum body width. These are approximate image-space guides affected by perspective, not engineering dimensions.

The source's bronze edge runs from low left, curves through the central overlap and wraps around the high cap. Its rear composition is roughly mirrored, with a smooth writing region to the left and bronze boundary to the right. The written specification explicitly calls for serif lettering, two body hearts, front/rear framed plaques, dense athletic texture, three base bands and bronze top/bottom trim.

## Final appearance review

Personally inspected the front, rear, right side, left side, perspective, top, upper-detail and base-detail PNGs, then independently rechecked the final top, side and detail renders after cleanup. The silhouette now has a visible shorter front shell, recessed black reveal and narrower tall core. This materially improves the interpretation of the reference. All twelve audit areas now have visible implementations or corrections; that is not the same as exact source fidelity or owner approval.
