# Every Step Legacy — revision 4 correction brief

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

Source: original document 63 reference board and the user's confirmed correction list. V3 was not approved. This revision concerns the original athletic Every Step Legacy exterior, not the five supplier-quote models or smaller variants.

## Confirmed corrections

1. **Front texture and stitching:** replace the conspicuous regular stud grid with a finer athletic surface; make the stitched region and connected ladder detail match the reference.
2. **Two curves:** model a full-height black sculptural blade between the bronze sweep and a separate black boundary. The second boundary rises into the lower writing-shell shoulder and wraps rearward. A small upper strap is insufficient.
3. **Top:** reproduce the angled, textured inset and restrained bronze border, avoiding an apparent open tube or pale reflective cap.
4. **Base:** follow the original reference's three textured bands, top/bottom bronze frame and low, wide plaques. Remove the extra bronze separator/inset rings introduced in v3.
5. **Back:** show the reference's reverse layout, smooth writing panel, second black curve and bronze boundary. Do not repeat the front stitching on the rear.
6. **Text:** increase body inscription size and readable prominence. Front: “before / the crowds / there was / you.” Rear: “every step / i took / began / beside you.” Preserve hearts and framed base text.
