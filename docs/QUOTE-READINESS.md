## Common quote questionnaire

- One finished sample per design, then unit and total costs at 10/25/50 per design. Show minimum order, setup fees and mixed-design quantity rules; these are scenarios, not commitments.
- Scope A: print supplied mesh geometry with stated units, orientation, material/process, finish and all exclusions. Scope B: CAD correction to achieve the original specification, quoted separately with editable deliverables and ownership terms.
- State usable cavity volume with closure engaged; wall/base thickness; assembly dimensions; drainage/support cleanup requirements; weak sculptural features; closure tolerances after finishing. Ask for a section drawing or analysis evidence, not just “printable.”
- Name material grade and process. Separate raw print, surface preparation, multicolor painting/hand detail, clear coat, hardware/metal plate, inserts, seals, assembly and QC.
- State whether finish work is in-house or subcontracted. Provide examples, finish sample approval method and repeatability/color matching approach.
- Separate packaging, freight, taxes/duties where known, engineering/tooling, lead times, quote validity, production location and defect/remake terms.
- Quote blank personalization zones as baseline; optional name/date treatment separately. Do not reproduce placeholder dates.
- Identify what the supplier cannot provide, including CAD engineering, metal hardware or detailed painting. A raw-print quote is useful but not a completed urn cost.
