## Terms and engineering issues to resolve from the incoming material

- **“Companion urn” is ambiguous.** Older recovered CAD guidance uses it for a dual-person vessel with a 400 in³ target. Mom may mean a matching small keepsake accompanying the main urn. Keep those separate until the intended meaning and capacity are clear.
- **Size and capacity are separate requirements.** A 10-inch version needs its own cavity and closure assessment. Uniform scaling changes volume by the cube of the scale factor; a reduced exterior cannot be assumed to retain the original capacity. The inherited adult minimum is 200 in³, subject to the actual SKU brief.
- **Finished assembled dimensions matter.** Midnight Blossom's body alone is about 10.45 inches tall if the source coordinates are millimeters; units and assembly remain unverified. We cannot call the recovered file a compliant 10-inch model.
- **Closure intent remains unresolved.** Older briefs request bottom access; Midnight Blossom has top access. Do not silently impose one requirement on all five designs.
- **A bundle needs a bill of materials.** Quote the urn, smaller vessel(s), jewelry, personalization, packaging and digital service separately, then calculate the combined offer. Added perceived value also adds costs and obligations.
- **A digital feature is not yet a delivered service.** Identify what exists, who operates it, what the customer gets, and the ongoing hosting/support commitment before costing or advertising it.
