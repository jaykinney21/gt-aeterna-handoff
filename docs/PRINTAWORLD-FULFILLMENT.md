# PrintAWorld fulfillment review

> Public handoff copy. Dated work status is historical; see the repository README for the newly authorized public download handoff and current instructions. Private source access and machine-specific account details are omitted.

Reviewed September 19, 2026. Research only; no contact, upload, registration, or order. Samples: ZIP **[sample ZIP omitted], United States**. Future orders: separate customer destinations.

**Assessment:** credible artistic fabrication candidate; direct-to-consumer fulfillment remains unconfirmed. Nationwide shipping alone does not establish a dropshipping service.

## Documented capabilities

- **Intake:** no signup required. Send references/files, dimensions, quantity, intended use, material/finish, ZIP and deadline. Pricing and minimums require a written quote; no published universal MOQ or one-unit urn price. Studios are in New York; nationwide U.S. delivery is offered. [Homepage](https://prtwd.com/)
- **Files/production:** STL, OBJ, STP, STEP and 3DM accepted for review. Cleaning, sanding, polishing, painting and metal finishing must be expressly included. Same-day production is exceptional; the urn's total turnaround needs a project-specific quote. Plating is a surface finish, not solid metal. [3D-printing service](https://prtwd.com/services/3d-printing/)
- **Artistic completion:** quoted projects can combine modeling, printing, mold making, assembly, painting and metal finishes. Packing and delivery can form part of the scope, alongside review/approval stages. This supports asking for a finished sample rather than a raw print. [Art fabrication](https://prtwd.com/services/art-fabrication/)
- **Delivery/terms:** the terms describe customer-supplied delivery information and shipping to the specified address; they do not expressly promise reseller fulfillment. Delivery dates are estimates, cancellation stops once production begins, and risk transfers at carrier handoff. The terms place design/material suitability responsibility on the customer and describe materials as decorative unless otherwise specified. Obtain explicit agreement on urn use, handling and closure requirements. [Terms within Confidential Policy](https://prtwd.com/confidential-policy/)
- **Defect reporting conflict:** Sales Policy requires inspection and written, photo-supported notice within **three business days**; its remedies are replacement or refund after their determination. The other terms specify a **10-day** warranty claim window and supplier-paid faulty-product transport. Ask which governs the quote; plan inspection within three business days until clarified. [Sales Policy](https://prtwd.com/sales-policy/) · [Other terms](https://prtwd.com/confidential-policy/)

## Questions for the quote request

1. Quote one finished sample per design to [sample ZIP omitted], separately identifying design correction, finishing, closure hardware, packaging and shipping.
2. After sample approval, can you manufacture and ship individual orders to changing U.S. customer addresses? What is the recurring minimum charge versus batches of 10/25/50?
3. Can packaging, labels and paperwork identify only our company, with no supplier invoice or promotional material? Can you include our QR/instruction card and consolidate keepsake/jewelry accessories?
4. Provide packed dimensions/weight, zone-based freight examples, handling fees, dispatch commitment and tracking process. Separate production from transit.
5. Establish an approved finish sample, closure/capacity checks, pre-shipment photos and damage-resistant packing specification. Clarify rejection, replacement, shipping-damage and return costs plus the conflicting inspection windows.

**Recommended role:** request a finished-urn sample and recurring fulfillment proposal in parallel. Do not label PrintAWorld an approved dropship partner until its written response confirms those operational requirements.
